قرآن کی مثالیں۔

: Nawasib al-Mudhari' (نواصب مضارع).

Understanding these specific particles—such as An (أن), Lan (لن), Kay (كي), and Hatta (حتی)—is essential for anyone looking to grasp the precise linguistic nuances of the Arabic language. In this lesson, we explore exactly how these tools influence the present tense verb (Fi'l Mudhari'), shifting it from the indicative state (Marfu') to the subjunctive state (Mansub), and how that fundamentally alters the meaning and structure of a sentence.
