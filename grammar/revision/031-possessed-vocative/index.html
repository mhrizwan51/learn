Revision 031: Possessed Vocative

In this video, we discuss the Arabic grammar rule of Munada Mudaf (منادى مضاف), an important concept in the chapter of vocatives. Unlike simple vocatives, where the noun comes directly after the particle of calling (يا), the Munada Mudaf is always in the construct state (idafah) and its ending is never built-in but rather declined according to its grammatical position.
You will learn:
• The definition of Munada Mudaf.
• Rules of parsing (i‘rab) for Munada Mudaf.
• Practical examples from Qur’an.
