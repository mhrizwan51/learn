Quranic Arabic Grammar

A short, structured course in Quranic Arabic grammar (nahw) and morphology (sarf), taught in Urdu by Muhammad Rizwan Zubair for students of Quran Tafsir. The 33 core lessons are about seven minutes each, 3¾ hours in total, each followed by a practice session. Lessons 1–12 cover the noun (ism) and declension (i‘rab); lessons 13–33 cover the verb (fi‘l), its conjugation (gardan) and the derived verb forms (abwab mazeed feeh). A later revision series goes over the same ground with fresh Quranic examples.
