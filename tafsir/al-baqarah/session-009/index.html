Session 009: Surah Al-Baqarah 63–73

سورہ بقرہ، آیات ۶۳ تا ۷۳ — لفظی ترجمہ اور تفسیر

Quran Class 002063 Sura Baqara Ayat 63-73 Session 009 · 1:12:09 · 2020-05-31 · Watch on YouTube

Tafseer of Sura Baqarah 63 to 73, when Bani Israel misaq was fortified as Toor Mountain was lifted over them, how they tried to trick the Saturday, the way they behaved when they were asked to sacrifice a cow in case of a murder identification
