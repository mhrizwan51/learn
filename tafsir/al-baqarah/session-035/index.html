Session 035: Surah Al-Baqarah 282–286

Dated November 29, 2020.
Ayat Timings: #282 3:27, #283 35:18, #284 48:11, #285 52:36, #286 58:32, QA 1:06:07.
Tafseer and word to word translation of Sura Baqarah 282 to 286, Write deeds for all the loans and have witnesses, collateral option in case of travelling, beautiful prayer to Allah to forgive us.
