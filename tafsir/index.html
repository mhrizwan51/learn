Quran Tafsir in Urdu

Quran Tafsir by Muhammad Rizwan Zubair is a verse-by-verse Urdu translation and explanation of the Holy Quran, recorded as weekly online classes. Each session translates the ayat word by word, explains the Arabic grammar behind the translation and relates the message to daily life, so that an Urdu speaker with no background in Arabic can follow the Quran in its own language.
