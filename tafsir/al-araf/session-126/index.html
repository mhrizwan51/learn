Session 126: Surah Al-A'raf 123–129

حضرت موسٰیؑ اور فرعون

Quran Tafseer Sura Araf 123 to 129 Session 126 حضرت موسٰیؑ اور فرعون · 1:03:02 · 2022-10-23 · Watch on YouTube

Ayat Timings: {123} 0:08, {124} 5:01, {125} 10:54, {126} 16:27, {127} 28:15, {128} 40:17, {129} 55:17, Comment 1:01:56.
