Session 120: Surah Al-A'raf 57–64

سورہ اعراف، آیات ۵۷ تا ۶۴ — لفظی ترجمہ اور تفسیر

حضرت نوحؑ

Quran Tafseer Sura Araf 57 to 64 Session 120 حضرت نوحؑ · 1:07:18 · 2022-09-04 · Watch on YouTube

Ayat Timings: {57} 0:02, {58} 19:13, {59} 30:05, {60} 44:13, {61} 48:03, {62} 48:56, {63} 52:15, {64} 56:38, Q&A 1:01:53.

The argument of Allah’s Oneness, day of Judgment and guidance with the example of rain. Message of Hazrat Nuh as and the response he received.
