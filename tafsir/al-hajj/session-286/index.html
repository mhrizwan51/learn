Session 286: Surah Al-Hajj 54–64

سورہ حج، آیات ۵۴ تا ۶۴ — لفظی ترجمہ اور تفسیر

Quran Tafseer Sura Hajj 54 till 64 Session 286 · 1:08:13 · 2026-04-12 · Watch on YouTube

سورہ حج کی آیات ۵۴ تا ۶۴ کا لفظی ترجمہ اور تفسیر،
کہ کس طرح اللہ تعالیٰ حق اور باطل کے درمیان فرق واضح کرتا ہے اور وہ لوگ جو علمِ راسخ رکھتے ہیں، کس طرح حق کے سامنے سرِ تسلیم خم کرتے ہیں۔ اس کے علاوہ اللہ کی راہ میں ہجرت کرنے والوں
کے اجر اور کائنات میں موجود اللہ کی قدرت کی نشانیوں پر تفصیلی گفتگو کی گئی ہے۔

The discussion highlights the distinction between those whose hearts are hardened and those who are blessed with knowledge and humility. Learn about Allah's ultimate sovereignty, the rewards for migration (Hijrah), and the powerful signs of nature that point to the Creator's existence.

Key Topics Covered:

Satanic Interference & Divine Strength [00:20]: How Allah nullifies the whispers of Satan and strengthens His verses as a test for mankind.

The Path of the Knowledgeable [03:09]: Why those with true knowledge recognize the Quran as the truth and submit their hearts to Allah.

The Fate of Disbelievers [13:15]: An explanation of "Yawm-e-Aqeem" (the Barren Day) and the suddenness of divine judgment.

The Virtue of Hijrah (Migration) [23:57]: The immense reward of "Rizq-e-Hasan" for those who leave their homes for the sake of Allah, whether they achieve victory or meet their end on the way.

Signs of Power: Day and Night [40:03]: Reflecting on how Allah merges the night into the day as a sign of His absolute control.

Spiritual Revival [46:34]: Just as rain turns a dead earth green, Allah's mercy can revive dead hearts.

Allah’s Self-Sufficiency [52:30]: Understanding Allah as Al-Ghani (Self-Sufficient) and Al-Hamid (Praiseworthy)
