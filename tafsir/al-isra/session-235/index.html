Session 235: Surah Al-Isra (Bani Israel) 66–74

سورہ بنی اسرائیل، آیات ۶۶ تا ۷۴ — لفظی ترجمہ اور تفسیر

Quran Tafseer Sura Bani Israel 66 till 74 Session 235 · 1:23:07 · 2025-03-16 · Watch on YouTube

سورہ بنی اسرائیل کی آیات ۶۶ تا ۷۴ کا لفظی ترجمہ اور تفسیر،

These verses highlight Allah’s power over the sea, human reliance on Him during difficulties, and the consequences of disbelief. They also discuss the trials faced by the Prophet ﷺ and the steadfastness required in delivering the divine message.

🔹 Key Topics Covered:
✅ Allah’s control over the sea and human dependency (Ayah 66)
✅ Man’s forgetfulness after being saved from distress (Ayah 67)
✅ Warning against shirk and heedlessness (Ayah 68-70)
✅ The honor given to humanity and their accountability (Ayah 71-72)
✅ Prophet Muhammad’s ﷺ unwavering commitment to revelation (Ayah 73-74)
