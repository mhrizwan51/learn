Your data

When you are signed in, this site saves your name, email address and profile picture from Google, your best and most recent score on each quiz, how many times you took it, and which lectures you opened and when. Nothing else is collected.

Sharing with your teacher is on by default so that your teacher can follow the class's progress. You can switch it off at any time with the switch above; while it is off your teacher cannot see any of your scores or lecture history. Your data is stored in Google Firebase and is never sold or shown publicly.
