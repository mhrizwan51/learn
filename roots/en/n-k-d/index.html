Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ن ک دn-k-d

(س) نَكِدَ يَنْكَدُ نَكَدًاnakida yankadu nakadan: (of a well) to run low; (of livelihood) to be straitened. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

نَكِدٌnakid (adjective on the pattern of فَعِلٌfaʿil): scanty and yielded only with difficulty, unproductive; ill-natured. Only once in the Quran: ﴿وَالَّذِيْ خَبُثَ لَا يَخْرُجُ اِلَّا نَكِدًا﴾ (Al-Aʿrāf 7:58)
