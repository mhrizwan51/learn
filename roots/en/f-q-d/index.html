Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ف ق دf-q-d

(ض) فَقَدَ يَفْقِدُ فَقْدًا وَفُقْدَانًاfaqada yafqidu faqdan wa-fuqdānan: (1) for a thing to be lost (2) to find a thing missing, to miss. ﴿قَالُوْا نَفْقِدُ صُوَاعَ الْمَلِكِ﴾ (Yūsuf 12:72)

Form V (tafaʿʿul) تَفَقَّدَ يَتَفَقَّدُ تَفَقُّدًاtafaqqada yatafaqqadu tafaqqudan: (1) to search for carefully (2) to inspect in order to see whether anything is lacking or missing. ﴿وَتَفَقَّدَ الطَّيْرَ﴾ (An-Naml 27:20)
