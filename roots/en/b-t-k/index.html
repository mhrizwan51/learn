Root ب ت ک b-t-k

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ب ت کb-t-k

(ن) بَتَكَ يَبْتُكُ بَتْكًاbataka yabtuku batkan: to cut, especially to cut by tearing or twisting off.

Form II (tafʿīl) بَتَّكَ يُبَتِّكُ تَبْتِيْكًاbattaka yubattiku tabtīkan: to cut repeatedly and extensively — the pattern tafʿīl here indicates repetition. Only once in the Quran, and that in the form tafʿīl: ﴿فَلَيُبَتِّكُنَّ آذَانَ الْأَنْعَامِ﴾ (An-Nisāʾ 4:119)
