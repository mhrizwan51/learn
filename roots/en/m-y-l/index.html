Quranic citations in this entry: 3 · اردو

م ی لm-y-l

(ض) مَالَ يَمِيْلُ مَيْلًاmāla yamīlu maylan (original form: مَيَلَ يَمْيِلُmayala yamyilu): to leave the right direction and incline to one side; with this basic sense it occurs in several meanings: (1) to go astray (2) to take sides with one party (3) to fall upon someone, to attack. ﴿فَلَا تَمِيْلُوْا كُلَّ الْمَيْلِ﴾ (An-Nisāʾ 4:129); ﴿اَنْ تَمِيْلُوْا مَيْلًا عَظِيْمًا﴾ (An-Nisāʾ 4:27); ﴿فَيَمِيْلُوْنَ عَلَيْكُمْ مَّيْلَةً وَّاحِدَةً﴾ (An-Nisāʾ 4:102)
