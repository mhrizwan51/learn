Root ع ز ل ʿ-z-l

Quranic citations in this entry: 3 · اردو

ع ز لʿ-z-l

(ض) عَزَلَ يَعْزِلُ عَزْلًاʿazala yaʿzilu ʿazlan: to separate someone; to set apart.

مَعْزُوْلٌmaʿzūl (passive participle, ism mafʿūl): separated, set apart. ﴿اِنَّهُمْ عَنِ السَّمْعِ لَمَعْزُوْلُوْنَ﴾ (Ash-Shuʿarāʾ 26:212)

مَعْزِلٌmaʿzil (noun of time or place, ẓarf): a place of separation; the edge of something. ﴿وَنَادٰى نُوْحُ ۨابْنَهُۥ وَكَانَ فِيْ مَعْزِلٍ﴾ (Hūd 11:42)

Form VIII (iftiʿāl) اِعْتَزَلَ يَعْتَزِلُ اِعْتِزَالًاiʿtazala yaʿtazilu iʿtizālan: to separate oneself from someone (that from which one separates comes without any preposition, i.e. as a direct object).

اِعْتَزِلْiʿtazil (imperative, amr). ﴿فَاعْتَزِلُوا النِّسَآءَ فِي الْمَحِيْضِ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:222)
