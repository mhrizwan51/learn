Quranic citations in this entry: 3 · اردو

ش ھ رsh-h-r

(ف) شَهَرَ يَشْهَرُ شَهْرًاshahara yashharu shahran: to make someone known publicly; to pass the period of a month.

شَهْرٌshahr pl. شُهُوْرٌ وَاَشْهُرٌshuhūr wa-ashhur (noun, ism dhāt): month. ﴿شَهْرُ رَمَضَانَ الَّذِيْٓ اُنْزِلَ فِيْهِ الْقُرْاٰنُ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:185); ﴿اِنَّ عِدَّةَ الشُّهُوْرِ عِنْدَ اللّٰهِ اثْنَا عَشَرَ شَهْرًا﴾ (At-Tawbah 9:36); ﴿اَلْحَجُّ اَشْهُرٌ مَّعْلُوْمٰتٌ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:197)

Form II (tafʿīl) شَهَّرَ يُشَهِّرُ تَشْهِيْرًاshahhara yushahhiru tashhīran: to make someone notorious; to publicise, mostly in order to disgrace (this verb is not used in the Quran).

Form VIII (iftiʿāl) اِشْتَهَرَ يَشْتَهِرُ اِشْتِهَارًاishtahara yashtahiru ishtihāran: to become famous; to gain renown (this verb is not used in the Quran).
