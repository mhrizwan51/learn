Root د م ر d-m-r

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

د م رd-m-r

(ن) دَمَرَ يَدْمُرُ دُمُوْرًاdamara yadmuru dumūran: to perish.

Form II (tafʿīl) دَمَّرَ يُدَمِّرُ تَدْمِيْرًاdammara yudammiru tadmīran: (1) to destroy; its object comes directly. ﴿فَدَمَّرْنٰهَا تَدْمِيْرًا﴾ (Al-Isrāʾ 17:16); (2) to bring destruction upon someone; here it takes the preposition عَلٰىʿalā. ﴿دَمَّرَ اللّٰهُ عَلَيْهِمْ﴾ (Muḥammad 47:10)
