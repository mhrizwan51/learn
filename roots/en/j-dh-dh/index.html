Root ج ذ ذ j-dh-dh

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ج ذ ذj-dh-dh

(ن) جَذَّ يَجُذُّ جَذًّاjadhdha yajudhdhu jadhdhan: to break a thing into pieces, to cut.

جُذَاذٌjudhādh: pieces. ﴿فَجَعَلَهُمْ جُذٰذًا﴾ (Al-Anbiyāʾ 21:58)

مَجْذُوْذٌmajdhūdh (passive participle, ism mafʿūl): broken, cut off. ﴿عَطَآءً غَيْرَ مَجْذُوْذٍ﴾ (Hūd 11:108)
