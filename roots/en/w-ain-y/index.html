Root و ع ی w-ʿ-y

Quranic citations in this entry: 4 · اردو

و ع یw-ʿ-y

(ض) وَعٰى يَعِيْ وَعْيًاwaʿā yaʿī wa-ʿyan (original form: وَعَيَ يَوْعِيُwaʿaya yawʿiyu): to collect (something in a vessel); to retain (a saying), to preserve in memory. ﴿وَتَعِيَهَآ اُذُنٌ وَّاعِيَةٌ﴾ (Al-Ḥāqqah 69:12)

وَاعِيَةٌwāʿiyah (feminine of the active participle (ism fāʿil) وَاعٍwāʿ): retentive, one who retains. (In the above-mentioned verse, al-Ḥāqqah 69:12.) وِعَآءٌwiʿāʾ pl. اَوْعِيَةٌawʿiyah (noun, ism dhāt): that in which something is collected, bag, sack, vessel. ﴿فَبَدَاَ بِاَوْعِيَتِهِمْ قَبْلَ وِعَآءِ اَخِيْهِ﴾ (Yūsuf 12:76)

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَوْعٰى يُوْعِيْ اِيْعَآءًawʿā yūʿī īʿāʾan: to keep a collected thing shut up (in a vessel) and preserved; to keep hidden (in the heart). ﴿وَجَمَعَ فَاَوْعٰى﴾ (Al-Maʿārij 70:18); ﴿وَاللّٰهُ اَعْلَمُ بِمَا يُوْعُوْنَ﴾ (Al-Inshiqāq 84:23)
