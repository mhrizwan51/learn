Root ل ح ف l-ḥ-f

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ل ح فl-ḥ-f

(ف) لَحَفَ يَلْحَفُ لَحْفًاlaḥafa yalḥafu laḥfan: to cover something with a cloth (liḥāf) (this verb is not used in the Quran).

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَلْحَفَ يُلْحِفُ اِلْحَافًاal-ḥafa yulḥifu ilḥāfan: to cling (in begging), to ask with insistence and importunity. Only once in the Quran: ﴿لَا يَسْـَٔلُوْنَ النَّاسَ اِلْحَافًا﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:273)
