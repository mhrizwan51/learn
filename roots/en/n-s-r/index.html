Root ن س ر n-s-r

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ن س رn-s-r

— نَسْرnasr pl. نُسُوْر وَأَنْسُرnusūr wa-ʾansur: a vulture, a large bird of prey. «نَسْرnasr» is also the name of one of the five idols of the people of Noah. Only once in the Quran, in the enumeration of the idols: ﴿وَلَا تَذَرُنَّ وَدًّا وَّلَا سُوَاعًا وَّلَا يَغُوْثَ وَيَعُوْقَ وَنَسْرًا﴾ (Nūḥ 71:23).
