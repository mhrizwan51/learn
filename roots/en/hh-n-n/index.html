Root ح ن ن ḥ-n-n

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ح ن نḥ-n-n

(ض) حَنَّ يَحِنُّ حَنِيْنًا وَحَنَانًاḥanna yaḥinnu ḥanīnan wa-ḥanānan: to be kind and affectionate towards someone; to yearn.

حَنَانٌḥanān (noun, ism dhāt): tenderness, kindness. ﴿وَّحَنَانًا مِّنْ لَّدُنَّا وَزَكٰوةً﴾ (Maryam 19:13)

حُنَيْنٌḥunayn (proper noun on the pattern of فُعَيْلfuʿayl): the name of a place between Makkah and Ṭāʾif where the Battle of Ḥunayn was fought. ﴿وَّيَوْمَ حُنَيْنٍ اِذْ اَعْجَبَتْكُمْ كَثْرَتُكُمْ﴾ (At-Tawbah 9:25)
