Root ش و ی sh-w-y

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ش و یsh-w-y

(ض) شَوٰى يَشْوِيْ شَيًّاshawā yashwī shayyan: to roast a thing. ﴿يَشْوِي الْوُجُوْهَ﴾ (Al-Kahf 18:29)

شَوًيshawany: the extremities of the body, a wound to which does not cause death, such as the skin. ﴿نَزَّاعَةً لِّلشَّوٰى﴾ (Al-Maʿārij 70:16)
