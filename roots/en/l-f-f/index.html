Root ل ف ف l-f-f

Quranic citations in this entry: 3 · اردو

ل ف فl-f-f

(ن) لَفَّ يَلُفُّ لَفًّاlaffa yaluffu laffan (original form: لَفَفَ يَلْفُفُlafafa yalfufu): to wrap one thing together with another, to join and gather together (this verb is not used in the Quran).

لَفِيْفٌlafīf (adjective on the pattern of فَعِيْلٌfaʿīl): a mixed gathering of people of different kinds, gathered together. ﴿جِئْنَا بِكُمْ لَفِيْفًا﴾ (Al-Isrāʾ 17:104)

اَلْفَافٌal-fāf (plural of لِفٌّliff): dense, intertwined (gardens). ﴿وَّجَنّٰتٍ اَلْفَافًا﴾ (An-Nabaʾ 78:16)

Form VIII (iftiʿāl) اِلْتَفَّ يَلْتَفُّ اِلْتِفَافًاiltaffa yaltaffu iltifāfan: to be wrapped together, to be joined to one another. ﴿وَالْتَفَّتِ السَّاقُ بِالسَّاقِ﴾ (Al-Qiyāmah 75:29)
