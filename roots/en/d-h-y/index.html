Root د ھ ی d-h-y

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

د ھ یd-h-y

(ف) دَهٰى يَدْهٰى دَهْيًاdahā yadhā dahyan: for a calamity to befall someone.

دَاهِيَةdāhiyah pl. دَوَاهٍdawāh: a great calamity, a grievous disaster.

اَدْهٰىadhā (elative, ism tafḍīl): more grievous and more dreadful. Only once in the Quran: ﴿وَالسَّاعَةُ اَدْهٰى وَاَمَرُّ﴾ (Al-Qamar 54:46) — i.e. the Hour is more dreadful and more bitter.
