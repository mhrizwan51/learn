Root م ج د m-j-d

Quranic citations in this entry: 3 · اردو

م ج دm-j-d

(ک) مَجُدَ يَمْجُدُ مَجَادَةًmajuda yamjudu majādatan: to be noble and of high rank, to possess honour.

مَجِيْدmajīd (pattern فَعِيْلfaʿīl): exceedingly noble and exalted. The lexicographers say that مَجْدmajd carries, along with honour, the sense of vastness and abundance. Four times in the Quran, and this attribute is applied to three things: to God, to the Throne, and to the Quran — ﴿ذُو الْعَرْشِ الْمَجِيْدُ﴾ (Al-Burūj 85:15), ﴿بَلْ هُوَ قُرْآنٌ مَّجِيْدٌ﴾ (Al-Burūj 85:21), ﴿إِنَّهُۥ حَمِيْدٌ مَّجِيْدٌ﴾ (Hūd 11:73).
