Root ص ر م ṣ-r-m

Quranic citations in this entry: 3 · اردو

ص ر مṣ-r-m

(ض) صَرَمَ يَصْرِمُ صَرْمًاṣarama yaṣrimu ṣarman: to cut, to pluck fruit; also to sever a relationship; the verb form (bāb) is determined from the Quranic form «يَصْرِمُyaṣrimu». ﴿لَيَصْرِمُنَّهَا مُصْبِحِيْنَ﴾ (Al-Qalam 68:17)

صَرِيْمٌṣarīm: cut off; and from this, the dark night (because it has parted from the day) or a burnt field. ﴿فَاَصْبَحَتْ كَالصَّرِيْمِ﴾ (Al-Qalam 68:20)

صَارِمٌṣārim pl. صَارِمُوْنَṣārimūna (active participle, ism fāʿil): one who cuts, one who plucks fruit. ﴿اَنِ اغْدُوْا عَلٰى حَرْثِكُمْ اِنْ كُنْتُمْ صٰرِمِيْنَ﴾ (Al-Qalam 68:22)This root occurs three times in the Quran, all three in the same story (the owners of the garden).
