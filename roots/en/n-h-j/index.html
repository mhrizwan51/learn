Root ن ھ ج n-h-j

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ن ھ جn-h-j

(ف) نَهَجَ يَنْهَجُ نَهْجًاnahaja yanhaju nahjan: (of a road) to be clear; to walk a clear road. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

مِنْهَاجٌminhāj (noun of instrument, ism ālah, on the pattern of مِفْعَالٌmifʿāl): a means of travelling, i.e. an open and clear road. Only once in the Quran: ﴿لِكُلٍّ جَعَلْنَا مِنْكُمْ شِرْعَةً وَّمِنْهَاجًا﴾ (Al-Māʾidah 5:48)
