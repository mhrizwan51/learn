Root ع م د ʿ-m-d

Quranic citations in this entry: 4 · اردو

ع م دʿ-m-d

(ض) عَمَدَ يَعْمِدُ عَمْدًاʿamada yaʿmidu ʿamdan: (1) to intend (2) to support, to set up a pillar. (this verb is not used in the Quran).

عِمَادٌʿimād (generic noun, ism jins; singular عِمَادَةٌʿimādah, plural عَمَدٌʿamad): that on which something rests, a pillar. ﴿اَللّٰهُ الَّذِيْ رَفَعَ السَّمٰوٰتِ بِغَيْرِ عَمَدٍ تَرَوْنَهَا﴾ (Ar-Raʿd 13:2); ﴿اِرَمَ ذَاتِ الْعِمَادِ﴾ (Al-Fajr 89:7)

Form V (tafaʿʿul) تَعَمَّدَ يَتَعَمَّدُ تَعَمُّدًاtaʿammada yataʿammadu taʿammudan: to intend deliberately (and firmly), to do on purpose.

مُتَعَمِّدٌmutaʿammid (active participle, ism fāʿil). ﴿وَلٰكِنْ مَّا تَعَمَّدَتْ قُلُوْبُكُمْ﴾ (Al-Aḥzāb 33:5); ﴿وَمَنْ يَّقْتُلْ مُؤْمِنًا مُّتَعَمِّدًا﴾ (An-Nisāʾ 4:93)

Form VIII (iftiʿāl) اِعْتَمَدَ يَعْتَمِدُ اِعْتِمَادًاiʿtamada yaʿtamidu iʿtimādan: to lean on something; to rely on someone (this verb is not used in the Quran).
