Root ب ز غ b-z-gh

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ب ز غb-z-gh

(ن) بَزَغَ يَبْزُغُ بُزُوْغًاbazagha yabzughu buzūghan: to rise, to burst forth — especially of the sun or the moon. The lexicographers say the original sense is to split through and emerge.

بَازِغbāzigh / بَازِغَةbāzighah (active participle, ism fāʿil): rising. Twice in the Quran, both in the argument of Abraham, peace be upon him: ﴿فَلَمَّا رَأَي الْقَمَرَ بَازِغًا﴾ (Al-Anʿām 6:77); ﴿فَلَمَّا رَأَي الشَّمْسَ بَازِغَةً﴾ (Al-Anʿām 6:78)
