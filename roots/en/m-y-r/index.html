Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

م ی رm-y-r

(ض) مَارَ يَمِيْرُ مَيْرًاmāra yamīru mayran (original form: مَيَرَ يَمْيِرُmayara yamyiru): to bring grain and provisions for someone. Only once in the Quran, in the words of Joseph's brothers: ﴿وَنَمِيْرُ اَهْلَنَا﴾ (Yūsuf 12:65)
