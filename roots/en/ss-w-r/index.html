Root ص و ر ṣ-w-r

Quranic citations in this entry: 5 · اردو

ص و رṣ-w-r

(ن) صَارَ يَصُوْرُ صَوْرًاṣāra yaṣūru ṣawran: (1) to call, to incline (2) to cut, to carve a figure.

صُرْṣur (imperative, amr). ﴿فَصُرْهُنَّ اِلَيْكَ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:260)

صُوْرَةٌṣūrah pl. صُوَرٌṣuwar (noun, ism dhāt): shape, form. ﴿فِيْٓ اَيِّ صُوْرَةٍ مَّا شَآءَ رَكَّبَكَ﴾ (Al-Infiṭār 82:8)

صُوْرٌṣūr (noun, ism dhāt): anything that gives a sound when blown into, a trumpet. ﴿وَنُفِخَ فِي الصُّوْرِ﴾ (Yā Sīn 36:51)

Form II (tafʿīl) صَوَّرَ يُصَوِّرُ تَصْوِيْرًاṣawwara yuṣawwiru taṣwīran: to give someone a shape or form.

مُصَوِّرٌmuṣawwir (active participle, ism fāʿil): the Fashioner, one who gives form. ﴿هُوَ الَّذِيْ يُصَوِّرُكُمْ فِي الْاَرْحَامِ﴾ (Āl ʿImrān 3:6); ﴿الْخَالِقُ الْبَارِئُ الْمُصَوِّرُ﴾ (Al-Ḥashr 59:24)

Form V (tafaʿʿul) تَصَوَّرَ يَتَصَوَّرُ تَصَوُّرًاtaṣawwara yataṣawwaru taṣawwuran: to form an image of something in the mind; to imagine (this verb is not used in the Quran).
