Root ک ش ف k-sh-f

Quranic citations in this entry: 5 · اردو

ک ش فk-sh-f

(ض) كَشَفَ يَكْشِفُ كَشْفًاkashafa yakshifu kashfan: (1) to lift the covering from a thing, to uncover (2) to remove (an affliction), to take away. ﴿فَكَشَفْنَا عَنْكَ غِطَآءَكَ﴾ (Qāf 50:22); ﴿وَكَشَفَتْ عَنْ سَاقَيْهَا﴾ (An-Naml 27:44); ﴿يَوْمَ يُكْشَفُ عَنْ سَاقٍ﴾ (Al-Qalam 68:42)

كَاشِفٌkāshif, feminine كَاشِفَةٌkāshifah (active participle, ism fāʿil): one who uncovers, who removes. ﴿فَلَا كَاشِفَ لَهُۥٓ اِلَّا هُوَ﴾ (Al-Anʿām 6:17); ﴿لَيْسَ لَهَا مِنْ دُوْنِ اللّٰهِ كَاشِفَةٌ﴾ (An-Najm 53:58)

Form VII (infiʿāl) اِنْكَشَفَ يَنْكَشِفُ اِنْكِشَافًاinkashafa yankashifu inkishāfan: to be uncovered; to be unveiled; to become manifest (this verb is not used in the Quran).
