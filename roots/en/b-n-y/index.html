Root ب ن ی b-n-y

Quranic citations in this entry: 4 · اردو

ب ن یb-n-y

(ض) بَنٰى يَبْنِيْ بَنْيًاbanā yabnī banyan: to build, to construct. ﴿وَالسَّمَآءِ وَمَا بَنٰهَا﴾ (Ash-Shams 91:5)

بِنَاءٌbināʾ (on the pattern fiʿāl, in the sense of a passive participle (ism mafʿūl)): a thing built, a roof. ﴿وَالسَّمَآءَ بِنَآءً﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:22)

بُنْيَانٌbunyān (intensive form on the pattern fuʿlān): solidly built, an edifice. ﴿كَاَنَّهُمْ بُنْيَانٌ مَّرْصُوْصٌ﴾ (Aṣ-Ṣaff 61:4)

بَنَّاءٌbannāʾ (intensive form on the pattern of فَعَّالfaʿʿāl): one who builds repeatedly and much, a builder. ﴿وَالشَّيٰطِيْنَ كُلَّ بَنَّآءٍ وَّغَوَّاصٍ﴾ (Ṣād 38:37)
