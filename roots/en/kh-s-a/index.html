Root خ س ا kh-s-ʾ

Quranic citations in this entry: 3 · اردو

خ س اkh-s-ʾ

(ف) خَسَاَ يَخْسَاُ خَسْاًkhasaʾa yakhsaʾu khasan: to drive away, to chase off like a dog (transitive); to be driven away, to be removed in humiliation (intransitive).

اِخْسَئُوْاikhsaʾūā (imperative, amr): begone, away with you. ﴿قَالَ اخْسَــُٔوْا فِيْهَا وَلَا تُكَلِّمُوْنِ﴾ (Al-Muʾminūn 23:108)

خَاسِئٌkhāsiʾ pl. خٰسِئِيْنَkhāsiʾīna (active participle, ism fāʿil): driven away, despised; worn out, frustrated. ﴿كُوْنُوْا قِرَدَةً خٰسِئِيْنَ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:65); ﴿يَنْقَلِبْ اِلَيْكَ الْبَصَرُ خَاسِئًا﴾ (Al-Mulk 67:4)
