Root ن و ی n-w-y

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ن و یn-w-y

(ض) نَوٰى يَنْوِيْ نِيَّةً وَنَوًيnawā yanwī niyyatan wa-nawany (original form: نَوَيَ يَنْوِيُnawaya yanwiyu): (1) to intend, to make an intention (2) to throw away a (date) stone. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

نَوًيnawany (generic noun, ism jins, singular نَوَاةٌnawāh): date stone. Only once in the Quran: ﴿اِنَّ اللّٰهَ فَالِقُ الْحَبِّ وَالنَّوٰى﴾ (Al-Anʿām 6:95)
