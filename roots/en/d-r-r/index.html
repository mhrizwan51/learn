Root د ر ر d-r-r

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

د ر رd-r-r

(ض) دَرَّ يَدِرُّ دَرًّا وَدُرُوْرًاdarra yadirru darran wa-durūran: (of the people of this world) to have worldly abundance; (of milk or rain) to flow copiously.

مِدْرَارٌmidrār (intensive form, ṣīghat al-mubālaghah): very abundant, pouring down continuously. ﴿يُرْسِلِ السَّمَآءَ عَلَيْكُمْ مِّدْرَارًا﴾ (Nūḥ 71:11)

(س) دَرَّ يَدَرُّ دَرًّاdarra yadarru darran: (of the face) to regain its glow after illness, to be radiant.

دُرِّيٌّdurriyy (relative adjective, nisbah): shining, brilliant (like a pearl). ﴿كَاَنَّهَا كَوْكَبٌ دُرِّيٌّ﴾ (An-Nūr 24:35)
