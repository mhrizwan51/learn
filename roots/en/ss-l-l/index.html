Root ص ل ل ṣ-l-l

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ص ل لṣ-l-l

(ض) صَلَّ يَصِلُّ صَلِيْلًاṣalla yaṣillu ṣalīlan: (1) of a thing, to make a sound (2) of a thing, to rot.

صَلَّالٌṣallāl (adjective on the pattern of فَعَّالٌfaʿʿāl): rotted dry clay that rings, i.e. makes a sound; in the Quran it occurs as صَلْصَالٌṣalṣāl, with one of its lāms changed to ṣād (see ṣ l ṣ l). ﴿خَلَقَ الْاِنْسَانَ مِنْ صَلْصَالٍ كَالْفَخَّارِ﴾ (Ar-Raḥmān 55:14)
