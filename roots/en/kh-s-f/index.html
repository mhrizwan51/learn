خ س فkh-s-f

(ض) خَسَفَ يَخْسِفُ خُسُوْفًا وَخَسْفًاkhasafa yakhsifu khusūfan wa-khasfan: (of a place) to sink, to cave in (intransitive); to cause someone to be swallowed up (transitive). ﴿فَخَسَفْنَا بِهِۦ وَبِدَارِهِ الْاَرْضَ﴾ (Al-Qaṣaṣ 28:81)(of the moon) to be eclipsed, to lose its light. ﴿وَخَسَفَ الْقَمَرُ﴾ (Al-Qiyāmah 75:8)
