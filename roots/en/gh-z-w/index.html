Root غ ز و gh-z-w

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

غ ز وgh-z-w

(ن) غَزَا يَغْزُوْ غَزْوًاghazā yaghzū ghazwan (original form: غَزَوَ يَغْزُوُghazawa yaghzuwu): to go out to war, to make a raid upon the enemy. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

غَازٍghāz pl. غُزًّيghuzzany (active participle (ism fāʿil)): one who goes out to war. ﴿اَوْ كَانُوْا غُزًّي﴾ (Āl ʿImrān 3:156)

غَزْوَةٌghazwah: a single going out to war, a raid. (This word is not used in the Quran.)
