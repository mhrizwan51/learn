Root ش ر ب sh-r-b

Quranic citations in this entry: 3 · اردو

ش ر بsh-r-b

(س) شَرِبَ يَشْرَبُ شُرْبًاshariba yashrabu shurban: to drink. ﴿وَكُلُوْا وَاشْرَبُوْا وَلَا تُسْرِفُوْا﴾ (Al-Aʿrāf 7:31)

مَشْرُوْبٌmashrūb (passive participle, ism mafʿūl): a drink, something to drink.

مَشْرَبٌmashrab (noun of time or place, ẓarf): drinking place, watering place. ﴿قَدْ عَلِمَ كُلُّ اُنَاسٍ مَّشْرَبَهُمْ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:60)

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَشْرَبَ يُشْرِبُ اِشْرَابًاashraba yushribu ishrāban: to give to drink; figuratively to make something sink deep into the heart. ﴿وَاُشْرِبُوْا فِيْ قُلُوْبِهِمُ الْعِجْلَ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:93)
