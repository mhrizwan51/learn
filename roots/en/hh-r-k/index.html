Root ح ر ک ḥ-r-k

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ح ر کḥ-r-k

(ک) حَرُكَ يَحْرُكُ حَرَكَةًḥaruka yaḥruku ḥarakatan: to stir, to move — the opposite of rest.

حَرَكَةḥarakah: motion; hence the grammatical term "ḥarakah" (vowel sign, iʿrāb).

Form II (tafʿīl) حَرَّكَ يُحَرِّكُ تَحْرِيْكًاḥarraka yuḥarriku taḥrīkan: to move, to set in motion. This root occurs only once in the Quran, and that in this verb form: ﴿لَا تُحَرِّكْ بِهِۦ لِسَانَكَ لِتَعْجَلَ بِهِۦ﴾ (Al-Qiyāmah 75:16)
