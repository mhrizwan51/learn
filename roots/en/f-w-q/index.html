Root ف و ق f-w-q

Quranic citations in this entry: 3 · اردو

ف و قf-w-q

(ن) فَاقَ يَفُوْقُ فَوْقًاfāqa yafūqu fawqan (original form: فَوَقَ يَفْوُقُfawaqa yafwuqu): to be high, to be above; to gain superiority over someone. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

فَوْقَfawqa (noun of time or place, ẓarf): above, over. ﴿وَهُوَ الْقَاهِرُ فَوْقَ عِبَادِهِۦ﴾ (Al-Anʿām 6:18)

فَوَاقٌfawāq: the interval between two milkings; hence respite, pause. ﴿مَا لَهَا مِنْ فَوَاقٍ﴾ (Ṣād 38:15)

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَفَاقَ يُفِيْقُ اِفَاقَةًafāqa yufīqu ifāqatan: to overcome illness or a swoon and come to one's senses, to recover. ﴿فَلَمَّآ اَفَاقَ قَالَ سُبْحٰنَكَ﴾ (Al-Aʿrāf 7:143)
