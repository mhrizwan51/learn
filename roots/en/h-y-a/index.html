Root ھ ی ء h-y-ʾ

Quranic citations in this entry: 3 · اردو

ھ ی ءh-y-ʾ

(ض) هَآءَ يَهِيْٓءُ هَيْئَةًhāʾa yahīʾu hayʾatan (original form: هَيَاَ يَهْيِئُhayaʾa yahyiʾu): to be of good shape and appearance; to be ready for something. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

هَيْئَةٌhayʾah (verbal noun, maṣdar, also a noun, ism dhāt): shape, form, figure. ﴿اَنِّيْٓ اَخْلُقُ لَكُمْ مِّنَ الطِّيْنِ كَهَيْـَٔةِ الطَّيْرِ﴾ (Āl ʿImrān 3:49)

Form II (tafʿīl) هَيَّاَ يُهَيِّئُ تَهْيِئَةًhayyaʾa yuhayyiʾu tahyiʾatan: to give shape to something; to furnish the means for a task, to bring about the means. ﴿وَهَيِّئْ لَنَا مِنْ اَمْرِنَا رَشَدًا﴾ (Al-Kahf 18:10); ﴿وَيُهَيِّئْ لَكُمْ مِّنْ اَمْرِكُمْ مِّرْفَقًا﴾ (Al-Kahf 18:16)
