Root ع ر ر ʿ-r-r

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ع ر رʿ-r-r

(ن، ض) عَرَّ يَعُرُّ وَيَعِرُّ عَرًّاʿarra yaʿurru wa-yaʿirru ʿarran: (1) to be afflicted with mange (2) to come as a petitioner (this verb is not used in the Quran).

مَعَرَّةٌmaʿarrah (noun, ism dhāt): distress, evil, harm. ﴿فَتُصِيْبَكُمْ مِّنْهُمْ مَّعَرَّةٌۢ بِغَيْرِ عِلْمٍ﴾ (Al-Fatḥ 48:25)

Form VIII (iftiʿāl) اِعْتَرَّ يَعْتَرُّ اِعْتِرَارًاiʿtarra yaʿtarru iʿtirāran: to come for a gift without asking (al-Munjid); to present oneself in order to beg but say nothing with the tongue, to become a petition in form (Ḥāfiẓ Aḥmad Yār).

مُعْتَرٌّmuʿtarr (active participle, ism fāʿil): one who becomes a petition in form. ﴿وَاَطْعِمُوا الْقَانِعَ وَالْمُعْتَرَّ﴾ (Al-Ḥajj 22:36)
