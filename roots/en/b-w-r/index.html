Root ب و ر b-w-r

Quranic citations in this entry: 3 · اردو

ب و رb-w-r

(ن) بَارَ يَبُوْرُ بَوْرًاbāra yabūru bawran: (1) to be unsaleable, to be a loss (2) to perish, to be ruined. ﴿تِجَارَةً لَّنْ تَبُوْرَ﴾ (Fāṭir 35:29)

بَوَارٌbawār (noun, ism dhāt): perdition, ruin. ﴿وَاَحَلُّوْا قَوْمَهُمْ دَارَ الْبَوَارِ﴾ (Ibrāhīm 14:28)

بُوْرٌbūr: plural of the active participle (ism fāʿil) بَائِرٌbāʾir; the word itself is also used for singular, plural, masculine and feminine alike: one who perishes. ﴿وَكُنْتُمْ قَوْمًۢا بُوْرًا﴾ (Al-Fatḥ 48:12)
