Root ع ی ن ʿ-y-n

Quranic citations in this entry: 6 · اردو

ع ی نʿ-y-n

(ض) عَانَ يَعِيْنُ عَيْنًاʿāna yaʿīnu ʿaynan (original form: عَيَنَ يَعْيِنُʿayana yaʿyinu): (1) to afflict with the evil eye (2) for tears to flow from the eye. (this verb is not used in the Quran).

عَيْنٌʿayn pl. اَعْيُنٌaʿyun (noun, ism dhāt): eye. ﴿وَالْعَيْنَ بِالْعَيْنِ﴾ (Al-Māʾidah 5:45); "بِاَعْيُنِنَاbiʾaʿyuninā": under Our watch. ﴿وَاصْنَعِ الْفُلْكَ بِاَعْيُنِنَا﴾ (Hūd 11:37)

عَيْنٌʿayn pl. عُيُوْنٌʿuyūn (noun, ism dhāt): a spring of water. ﴿عَيْنًا يَّشْرَبُ بِهَا عِبَادُ اللّٰهِ﴾ (Al-Insān 76:6); ﴿اِنَّ الْمُتَّقِيْنَ فِيْ جَنّٰتٍ وَّعُيُوْنٍ﴾ (Al-Ḥijr 15:45)

عَيْنَآءُʿaynāʾu pl. عِيْنٌʿīn (feminine of اَفْعَلُafʿalu, the pattern of colours and defects, on the pattern of فَعْلَآءُfaʿlāʾu): a woman with large, beautiful eyes; also the wild cow (because its eye is large and wide). ﴿وَحُوْرٌ عِيْنٌ﴾ (Al-Wāqiʿah 56:22)

مَعِيْنٌmaʿīn (اصلًا مَعْيِنٌaṣlan maʿyin, noun of time or place, ẓarf, on the pattern of مَفْعِلٌmafʿil): the place where a spring issues forth; since the water there is very pure, the word is used in the sense of flowing, clear water. ﴿فَمَنْ يَّاْتِيْكُمْ بِمَآءٍ مَّعِيْنٍ﴾ (Al-Mulk 67:30)

Form II (tafʿīl) عَيَّنَ يُعَيِّنُ تَعْيِيْنًاʿayyana yuʿayyinu taʿyīnan: to single out and appoint something; to specify (this verb is not used in the Quran).

Form V (tafaʿʿul) تَعَيَّنَ يَتَعَيَّنُ تَعَيُّنًاtaʿayyana yataʿayyanu taʿayyunan: to become fixed and settled (this verb is not used in the Quran).
