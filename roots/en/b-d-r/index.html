Root ب د ر b-d-r

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ب د رb-d-r

(ن) بَدَرَ يَبْدُرُ بُدُوْرًاbadara yabduru budūran: to hasten, to rush towards a thing.

Form III (mufāʿalah) بَادَرَ يُبَادِرُ مُبَادَرَةً وَبِدَارًاbādara yubādiru mubādaratan wa-bidāran: to hasten in rivalry, to try to outstrip one another. Only once in the Quran, as the verbal noun (maṣdar) of form mufāʿalah: ﴿وَلَا تَأْكُلُوْهَا إِسْرَافًا وَّبِدَارًا أَنْ يَّكْبَرُوْا﴾ (An-Nisāʾ 4:6)

بَدْرbadr: the full moon (because it rises even before sunset); also the site of the Battle of Badr. ﴿وَلَقَدْ نَصَرَكُمُ اللّٰهُ بِبَدْرٍ﴾ (Āl ʿImrān 3:123)
