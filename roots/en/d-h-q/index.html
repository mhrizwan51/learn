Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

د ھ قd-h-q

— دِهَاقdihāq: filled to the brim, overflowing. The lexicographers derive it from اَدْهَقَ الْكَاْسَadhaqa al-kāsa (to fill a cup to the brim). Only once in the Quran, among the rewards of the people of Paradise: ﴿وَّكَاْسًا دِهَاقًا﴾ (An-Nabaʾ 78:34)
