Root و ذ ر w-dh-r

Quranic citations in this entry: 3 · اردو

و ذ رw-dh-r

(ف) وَذَرَ يَذَرُ وَذْرًاwadhara yadharu wa-dhran (original form: وَذَرَ يَوْذَرُwadhara yawdharu): to leave something; ذَرْdhar (imperative, amr): leave!; the perfect of this verb does not occur in the Quran, only the imperfect and the imperative occur. ﴿ذَرْهُمْ يَاْكُلُوْا وَيَتَمَتَّعُوْا﴾ (Al-Ḥijr 15:3); ﴿وَالَّذِيْنَ يُتَوَفَّوْنَ مِنْكُمْ وَيَذَرُوْنَ اَزْوَاجًا﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:234); ﴿لَا تُبْقِيْ وَلَا تَذَرُ﴾ (Al-Muddaththir 74:28)
