Root س ھ ل s-h-l

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

س ھ لs-h-l

(ک) سَهُلَ يَسْهُلُ سُهُوْلَةًsahula yashulu suhūlatan: to be soft, to be easy.

سَهْلٌsahl pl. سُهُوْلٌsuhūl (noun, ism dhāt): soft, level ground, plain. ﴿تَتَّخِذُوْنَ مِنْ سُهُوْلِهَا قُصُوْرًا﴾ (Al-Aʿrāf 7:74)

Form II (tafʿīl) سَهَّلَ يُسَهِّلُ تَسْهِيْلًاsahhala yusahhilu tashīlan: to make a task easy; to facilitate (this verb is not used in the Quran).
