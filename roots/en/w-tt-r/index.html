و ط رw-ṭ-r

— وَطَرwaṭar pl. أَوْطَارawṭār: need, purpose, a want that is in the heart and is fulfilled. The lexicographers say that وَطَرwaṭar is used especially of a need that has already been fulfilled. Twice in the Quran, both in Sūrat al-Aḥzāb and both in the same verse: ﴿فَلَمَّا قَضٰى زَيْدٌ مِّنْهَا وَطَرًا زَوَّجْنَاكَهَا … إِذَا قَضَوْا مِنْهُنَّ وَطَرًا﴾ (Al-Aḥzāb 33:37).
