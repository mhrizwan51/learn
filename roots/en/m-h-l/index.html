Root م ھ ل m-h-l

م ھ لm-h-l

(ف) مَهَلَ يَمْهَلُ مَهْلًاmahala yamhalu mahlan: to act with deliberateness, not to hurry. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

مُهْلٌmuhl (noun, ism dhāt): molten metal; also pus, and the dregs of oil. ﴿يُغَاثُوْا بِمَآءٍ كَالْمُهْلِ﴾ (Al-Kahf 18:29); ﴿كَالْمُهْلِ يَغْلِيْ فِي الْبُطُوْنِ﴾ (Ad-Dukhān 44:45)

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَمْهَلَ يُمْهِلُ اِمْهَالًاamhala yumhilu imhālan: to grant respite; اَمْهِلْamhil (imperative, amr): grant respite. ﴿اَمْهِلْهُمْ رُوَيْدًا﴾ (Aṭ-Ṭāriq 86:17)

Form II (tafʿīl) مَهَّلَ يُمَهِّلُ تَمْهِيْلًاmahhala yumahhilu tamhīlan: to give rein gradually; مَهِّلْmahhil (imperative, amr): give rein. ﴿فَمَهِّلِ الْكٰفِرِيْنَ﴾ (Aṭ-Ṭāriq 86:17); ﴿وَمَهِّلْهُمْ قَلِيْلًا﴾ (Al-Muzzammil 73:11)
