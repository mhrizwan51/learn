Root ث ر ی th-r-y

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ث ر یth-r-y

(س) ثَرِيَ يَثْرٰى ثَرًيthariya yathrā tharany: for soil to become wet and moist after being dry.

ثَرًيtharany (noun, ism dhāt): wetness, moisture; then also used for moist earth. ﴿وَمَا بَيْنَهُمَا وَمَا تَحْتَ الثَّرٰى﴾ (Ṭā Hā 20:6)
