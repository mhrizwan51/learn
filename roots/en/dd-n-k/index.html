Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ض ن کḍ-n-k

(ک) ضَنُكَ يَضْنُكُ ضَنَاكَةًḍanuka yaḍnuku ḍanākatan: (1) to be of weak judgement or weak intellect (2) to be narrow, straitened.

ضَنْكٌḍank: weak, straitened. ﴿فَاِنَّ لَهُۥ مَعِيْشَةً ضَنْكًا﴾ (Ṭā Hā 20:124)
