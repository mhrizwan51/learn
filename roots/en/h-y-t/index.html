Root ھ ی ت h-y-t

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ھ ی تh-y-t

— No verb is in use from the bare triliteral (thulāthī mujarrad).

هَيْتَhayta (verbal interjection, ism fiʿl): come!, come here!; it is used with the pronouns of the second person: هَيْتَ لَكَhayta laka, لَكِlaki, لَكُمَاlakumā, لَكُمْlakum, لَكُنَّlakunna, all with the same meaning. Only once in the Quran: ﴿وَغَلَّقَتِ الْاَبْوَابَ وَقَالَتْ هَيْتَ لَكَ﴾ (Yūsuf 12:23) Note: the «هَيْهَاتَhayhāta» of ﴿هَيْهَاتَ هَيْهَاتَ لِمَا تُوْعَدُوْنَ﴾ (Al-Muʾminūn 23:36) (a verbal interjection, ism fiʿl, meaning: how far it is!) is a different word.
