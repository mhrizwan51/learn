Root م ع ز m-ʿ-z

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

م ع زm-ʿ-z

(ف) مَعَزَ يَمْعَزُ مَعْزًاmaʿaza yamʿazu maʿzan: to separate goats from sheep; also to be hard and firm. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

مَعْزٌmaʿz (generic noun, ism jins): goats. Only once in the Quran: ﴿وَمِنَ الْمَعْزِ اثْنَيْنِ﴾ (Al-Anʿām 6:143)
