ق ن عq-n-ʿ

(س) قَنِعَ يَقْنَعُ قَنَاعَةًqaniʿa yaqnaʿu qanāʿatan: to be content with whatever falls to one's share, to be satisfied. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

قَانِعٌqāniʿ (active participle, ism fāʿil): the contented one, i.e. the needy person who does not beg. ﴿وَاَطْعِمُوا الْقَانِعَ وَالْمُعْتَرَّ﴾ (Al-Ḥajj 22:36)

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَقْنَعَ يُقْنِعُ اِقْنَاعًاaqnaʿa yuqniʿu iqnāʿan: to raise (the head) upward.

مُقْنِعٌmuqniʿ pl. مُقْنِعِيْنَmuqniʿīna (active participle, ism fāʿil): with heads raised. ﴿مُهْطِعِيْنَ مُقْنِعِيْ رُءُوْسِهِمْ﴾ (Ibrāhīm 14:43)
