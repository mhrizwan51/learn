Root و ز ر w-z-r

Quranic citations in this entry: 5 · اردو

و ز رw-z-r

(ض) وَزَرَ يَزِرُ وِزْرًاwazara yaziru wizran (original form: وَزَرَ يَوْزِرُwazara yawziru): (1) to take refuge in a mountain (2) to carry a heavy thing; then of bearing the burden of sin. ﴿وَلَا تَزِرُ وَازِرَةٌ وِّزْرَ اُخْرٰى﴾ (Al-Anʿām 6:164)

وِزْرٌwizr pl. اَوْزَارٌawzār (verbal noun (maṣdar), also noun (ism dhāt)): burden; the burden of sin; weapons and equipment (of war). ﴿وَوَضَعْنَا عَنْكَ وِزْرَكَ﴾ (Ash-Sharḥ 94:2); ﴿حَتّٰى تَضَعَ الْحَرْبُ اَوْزَارَهَا﴾ (Muḥammad 47:4)

وَازِرٌwāzir, feminine وَازِرَةٌwāzirah (active participle, ism fāʿil): one who bears a burden. (In the above-mentioned verse, al-Anʿām 6:164.) وَزَرٌwazar (noun, ism dhāt): refuge. Only once in the Quran: ﴿كَلَّا لَا وَزَرَ﴾ (Al-Qiyāmah 75:11)

وَزِيْرٌwazīr (adjective on the pattern of فَعِيْلٌfaʿīl): one who always shares the burden in every circumstance, helper, minister. ﴿وَاجْعَلْ لِّيْ وَزِيْرًا مِّنْ اَهْلِيْ﴾ (Ṭā Hā 20:29)
