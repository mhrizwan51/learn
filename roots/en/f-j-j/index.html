Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ف ج جf-j-j

(ن) فَجَّ يَفُجُّ فَجًّاfajja yafujju fajjan (original form: فَجَجَ يَفْجُجُfajaja yafjuju): to set the feet wide apart; to spread out, to widen. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

فَجٌّfajj pl. فِجَاجٌfijāj (adjective): wide; then used for a wide pass between two mountains. ﴿يَاْتِيْنَ مِنْ كُلِّ فَجٍّ عَمِيْقٍ﴾ (Al-Ḥajj 22:27); ﴿لِتَسْلُكُوْا مِنْهَا سُبُلًا فِجَاجًا﴾ (Nūḥ 71:20)
