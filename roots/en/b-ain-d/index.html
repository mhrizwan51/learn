Root ب ع د b-ʿ-d

Quranic citations in this entry: 6 · اردو

ب ع دb-ʿ-d

(ک) بَعُدَ يَبْعُدُ بُعْدًاbaʿuda yabʿudu buʿdan: to be far. ﴿وَلٰكِنْۢ بَعُدَتْ عَلَيْهِمُ الشُّقَّةُ﴾ (At-Tawbah 9:42)

(س) بَعِدَ يَبْعَدُ بَعَدًاbaʿida yabʿadu baʿadan: to be ruined, to perish. ﴿اَلَا بُعْدًا لِّمَدْيَنَ كَمَا بَعِدَتْ ثَمُوْدُ﴾ (Hūd 11:95)

بُعْدٌbuʿd (noun, ism dhāt): distance or remoteness; destruction. (example above) بَعِيْدٌbaʿīd (adjective on the pattern faʿīl): far. ﴿فَقَدْ ضَلَّ ضَلٰلًۢا بَعِيْدًا﴾ (An-Nisāʾ 4:116)

بَعْدُbaʿdu (noun of time, ẓarf zamān): behind someone or after someone; it mostly occurs as an annexed noun (muḍāf). If its governed noun (muḍāf ilayh) is mentioned it is declinable (muʿrab), and if it is elided it is indeclinable (mabnī) on ḍammah, بَعْدُbaʿdu. ﴿لِلّٰهِ الْاَمْرُ مِنْ قَبْلُ وَمِنْۢ بَعْدُ﴾ (Ar-Rūm 30:4)

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَبْعَدَ يُبْعِدُ اِبْعَادًاabʿada yubʿidu ibʿādan: to remove far, to keep far away; مُبْعَدٌmubʿad (passive participle, ism mafʿūl): removed far, kept far away. ﴿اُولٰٓئِكَ عَنْهَا مُبْعَدُوْنَ﴾ (Al-Anbiyāʾ 21:101)

Form III (mufāʿalah) بَاعَدَ يُبَاعِدُ مُبَاعَدَةًbāʿada yubāʿidu mubāʿadatan: to put someone far from someone; بَاعِدْbāʿid (imperative, amr): put far away. ﴿رَبَّنَا بٰعِدْ بَيْنَ اَسْفَارِنَا﴾ (Sabaʾ 34:19)
