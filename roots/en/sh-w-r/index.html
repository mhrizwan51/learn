Root ش و ر sh-w-r

Quranic citations in this entry: 3 · اردو

ش و رsh-w-r

(ن) شَارَ يَشُوْرُ شَوْرًاshāra yashūru shawran: (1) to extract the essence of a thing (2) to get to know someone.

شُوْرٰىshūrā (noun, ism dhāt): mutual consultation. ﴿وَاَمْرُهُمْ شُوْرٰى بَيْنَهُمْ﴾ (Ash-Shūrā 42:38)

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَشَارَ يُشِيْرُ اِشَارَةًashāra yushīru ishāratan: to make someone known, to point out. ﴿فَاَشَارَتْ اِلَيْهِ﴾ (Maryam 19:29)

Form III (mufāʿalah) شَاوَرَ يُشَاوِرُ مُشَاوَرَةًshāwara yushāwiru mushāwaratan: to draw the essence out of someone's mind, to take counsel. ﴿وَشَاوِرْهُمْ فِي الْاَمْرِ﴾ (Āl ʿImrān 3:159)
