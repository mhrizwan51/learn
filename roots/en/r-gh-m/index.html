Root ر غ م r-gh-m

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ر غ مr-gh-m

(س) رَغِمَ يَرْغَمُ رَغْمًاraghima yarghamu raghman: (1) to be humbled, to have one's nose rubbed in the dust (2) رَغِمَ اِلٰىraghima ilā: to go to someone.

Form III (mufāʿalah) رَاغَمَ يُرَاغِمُ مُرَاغَمَةًrāghama yurāghimu murāghamatan: to go to one another; to leave one's enemy humbled.

مُرَاغَمٌmurāgham (passive participle, ism mafʿūl, in the sense of a noun of place, ẓarf): a place to go to, refuge. ﴿يَجِدْ فِي الْاَرْضِ مُرٰغَمًا كَثِيْرًا وَّسَعَةً﴾ (An-Nisāʾ 4:100)
