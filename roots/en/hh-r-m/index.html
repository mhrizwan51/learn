Root ح ر م ḥ-r-m

Quranic citations in this entry: 5 · اردو

ح ر مḥ-r-m

(ض) حَرَمَ يَحْرِمُ حِرْمًا وَحِرْمَانًاḥarama yaḥrimu ḥirman wa-ḥirmānan: to bar (from the use of something because it is harmful; from irreverence towards something because of its sanctity); to deprive.

مَحْرُوْمٌmaḥrūm (passive participle, ism mafʿūl): one who is barred, deprived. ﴿وَفِيْٓ اَمْوَالِهِمْ حَقٌّ لِّلسَّآئِلِ وَالْمَحْرُوْمِ﴾ (Adh-Dhāriyāt 51:19)

حَرَامٌḥarām / حُرُمٌḥurum pl. حُرُمَاتٌḥurumāt: that whose use, or irreverence towards which, is barred. ﴿فَوَلِّ وَجْهَكَ شَطْرَ الْمَسْجِدِ الْحَرَامِ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:144); ﴿مِنْهَآ اَرْبَعَةٌ حُرُمٌ﴾ (At-Tawbah 9:36)

Form II (tafʿīl) حَرَّمَ يُحَرِّمُ تَحْرِيْمًاḥarrama yuḥarrimu taḥrīman: to make unlawful. ﴿وَاَحَلَّ اللّٰهُ الْبَيْعَ وَحَرَّمَ الرِّبٰوا﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:275)

مُحَرَّمٌmuḥarram (passive participle, ism mafʿūl): made unlawful. ﴿قُلْ لَّآ اَجِدُ فِيْ مَآ اُوْحِيَ اِلَيَّ مُحَرَّمًا﴾ (Al-Anʿām 6:145)

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَحْرَمَ يُحْرِمُ اِحْرَامًاaḥrama yuḥrimu iḥrāman: to enter a state of sanctity; to make the intention of Hajj or ʿUmrah (this verb is not used in the Quran).

Form VIII (iftiʿāl) اِحْتَرَمَ يَحْتَرِمُ اِحْتِرَامًاiḥtarama yaḥtarimu iḥtirāman: to regard someone as inviolable; to respect; to honour (this verb is not used in the Quran).
