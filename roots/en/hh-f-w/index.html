ح ف وḥ-f-w

(ن) حَفَا يَحْفُوْ حَفْوًاḥafā yaḥfū ḥafwan: (1) to give someone something. (2) to honour, to be kind and affectionate.

(س) حَفِيَ يَحْفٰى حَفًاḥafiya yaḥfā ḥafan: (1) of an animal, to have worn hooves from much travelling. (2) of a person, to have complete knowledge of something.

حَفِيٌّḥafiyy (adjective on the pattern of faʿīl): (1) kind, affectionate. ﴿اِنَّهُۥ كَانَ بِيْ حَفِيًّا﴾ (Maryam 19:47); (2) possessing complete knowledge. ﴿يَسْـَٔلُوْنَكَ كَاَنَّكَ حَفِيٌّ عَنْهَا﴾ (Al-Aʿrāf 7:187)

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَحْفٰى يُحْفِيْ اِحْفَاءًaḥfā yuḥfī iḥfāʾan: to press someone insistently for something. ﴿اِنْ يَّسْـَٔلْكُمُوْهَا فَيُحْفِكُمْ تَبْخَلُوْا﴾ (Muḥammad 47:37)
