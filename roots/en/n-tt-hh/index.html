Root ن ط ح n-ṭ-ḥ

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ن ط حn-ṭ-ḥ

(ف، ض) نَطَحَ يَنْطَحُ وَيَنْطِحُ نَطْحًاnaṭaḥa yanṭaḥu wa-yanṭiḥu naṭḥan: to butt with the horns. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

نَطِيْحَةٌnaṭīḥah (on the pattern of فَعِيْلٌfaʿīl, in the sense of a passive participle): an animal that dies from being gored (by another). Only once in the Quran, in the enumeration of forbidden animals: ﴿وَالْمُتَرَدِّيَةُ وَالنَّطِيْحَةُ﴾ (Al-Māʾidah 5:3)
