Quranic citations in this entry: 3 · اردو

ب ر زb-r-z

(ن) بَرَزَ يَبْرُزُ بُرُوْزًاbaraza yabruzu burūzan: to appear in the clear and open: (1) to come out from somewhere. (2) to come before someone, to appear. ﴿وَبَرَزُوْا لِلّٰهِ جَمِيْعًا﴾ (Ibrāhīm 14:21)

بَارِزٌbāriz (active participle, ism fāʿil): one who comes out, who appears; بَارِزَةٌbārizah: made clear and open. ﴿وَتَرَي الْاَرْضَ بَارِزَةً﴾ (Al-Kahf 18:47)

Form II (tafʿīl) بَرَّزَ يُبَرِّزُ تَبْرِيْزًاbarraza yubarrizu tabrīzan: to bring out, to bring forward, to make manifest. ﴿وَبُرِّزَتِ الْجَحِيْمُ لِمَنْ يَّرٰى﴾ (An-Nāziʿāt 79:36)
