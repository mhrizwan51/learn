ف ت قf-t-q

(ن، ض) فَتَقَ يَفْتُقُ وَيَفْتِقُ فَتْقًاfataqa yaftuqu wa-yaftiqu fatqan: to split apart something that is joined, to unstitch, to open — the opposite of رَتْقratq (to join, to close). ﴿اَنَّ السَّمٰوٰتِ وَالْاَرْضَ كَانَتَا رَتْقًا فَفَتَقْنٰهُمَا﴾ (Al-Anbiyāʾ 21:30). Only once in the Quran.
