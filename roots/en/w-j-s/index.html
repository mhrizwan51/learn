Root و ج س w-j-s

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

و ج سw-j-s

(ض) وَجَسَ يَجِسُ وَجْسًاwajasa yajisu wa-jsan: to be hidden; to sense a faint sound or a misgiving (in the heart). (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَوْجَسَ يُوْجِسُ اِيْجَاسًاawjasa yūjisu ījāsan: of the heart, to sense some (hidden) feeling, such as unease and fear. ﴿فَاَوْجَسَ فِيْ نَفْسِهِۦ خِيْفَةً مُّوْسٰى﴾ (Ṭā Hā 20:67); ﴿نَكِرَهُمْ وَاَوْجَسَ مِنْهُمْ خِيْفَةً﴾ (Hūd 11:70)
