Root ھ ن ء h-n-ʾ

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ھ ن ءh-n-ʾ

(ف، س) هَنَاَ يَهْنَاُ وَهَنِئَ يَهْنَاُ هَنَآءَةًhanaʾa yahnaʾu wa-haniʾa yahnaʾu hanāʾatan: (of food) to be pleasant and easily digested. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

هَنِيْٓءٌhanīʾ (adjective on the pattern of فَعِيْلٌfaʿīl): pleasant always and in every state, free of trouble. ﴿كُلُوْا وَاشْرَبُوْا هَنِيْٓـًٔۢا بِمَا كُنْتُمْ تَعْمَلُوْنَ﴾ (Aṭ-Ṭūr 52:19); ﴿فَكُلُوْهُ هَنِيْٓـًٔا مَّرِيْٓـًٔا﴾ (An-Nisāʾ 4:4)

Form II (tafʿīl) هَنَّاَ يُهَنِّئُ تَهْنِئَةًhannaʾa yuhanniʾu tahniʾatan: to congratulate (this verb is not used in the Quran).
