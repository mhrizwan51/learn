Root ذ ع ن dh-ʿ-n

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ذ ع نdh-ʿ-n

(س) ذَعِنَ يَذْعَنُ ذَعَنًاdhaʿina yadhʿanu dhaʿanan: to be submissive and obedient, to acknowledge the truth.

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَذْعَنَ يُذْعِنُ اِذْعَانًاadhʿana yudhʿinu idhʿānan: synonymous with the bare triliteral (thulāthī mujarrad).

مُذْعِنٌmudhʿin (active participle, ism fāʿil): one who submits, who acknowledges. ﴿وَاِنْ يَّكُنْ لَّهُمُ الْحَقُّ يَاْتُوْٓا اِلَيْهِ مُذْعِنِيْنَ﴾ (An-Nūr 24:49)
