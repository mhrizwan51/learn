ب ش رb-sh-r

(ن) بَشَرَ يَبْشُرُ بَشْرًاbashara yabshuru bashran: to peel off the hide and expose the skin.

(س) بَشِرَ يَبْشَرُ بَشَرًاbashira yabsharu basharan: to rejoice.

بَشَرٌbashar: human being (whose skin is exposed). ﴿قُلْ اِنَّمَآ اَنَا بَشَرٌ مِّثْلُكُمْ﴾ (Al-Kahf 18:110)

بُشْرٰىbushrā (feminine of the elative, ism tafḍīl): glad tidings at hearing which the skin of the face lights up with joy. ﴿لَهُمُ الْبُشْرٰى فِي الْحَيٰوةِ الدُّنْيَا﴾ (Yūnus 10:64)

بَشِيْرٌbashīr: bearer of glad tidings. ﴿اِنَّآ اَرْسَلْنٰكَ بِالْحَقِّ بَشِيْرًا وَّنَذِيْرًا﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:119)

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَبْشَرَ يُبْشِرُ اِبْشَارًاabshara yubshiru ibshāran: to receive glad tidings, to be given good news; imperative (amr) اَبْشِرُوْاabshirūā (joined to the following word with a kasrah). ﴿وَاَبْشِرُوْا بِالْجَنَّةِ﴾ (Fuṣṣilat 41:30)

Form II (tafʿīl) بَشَّرَ يُبَشِّرُ تَبْشِيْرًاbashshara yubashshiru tabshīran: to announce glad tidings. ﴿وَبَشِّرِ الصّٰبِرِيْنَ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:155)

Form III (mufāʿalah) بَاشَرَ يُبَاشِرُ مُبَاشَرَةًbāshara yubāshiru mubāsharatan: to live together, to have conjugal relations. ﴿فَالْئٰنَ بَاشِرُوْهُنَّ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:187)

Form X (istifʿāl) اِسْتَبْشَرَ يَسْتَبْشِرُ اِسْتِبْشَارًاistabshara yastabshiru istibshāran: to be glad, to rejoice. ﴿فَاسْتَبْشِرُوْا بِبَيْعِكُمُ الَّذِيْ بَايَعْتُمْ بِهِۦ﴾ (At-Tawbah 9:111)
