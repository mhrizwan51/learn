Root د ل و d-l-w

Quranic citations in this entry: 4 · اردو

د ل وd-l-w

(ن) دَلَا يَدْلُوْ دَلْوًاdalā yadlū dalwan: to let a bucket down into a well and draw it up; to coax and cajole someone into getting something done.

دَلْوٌdalw: a bucket.

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَدْلٰى يُدْلِيْ اِدْلَاءًadlā yudlī idlāʾan: to let something down into a well; to convey something to someone. ﴿فَاَدْلٰى دَلْوَهُۥ﴾ (Yūsuf 12:19); ﴿وَتُدْلُوْا بِهَآ اِلَي الْحُكَّامِ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:188)

Form II (tafʿīl) دَلّٰى يُدَلِّيْ تَدْلِيَةًdallā yudallī tadliyatan: to lead astray by cajolery. ﴿فَدَلّٰىهُمَا بِغُرُوْرٍ﴾ (Al-Aʿrāf 7:22)

Form V (tafaʿʿul) تَدَلّٰى يَتَدَلّٰى تَدَلِّيًاtadallā yatadallā tadalliyan: to hang down, to draw near. ﴿ثُمَّ دَنَا فَتَدَلّٰى﴾ (An-Najm 53:8)
