Root ش خ ص sh-kh-ṣ

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ش خ صsh-kh-ṣ

(ف) شَخَصَ يَشْخَصُ شُخُوْصًاshakhaṣa yashkhaṣu shukhūṣan: (of the eyes) to remain wide open, to stare fixedly. ﴿لِيَوْمٍ تَشْخَصُ فِيْهِ الْاَبْصَارُ﴾ (Ibrāhīm 14:42)

شَاخِصَةٌshākhiṣah (active participle, ism fāʿil): remaining wide open, staring. ﴿فَاِذَا هِيَ شَاخِصَةٌ اَبْصَارُ الَّذِيْنَ كَفَرُوْا﴾ (Al-Anbiyāʾ 21:97)

Form II (tafʿīl) شَخَّصَ يُشَخِّصُ تَشْخِيْصًاshakhkhaṣa yushakhkhiṣu tashkhīṣan: to identify and determine something (this verb is not used in the Quran).
