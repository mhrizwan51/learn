Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

م ی دm-y-d

(ض) مَادَ يَمِيْدُ مَيْدًاmāda yamīdu maydan (original form: مَيَدَ يَمْيِدُmayada yamyidu): to move, to quake, to sway. ﴿وَاَلْقٰى فِي الْاَرْضِ رَوَاسِيَ اَنْ تَمِيْدَ بِكُمْ﴾ (An-Naḥl 16:15)

مَآئِدَةٌmāʾidah (feminine of the active participle (ism fāʿil) مَآئِدٌmāʾid): that which sways; thence used of a table laid with food, because the food set upon it keeps moving about among the guests; the name of Sūrat al-Māʾidah is from this. ﴿رَبَّنَآ اَنْزِلْ عَلَيْنَا مَآئِدَةً مِّنَ السَّمَآءِ﴾ (Al-Māʾidah 5:114)
