Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ج ب نj-b-n

— From this root also comes جُبْنjubn (cowardice).

جَبِيْنjabīn pl. أَجْبِنَة وَجُبُنajbinah wa-jubun: the side of the forehead, the part near the temple; the lexicographers say the forehead has two جَبِينjabīn and in between is the جَبْهَةjabhah. Only once in the Quran: ﴿وَتَلَّهُۥ لِلْجَبِيْنِ﴾ (Aṣ-Ṣāffāt 37:103)
