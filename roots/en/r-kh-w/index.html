Root ر خ و r-kh-w

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ر خ وr-kh-w

(ک) رَخُوَ يَرْخُوْ رَخَاوَةًrakhuwa yarkhū rakhāwatan: to be soft and slack.

رُخَاءٌrukhāʾ: a soft, pleasant wind, one that is not violent. Only once in the Quran, describing the wind made subservient to Sulaymān (peace be upon him): ﴿تَجْرِيْ بِاَمْرِهِۦ رُخَآءً حَيْثُ اَصَابَ﴾ (Ṣād 38:36)
