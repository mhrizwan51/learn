Root ن ش ز n-sh-z

Quranic citations in this entry: 4 · اردو

ن ش زn-sh-z

(ن، ض) نَشَزَ يَنْشُزُ وَيَنْشِزُ نَشْزًا وَنُشُوْزًاnashaza yanshuzu wa-yanshizu nashzan wa-nushūzan: to protrude from one's place, to rise up; to rise (from an assembly); also (of either spouse) to be rebellious and ill-treat the other. ﴿وَاِذَا قِيْلَ انْشُزُوْا فَانْشُزُوْا﴾ (Al-Mujādilah 58:11)

نُشُوْزٌnushūz (verbal noun, maṣdar): rebelliousness, ill-treatment. ﴿وَالّٰتِيْ تَخَافُوْنَ نُشُوْزَهُنَّ﴾ (An-Nisāʾ 4:34); ﴿وَاِنِ امْرَاَةٌ خَافَتْ مِنْۢ بَعْلِهَا نُشُوْزًا﴾ (An-Nisāʾ 4:128)

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَنْشَزَ يُنْشِزُ اِنْشَازًاanshaza yunshizu inshāzan: to raise something from its place, to lift it up (and join it together). ﴿وَانْظُرْ اِلَي الْعِظَامِ كَيْفَ نُنْشِزُهَا﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:259)
