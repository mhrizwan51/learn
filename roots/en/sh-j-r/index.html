Quranic citations in this entry: 3 · اردو

ش ج رsh-j-r

(ن) شَجَرَ يَشْجُرُ شَجْرًاshajara yashjuru shajran: to differ with one another, to dispute. ﴿حَتّٰى يُحَكِّمُوْكَ فِيْمَا شَجَرَ بَيْنَهُمْ﴾ (An-Nisāʾ 4:65)

شَجَرٌshajar (generic noun, ism jins, singular شَجَرَةٌshajarah): tree (because the branches of a tree spread out in different directions). ﴿وَلَا تَقْرَبَا هٰذِهِ الشَّجَرَةَ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:35); ﴿وَّالنَّجْمُ وَالشَّجَرُ يَسْجُدٰنِ﴾ (Ar-Raḥmān 55:6)
