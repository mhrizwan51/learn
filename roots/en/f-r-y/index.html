Root ف ر ی f-r-y

Quranic citations in this entry: 3 · اردو

ف ر یf-r-y

(ض) فَرٰى يَفْرِيْ فَرْيًاfarā yafrī faryan (original form: فَرَيَ يَفْرِيُfaraya yafriyu): (1) to cut, to slit (2) to say something false or baseless. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

فَرِيٌّfariyy (adjective on the pattern of فَعِيْلٌfaʿīl): strange and astonishing; according to some, a fabricated, very evil thing. ﴿لَقَدْ جِئْتِ شَيْئًا فَرِيًّا﴾ (Maryam 19:27)

Form VIII (iftiʿāl) اِفْتَرٰى يَفْتَرِيْ اِفْتِرَآءًiftarā yaftarī iftirāʾan (original form: اِفْتَرَيَ يَفْتَرِيُiftaraya yaftariyu): to say something baseless deliberately, to fabricate a lie. ﴿اَمْ يَقُوْلُوْنَ افْتَرٰىهُ﴾ (Yūnus 10:38); ﴿وَمَنْ اَظْلَمُ مِمَّنِ افْتَرٰى عَلَي اللّٰهِ كَذِبًا﴾ (Al-Anʿām 6:21); مُفْتَرٍmuftar (active participle (ism fāʿil)): one who fabricates a lie; مُفْتَرًيmuftarany (passive participle (ism mafʿūl)): fabricated.
