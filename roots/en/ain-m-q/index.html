Root ع م ق ʿ-m-q

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ع م قʿ-m-q

(س، ک) عَمِقَ يَعْمَقُʿamiqa yaʿmaqu / عَمُقَ يَعْمُقُ عُمْقًاʿamuqa yaʿmuqu ʿumqan: (1) to be far off, to be long or extended (2) to be deep. (this verb is not used in the Quran).

عَمِيْقٌʿamīq (adjective on the pattern of فَعِيْلٌfaʿīl): far off, deep. ﴿يَاْتِيْنَ مِنْ كُلِّ فَجٍّ عَمِيْقٍ﴾ (Al-Ḥajj 22:27)
