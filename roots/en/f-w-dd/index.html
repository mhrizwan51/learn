Root ف و ض f-w-ḍ

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ف و ضf-w-ḍ

— No bare triliteral verb is in use.

Form II (tafʿīl) فَوَّضَ يُفَوِّضُ تَفْوِيْضًاfawwaḍa yufawwiḍu tafwīḍan: to entrust the whole matter to someone, to commit it to him — and this is the highest form of reliance upon God (tawakkul). Only once in the Quran, spoken by the believer of Pharaoh's household: ﴿وَاُفَوِّضُ اَمْرِيْٓ اِلَي اللّٰهِ اِنَّ اللّٰهَ بَصِيْرٌۢ بِالْعِبَادِ﴾ (Ghāfir 40:44)
