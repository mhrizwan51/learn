Root ذ و د dh-w-d

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ذ و دdh-w-d

(ن) ذَادَ يَذُوْدُ ذَوْدًاdhāda yadhūdu dhawdan (original form: ذَوَدَ يَذْوُدُdhawada yadhwudu): to drive back, to keep away; especially to hold animals back from water. Only once in the Quran: ﴿وَوَجَدَ مِنْ دُوْنِهِمُ امْرَاَتَيْنِ تَذُوْدٰنِ﴾ (Al-Qaṣaṣ 28:23)
