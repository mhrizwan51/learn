Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ر ی شr-y-sh

(ض) رَاشَ يَرِيْشُ رَيْشًاrāsha yarīshu rayshan: to set someone's condition right.

رِيْشٌrīsh: a bird's feathers; and figuratively the clothing and adornment that covers a person and beautifies him — the Quran uses it in this sense. Only once in the Quran: ﴿قَدْ اَنْزَلْنَا عَلَيْكُمْ لِبَاسًا يُّوَارِيْ سَوْاٰتِكُمْ وَرِيْشًا﴾ (Al-Aʿrāf 7:26)
