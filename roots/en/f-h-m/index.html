Root ف ھ م f-h-m

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ف ھ مf-h-m

(س) فَهِمَ يَفْهَمُ فَهْمًاfahima yafhamu fahman: to understand the reality of a matter or statement. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

Form II (tafʿīl) فَهَّمَ يُفَهِّمُ تَفْهِيْمًاfahhama yufahhimu tafhīman: to make understand, to grant understanding. This root occurs only once in the Quran: ﴿فَفَهَّمْنٰهَا سُلَيْمٰنَ﴾ (Al-Anbiyāʾ 21:79)

Form III (mufāʿalah) فَاهَمَ يُفَاهِمُ مُفَاهَمَةًfāhama yufāhimu mufāhamatan: to understand one another; to reach mutual understanding (this verb is not used in the Quran).
