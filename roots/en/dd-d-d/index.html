Root ض د د ḍ-d-d

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ض د دḍ-d-d

(ن) ضَدَّ يَضُدُّ ضَدًّاḍadda yaḍuddu ḍaddan: to prevail in a dispute.

ضِدٌّḍidd: opponent, adversary. ﴿وَيَكُوْنُوْنَ عَلَيْهِمْ ضِدًّا﴾ (Maryam 19:82)

Form VI (tafāʿul) تَضَادَّ يَتَضَادُّ تَضَادًّاtaḍādda yataḍāddu taḍāddan: (of two things) to be opposites of each other (this verb is not used in the Quran).
