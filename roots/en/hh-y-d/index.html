ح ی دḥ-y-d

(ض) حَيَدَ يَحْيِدُḥayada yaḥyidu (original form) ⟵ حَادَ يَحِيْدُ حَيْدًا وَحُيُوْدًاḥāda yaḥīdu ḥaydan wa-ḥuyūdan: to shy away, to turn aside, to evade. Only once in the Quran: ﴿ذٰلِكَ مَا كُنْتَ مِنْهُ تَحِيْدُ﴾ (Qāf 50:19) — i.e. death is the very thing from which you used to flee.
