Root ج و ز j-w-z

Quranic citations in this entry: 3 · اردو

ج و زj-w-z

(ن) جَازَ يَجُوْزُ جَوْزًاjāza yajūzu jawzan: to be in the middle of something, to pass through.

Form II (tafʿīl) جَوَّزَ يُجَوِّزُ تَجْوِيْزًاjawwaza yujawwizu tajwīzan: to declare permissible; to consider allowable (this verb is not used in the Quran).

Form III (mufāʿalah) جَاوَزَ يُجَاوِزُ مُجَاوَزَةًjāwaza yujāwizu mujāwazatan: to go beyond the middle, to pass, to pass through the middle of a river, i.e. to cross it. ﴿فَلَمَّا جَاوَزَهُۥ هُوَ وَالَّذِيْنَ اٰمَنُوْا مَعَهُۥ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:249); ﴿وَجٰوَزْنَا بِبَنِيْٓ اِسْرَآءِيْلَ الْبَحْرَ﴾ (Al-Aʿrāf 7:138)

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَجَازَ يُجِيْزُ اِجَازَةًajāza yujīzu ijāzatan: to allow; to permit something (this verb is not used in the Quran).

Form VI (tafāʿul) تَجَاوَزَ يَتَجَاوَزُ تَجَاوُزًاtajāwaza yatajāwazu tajāwuzan: to pass beyond something, to overlook it. ﴿وَنَتَجَاوَزُ عَنْ سَيِّاٰتِهِمْ﴾ (Al-Aḥqāf 46:16)
