Root ق ر ف q-r-f

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ق ر فq-r-f

(ض) قَرَفَ يَقْرِفُ قَرْفًاqarafa yaqrifu qarfan: to strip the bark off a tree, to peel something. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

Form VIII (iftiʿāl) اِقْتَرَفَ يَقْتَرِفُ اِقْتِرَافًاiqtarafa yaqtarifu iqtirāfan: to earn deliberately; to commit (evil).

مُقْتَرِفٌmuqtarif pl. مُقْتَرِفُوْنَmuqtarifūna (active participle, ism fāʿil): one who earns, one who commits. ﴿وَمَنْ يَّقْتَرِفْ حَسَنَةً نَّزِدْ لَهُۥ فِيْهَا حُسْنًا﴾ (Ash-Shūrā 42:23); ﴿وَاَمْوَالُ نِ اقْتَرَفْتُمُوْهَا﴾ (At-Tawbah 9:24)
