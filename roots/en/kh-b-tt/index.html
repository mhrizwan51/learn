Root خ ب ط kh-b-ṭ

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

خ ب طkh-b-ṭ

(ض) خَبَطَ يَخْبِطُ خَبْطًاkhabaṭa yakhbiṭu khabṭan: to strike at random, to flail about blindly; the original sense is a camel's striking with its feet heedlessly.

Form V (tafaʿʿul) تَخَبَّطَ يَتَخَبَّطُ تَخَبُّطًاtakhabbaṭa yatakhabbaṭu takhabbuṭan: to strike someone like a madman, to drive out of one's senses by a touch. Only once in the Quran, and that in Form V (tafaʿʿul): ﴿كَمَا يَقُوْمُ الَّذِيْ يَتَخَبَّطُهُ الشَّيْطٰنُ مِنَ الْمَسِّ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:275)
