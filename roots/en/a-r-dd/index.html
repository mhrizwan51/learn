Root أ ر ض ʾ-r-ḍ

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

أ ر ضʾ-r-ḍ

— The bare triliteral is not used in the Quran; the lexicographers record أَرَضَتِ الأَرْضُaraḍati al-ʾarḍ (to sprout herbage) and أَرِضَتْariḍat (to be soft and fertile).

أَرْضarḍ pl. أَرَضُون وَأَرَاضِيaraḍūn wa-ʾarāḍī: earth, land; feminine by usage. In the Quran it occurs in all three senses: (1) the whole earth, (2) a particular territory, (3) the lower level. ﴿اَلَّذِيْ جَعَلَ لَكُمُ الْاَرْضَ فِرَاشًا﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:22)

أَرِيضarīḍ (pattern (wazn) فَعِيلfaʿīl): fertile, spacious.

أَرَضَةaraḍah: the worm that eats wood (termite). This is what is meant in the Quran by "دَآبَّةُ الْاَرْضِdābbatu al-ʾarḍ": ﴿اِلَّا دَآبَّةُ الْاَرْضِ تَاْكُلُ مِنْسَاَتَهُۥ﴾ (Sabaʾ 34:14)
