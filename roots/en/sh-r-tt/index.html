Root ش ر ط sh-r-ṭ

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ش ر طsh-r-ṭ

(ن) شَرَطَ يَشْرُطُ شَرْطًاsharaṭa yashruṭu sharṭan: to set a mark, to stipulate a condition.

شَرَطٌsharaṭ pl. اَشْرَاطٌashrāṭ: sign, token; «اَشْرَاطُ السَّاعَةِashrāṭu as-sāʿat» i.e. the signs of the Resurrection. Only once in the Quran: ﴿فَقَدْ جَآءَ اَشْرَاطُهَا﴾ (Muḥammad 47:18)

شَرِيْطَةٌsharīṭah: condition, stipulation.
