Root س ج ل s-j-l

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

س ج لs-j-l

(ن) سَجَلَ يَسْجُلُ سَجْلًاsajala yasjulu sajlan: (1) to pour water from above (2) to read a book continuously.

سِجِلٌّsijill (noun, ism dhāt): the sheets on which claims and judgements are written and which the judge keeps in his custody; the court record (judicial record). ﴿يَوْمَ نَطْوِي السَّمَآءَ كَطَيِّ السِّجِلِّ لِلْكُتُبِ﴾ (Al-Anbiyāʾ 21:104)

سِجِّيْلٌsijjīl (noun, ism dhāt): pellets of wet clay hardened by baking in fire; stones of clay; pebbles. ﴿حِجَارَةً مِّنْ سِجِّيْلٍ﴾ (Al-Ḥijr 15:74)
