Root و ف ض w-f-ḍ

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

و ف ضw-f-ḍ

— أَوْفَضَ يُوْفِضُ إِيْفَاضًاawfaḍa yūfiḍu īfāḍan (verb form ifʿāl): to run swiftly; to rush. The lexicographers say the basic sense is haste and agitation. Only once in the Quran, in the scene of the dead emerging from their graves on the Day of Resurrection: ﴿يَوْمَ يَخْرُجُوْنَ مِنَ الْأَجْدَاثِ سِرَاعًا كَأَنَّهُمْ إِلٰى نُصُبٍ يُّوْفِضُوْنَ﴾ (Al-Maʿārij 70:43).
