Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ب س قb-s-q

(ن) بَسَقَ يَبْسُقُ بُسُوْقًاbasaqa yabsuqu busūqan: to be tall and lofty, to rise high and tower over.

بَاسِقَاتbāsiqāt (active participle (ism fāʿil), feminine plural): tall and lofty. Only once in the Quran: ﴿وَالنَّخْلَ بَاسِقَاتٍ لَّهَا طَلْعٌ نَّضِيْدٌ﴾ (Qāf 50:10)
