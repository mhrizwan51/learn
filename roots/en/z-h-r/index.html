ز ھ رz-h-r

(س) زَهِرَ يَزْهَرُ زَهَرًاzahira yazharu zaharan: to be beautiful; to be radiant.

زَهْرَةٌzahrah (noun, ism dhāt): beauty; splendour; originally: bud and blossom. ﴿زَهْرَةَ الْحَيٰوةِ الدُّنْيَا﴾ (Ṭā Hā 20:131)
