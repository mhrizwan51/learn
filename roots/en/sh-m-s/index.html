Root ش م س sh-m-s

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ش م سsh-m-s

— شَمْسٌshams pl. شُمُوْسٌshumūs: the sun; it is feminine by usage (muʾannath samāʿī). The lexicographers say that the original sense is of height and appearing. ﴿وَالشَّمْسِ وَضُحٰهَا﴾ (Ash-Shams 91:1); ﴿لَا الشَّمْسُ يَنْۢبَغِيْ لَهَآ اَنْ تُدْرِكَ الْقَمَرَ﴾ (Yā Sīn 36:40)

شَمُوْسٌshamūs: a restive horse.This root occurs 33 times in the Quran, every time as a noun.
