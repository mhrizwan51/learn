س ل قs-l-q

(ن) سَلَقَ يَسْلُقُ سَلْقًاsalaqa yasluqu salqan: to wound with a sharp, bitter tongue, to be abusive; the original sense is that of flaying the skin and plunging into boiling water, which is why it is also used of harsh speech. It occurs in the description of the hypocrites, i.e. they pierced you with sharp tongues. ﴿سَلَقُوْكُمْ بِاَلْسِنَةٍ حِدَادٍ﴾ (Al-Aḥzāb 33:19)Only once in the Quran.
