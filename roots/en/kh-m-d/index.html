Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

خ م دkh-m-d

(ن، س) خَمَدَ يَخْمُدُkhamada yakhmudu / خَمِدَ يَخْمَدُ خَمْدًا وَخُمُوْدًاkhamida yakhmadu khamdan wa-khumūdan: (of flames) to die down while the embers are not yet extinguished; to wither, to be extinguished and lie still.

خَامِدٌkhāmid pl. خٰمِدُوْنَkhāmidūna (active participle, ism fāʿil): extinguished, lifeless. ﴿فَاِذَا هُمْ خٰمِدُوْنَ﴾ (Yā Sīn 36:29); ﴿حَتّٰى جَعَلْنٰهُمْ حَصِيْدًا خٰمِدِيْنَ﴾ (Al-Anbiyāʾ 21:15)
