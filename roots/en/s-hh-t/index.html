Root س ح ت s-ḥ-t

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

س ح تs-ḥ-t

(ف) سَحَتَ يَسْحَتُ سَحْتًاsaḥata yasḥatu saḥtan: to peel the skin off fruit or strip the fat from meat; also to destroy someone.

سُحْتٌsuḥt (noun, ism dhāt): unlawful and impure earnings, which bring disgrace in this world and ruin in the Hereafter. ﴿اَكّٰلُوْنَ لِلسُّحْتِ﴾ (Al-Māʾidah 5:42)

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَسْحَتَ يُسْحِتُ اِسْحَاتًاasḥata yusḥitu isḥātan: to uproot; to exterminate. ﴿فَيُسْحِتَكُمْ بِعَذَابٍ﴾ (Ṭā Hā 20:61)
