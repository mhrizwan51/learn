Root ر ق ی r-q-y

Quranic citations in this entry: 4 · اردو

ر ق یr-q-y

(س) رَقِيَ يَرْقٰى رُقِيًّاraqiya yarqā ruqiyyan: to climb up onto something. ﴿اَوْ تَرْقٰى فِي السَّمَآءِ ۭ وَلَنْ نُّؤْمِنَ لِرُقِيِّكَ﴾ (Al-Isrāʾ 17:93)

تَرْقُوَةٌtarquwah pl. تَرَاقِيْtarāqī (noun, ism dhāt): collarbone (because when the soul departs the breath rises up to the collarbone). ﴿كَلَّآ اِذَا بَلَغَتِ التَّرَاقِيَ﴾ (Al-Qiyāmah 75:26)

(ض) رَقٰى يَرْقِيْ رَقْيًاraqā yarqī raqyan: to recite a charm or incantation in order to bring benefit or ward off harm.

رَاقٍrāq (active participle, ism fāʿil): one who recites charms, an enchanter. ﴿وَقِيْلَ مَنْ ۜ رَاقٍ﴾ (Al-Qiyāmah 75:27)

Form V (tafaʿʿul) تَرَقّٰى يَتَرَقّٰى تَرَقِّيًاtaraqqā yataraqqā taraqqiyan: to rise step by step; to advance; to progress (this verb is not used in the Quran).

Form VIII (iftiʿāl) اِرْتَقٰى يَرْتَقِيْ اِرْتِقَاءًirtaqā yartaqī irtiqāʾan: to ascend deliberately, to climb up. ﴿فَلْيَرْتَقُوْا فِي الْاَسْبَابِ﴾ (Ṣād 38:10)
