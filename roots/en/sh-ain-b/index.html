Root ش ع ب sh-ʿ-b

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ش ع بsh-ʿ-b

(ف) شَعَبَ يَشْعَبُ شَعْبًاshaʿaba yashʿabu shaʿban: it is one of the words of opposite senses (aḍdād) — both to gather and to separate.

شَعْبٌshaʿb pl. شُعُوْبٌshuʿūb: a great national branch, the largest human group from which tribes branch off. ﴿وَجَعَلْنٰكُمْ شُعُوْبًا وَّقَبَآئِلَ لِتَعَارَفُوْا﴾ (Al-Ḥujurāt 49:13)

شُعْبَةٌshuʿbah pl. شُعَبٌshuʿab: branch, portion. ﴿اِنْطَلِقُوْٓا اِلٰى ظِلٍّ ذِيْ ثَلٰثِ شُعَبٍ﴾ (Al-Mursalāt 77:30)

شِعْبٌshiʿb: a درّهdrrh between mountains.This root occurs twice in the Quran.
