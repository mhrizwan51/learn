Quranic citations in this entry: 3 · اردو

ر ھ نr-h-n

(ف) رَهَنَ يَرْهَنُ رَهْنًاrahana yarhanu rahnan: to take a security for something, to give as a pledge.

رِهَانٌrihān (plural of رَهْنٌrahn; noun, ism dhāt): security, things held in pledge. ﴿فَرِهٰنٌ مَّقْبُوْضَةٌ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:283)

رَهِيْنٌrahīn / رَهِيْنَةٌrahīnah (on the pattern of فَعِيْلٌfaʿīl, in the sense of a passive participle, ism mafʿūl): pledged, held in pawn. ﴿كُلُّ نَفْسٍۢ بِمَا كَسَبَتْ رَهِيْنَةٌ﴾ (Al-Muddaththir 74:38); ﴿كُلُّ امْرِئٍۢ بِمَا كَسَبَ رَهِيْنٌ﴾ (Aṭ-Ṭūr 52:21)
