Root ث ب ر th-b-r

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ث ب رth-b-r

(ن) ثَبَرَ يَثْبُرُ ثُبُوْرًاthabara yathburu thubūran: to be laid waste, to perish (intransitive); ثَبْرًاthabran: to lay waste, to destroy (transitive).

ثُبُوْرٌthubūr: destruction, ruin. ﴿دَعَوْا هُنَالِكَ ثُبُوْرًا﴾ (Al-Furqān 25:13)

مَثْبُوْرٌmathbūr (passive participle, ism mafʿūl): laid waste, destroyed. ﴿وَاِنِّيْ لَاَظُنُّكَ يٰفِرْعَوْنُ مَثْبُوْرًا﴾ (Al-Isrāʾ 17:102)
