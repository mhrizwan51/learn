Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

س غ بs-gh-b

(س) سَغِبَ يَسْغَبُ سَغَبًاsaghiba yasghabu saghaban: to be hungry; to be starving.

مَسْغَبَةٌmasghabah (noun of time or place (ẓarf) / mīmī verbal noun (maṣdar mīmī)): a day of hunger; a time of famine; i.e. feeding on a day of hunger. ﴿اَوْ اِطْعٰمٌ فِيْ يَوْمٍ ذِيْ مَسْغَبَةٍ﴾ (Al-Balad 90:14)Only once in the Quran.
