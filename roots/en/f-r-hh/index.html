Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ف ر حf-r-ḥ

(س) فَرِحَ يَفْرَحُ فَرَحًاfariḥa yafraḥu faraḥan: (1) to be very glad (2) to be puffed up with joy, to exult. ﴿فَبِذٰلِكَ فَلْيَفْرَحُوْا﴾ (Yūnus 10:58)

فَرِحٌfariḥ pl. فَرِحُوْنَfariḥūna (adjective on the pattern of فَعِلٌfaʿil): one who rejoices greatly; one who exults. ﴿اِنَّ اللّٰهَ لَا يُحِبُّ الْفَرِحِيْنَ﴾ (Al-Qaṣaṣ 28:76)

Form II (tafʿīl) فَرَّحَ يُفَرِّحُ تَفْرِيْحًاfarraḥa yufarriḥu tafrīḥan: to make someone happy; to gladden the heart (this verb is not used in the Quran).
