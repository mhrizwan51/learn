ب خ عb-kh-ʿ

(ف) بَخَعَ يَبْخَعُ بَخْعًاbakhaʿa yabkhaʿu bakhʿan: to bring oneself to the point of destruction through grief and غصّهghṣṣh.

بَاخِعٌbākhiʿ (active participle, ism fāʿil): one who brings himself to destruction. ﴿فَلَعَلَّكَ بَاخِعٌ نَّفْسَكَ﴾ (Al-Kahf 18:6)
