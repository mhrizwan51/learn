Root و ر د w-r-d

Quranic citations in this entry: 8 · اردو

و ر دw-r-d

(ض) وَرَدَ يَرِدُ وُرُوْدًاwarada yaridu wurūdan (original form: وَرَدَ يَوْرِدُwarada yawridu): to reach or come to water (a watering place); then of arriving at any place. ﴿وَلَمَّا وَرَدَ مَآءَ مَدْيَنَ﴾ (Al-Qaṣaṣ 28:23)

وَارِدٌwārid (active participle, ism fāʿil): (1) one who arrives (2) the water-drawer (for a caravan), water-carrier. ﴿وَاِنْ مِّنْكُمْ اِلَّا وَارِدُهَا﴾ (Maryam 19:71); ﴿فَاَرْسَلُوْا وَارِدَهُمْ فَاَدْلٰى دَلْوَهُۥ﴾ (Yūsuf 12:19)

وِرْدٌwird (noun, ism dhāt): (1) the place where water is reached, watering place (2) the thirsty (who come to the watering place); مَوْرُوْدٌmawrūd (passive participle, ism mafʿūl): that which is reached. ﴿وَنَسُوْقُ الْمُجْرِمِيْنَ اِلٰى جَهَنَّمَ وِرْدًا﴾ (Maryam 19:86); ﴿وَبِئْسَ الْوِرْدُ الْمَوْرُوْدُ﴾ (Hūd 11:98)

وَرِيْدٌwarīd (on the pattern of فَعِيْلٌfaʿīl): the vein of the neck through which blood reaches the whole body (حَبْلُ الْوَرِيْدḥablu al-warīd); it is this vein that is cut at slaughter. Only once in the Quran: ﴿وَنَحْنُ اَقْرَبُ اِلَيْهِ مِنْ حَبْلِ الْوَرِيْدِ﴾ (Qāf 50:16)

(ک) وَرُدَ يَوْرُدُ وُرُوْدَةًwaruda yawrudu wurūdatan: to be yellowish red, i.e. rose-coloured. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

وَرْدٌward (rose): (this word is not used in the Quran).

وَرْدَةٌwardah (noun, ism dhāt): rosy colour; rose. Only once in the Quran: ﴿فَاِذَا انْشَقَّتِ السَّمَآءُ فَكَانَتْ وَرْدَةً كَالدِّهَانِ﴾ (Ar-Raḥmān 55:37)

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَوْرَدَ يُوْرِدُ اِيْرَادًاawrada yūridu īrādan: to bring someone to a place (to a watering place). ﴿يَقْدُمُ قَوْمَهُۥ يَوْمَ الْقِيٰمَةِ فَاَوْرَدَهُمُ النَّارَ﴾ (Hūd 11:98)
