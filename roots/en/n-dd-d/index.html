Root ن ض د n-ḍ-d

Quranic citations in this entry: 3 · اردو

ن ض دn-ḍ-d

(ض) نَضَدَ يَنْضِدُ نَضْدًاnaḍada yanḍidu naḍdan: to pile goods one upon another, to arrange in layers. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

مَنْضُوْدٌmanḍūd (passive participle, ism mafʿūl): piled up in layers. ﴿وَّطَلْحٍ مَّنْضُوْدٍ﴾ (Al-Wāqiʿah 56:29); ﴿حِجَارَةً مِّنْ سِجِّيْلٍ مَّنْضُوْدٍ﴾ (Hūd 11:82)

نَضِيْدٌnaḍīd (adjective on the pattern of فَعِيْلٌfaʿīl): layered, closely set. ﴿لَّهَا طَلْعٌ نَّضِيْدٌ﴾ (Qāf 50:10)
