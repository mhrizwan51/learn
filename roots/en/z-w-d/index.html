Root ز و د z-w-d

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ز و دz-w-d

(ن) زَادَ يَزُوْدُ زَوْدًاzāda yazūdu zawdan: to provide travelling provisions.

زَادٌzād (noun, ism dhāt): provisions for the journey. ﴿فَاِنَّ خَيْرَ الزَّادِ التَّقْوٰى﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:197)

Form V (tafaʿʿul) تَزَوَّدَ يَتَزَوَّدُ تَزَوُّدًاtazawwada yatazawwadu tazawwudan: to take provisions along for a journey. ﴿وَتَزَوَّدُوْا﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:197)
