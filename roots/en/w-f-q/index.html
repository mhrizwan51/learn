Root و ف ق w-f-q

Quranic citations in this entry: 3 · اردو

و ف قw-f-q

(ض) وَفَقَ يَفِقُ وَفْقًاwafaqa yafiqu wa-fqan (original form: وَفَقَ يَوْفِقُwafaqa yawfiqu): to be fitting and suitable. (this verb is not used in the Quran).

وِفَاقٌwifāq (verbal noun, maṣdar): correspondence, fit; "جَزَآءً وِّفَاقًاjazāʾan wwifāqan" = a recompense that exactly matches the deed. Only once in the Quran: ﴿جَزَآءً وِّفَاقًا﴾ (An-Nabaʾ 78:26)

Form II (tafʿīl) وَفَّقَ يُوَفِّقُ تَوْفِيْقًاwaffaqa yuwaffiqu tawfīqan: to bring the means into accord (with the aim), to grant success (tawfīq); to bring about agreement (between two parties). ﴿اِنْ يُّرِيْدَآ اِصْلَاحًا يُّوَفِّقِ اللّٰهُ بَيْنَهُمَا﴾ (An-Nisāʾ 4:35); ﴿وَمَا تَوْفِيْقِيْٓ اِلَّا بِاللّٰهِ﴾ (Hūd 11:88)

Form III (mufāʿalah) وَافَقَ يُوَافِقُ مُوَافَقَةًwāfaqa yuwāfiqu muwāfaqatan: to be in accord with someone. (this verb is not used in the Quran).

Form VIII (iftiʿāl) اِتَّفَقَ يَتَّفِقُ اِتِّفَاقًاittafaqa yattafiqu ittifāqan (اصلاً اِوْتَفَقَaṣlan iwtafaqa): to agree with one another. (this verb is not used in the Quran).
