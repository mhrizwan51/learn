Root ل ج ء l-j-ʾ

Quranic citations in this entry: 3 · اردو

ل ج ءl-j-ʾ

(ف) لَجَاَ يَلْجَاُ لَجْئًاlajaʾa yaljaʾu lajʾan: to take refuge in a place or with someone (this verb is not used in the Quran).

مَلْجَاٌmaljaʾ (noun of time or place, ẓarf, on the pattern of مَفْعَلٌmafʿal): a place of refuge, a shelter. ﴿لَوْ يَجِدُوْنَ مَلْجَـًٔا اَوْ مَغٰرٰتٍ اَوْ مُدَّخَلًا﴾ (At-Tawbah 9:57); ﴿وَظَنُّوْٓا اَنْ لَّا مَلْجَاَ مِنَ اللّٰهِ اِلَّآ اِلَيْهِ﴾ (At-Tawbah 9:118); ﴿مَا لَكُمْ مِّنْ مَّلْجَاٍ يَّوْمَئِذٍ﴾ (Ash-Shūrā 42:47)

Form VIII (iftiʿāl) اِلْتَجَاَ يَلْتَجِئُ اِلْتِجَاءًiltajaʾa yaltajiʾu iltijāʾan: to come under someone's protection; to seek refuge (this verb is not used in the Quran).
