Root خ ب ر kh-b-r

Quranic citations in this entry: 3 · اردو

خ ب رkh-b-r

(ن) خَبَرَ يَخْبُرُ خُبْرًا وَخِبْرَةًkhabara yakhburu khubran wa-khibratan: to learn the inner reality of something by testing it and by experience. ﴿وَكَيْفَ تَصْبِرُ عَلٰى مَا لَمْ تُحِطْ بِهِۦ خُبْرًا﴾ (Al-Kahf 18:68)

(ک) خَبُرَ يَخْبُرُ خُبُوْرًاkhabura yakhburu khubūran: to know something thoroughly, to be well acquainted with it.

خَبَرٌkhabar pl. اَخْبَارٌakhbār (noun, ism dhāt): awareness, news, information. ﴿يَوْمَئِذٍ تُحَدِّثُ اَخْبَارَهَا﴾ (Az-Zalzalah 99:4)

خَبِيْرٌkhabīr (adjective on the pattern of فَعِيْلٌfaʿīl): one who is aware of the inner reality — it differs from عَلِيْمʿalīm in that khabīr denotes knowledge of hidden realities. ﴿وَهُوَ اللَّطِيْفُ الْخَبِيْرُ﴾ (Al-Mulk 67:14)

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَخْبَرَ يُخْبِرُ اِخْبَارًاakhbara yukhbiru ikhbāran: to inform.
