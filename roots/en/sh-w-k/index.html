Root ش و ک sh-w-k

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ش و کsh-w-k

(ن) شَاكَ يَشُوْكُ شَوْكًاshāka yashūku shawkan: to prick someone with a thorn.

شَوْكٌshawk (generic noun (ism jins), singular شَوْكَةٌshawkah) pl. اَشْوَاكٌashwāk: thorn; also weapons of war and might. ﴿وَتَوَدُّوْنَ اَنَّ غَيْرَ ذَاتِ الشَّوْكَةِ تَكُوْنُ لَكُمْ﴾ (Al-Anfāl 8:7)
