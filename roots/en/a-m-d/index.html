Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ا م دʾ-m-d

(س) اَمِدَ يَاْمَدُ اَمَدًاamida yāmadu amadan: to be angry (in view of the outcome of something).

اَمَدٌamad: the furthest limit of a thing, a term. ﴿فَطَالَ عَلَيْهِمُ الْاَمَدُ فَقَسَتْ قُلُوْبُهُمْ﴾ (Al-Ḥadīd 57:16)
