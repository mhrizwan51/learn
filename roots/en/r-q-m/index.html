Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ر ق مr-q-m

(ن) رَقَمَ يَرْقُمُ رَقْمًاraqama yarqumu raqman: to write in bold letters, to inscribe.

مَرْقُوْمٌmarqūm (passive participle, ism mafʿūl): written, inscribed. ﴿كِتٰبٌ مَّرْقُوْمٌ﴾ (Al-Muṭaffifīn 83:9)

رَقِيْمٌraqīm (pattern of فَعِيْلٌfaʿīl, in the sense of a passive participle, ism mafʿūl): an inscribed tablet, a tablet with words engraved on it. ﴿اَنَّ اَصْحٰبَ الْكَهْفِ وَالرَّقِيْمِ﴾ (Al-Kahf 18:9)
