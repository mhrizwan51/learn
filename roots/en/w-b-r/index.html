Root و ب ر w-b-r

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

و ب رw-b-r

(س) وَبِرَ يَوْبَرُ وَبَرًاwabira yawbaru wa-baran: to have much fur (wool). (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

وَبَرٌwabar pl. اَوْبَارٌawbār (noun, ism dhāt): the soft hair, fur (of camels, hares and the like). Only once in the Quran: ﴿وَمِنْ اَصْوَافِهَا وَاَوْبَارِهَا وَاَشْعَارِهَآ اَثَاثًا وَّمَتَاعًا اِلٰى حِيْنٍ﴾ (An-Naḥl 16:80)
