Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ن و قn-w-q

— نَاقَةnāqah pl. نُوْق وَنِيَاق وَأَيْنُقnūq wa-niyāq wa-ʾaynuq: she-camel. Seven times in the Quran, each time in the account of the she-camel of Thamūd that was sent as a sign: ﴿هٰذِهِۦ نَاقَةُ اللّٰهِ لَكُمْ آيَةً﴾ (Al-Aʿrāf 7:73) and ﴿فَعَقَرُوا النَّاقَةَ﴾ (Al-Aʿrāf 7:77).
