Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ق و عq-w-ʿ

(ن) قَاعَ يَقُوْعُ قَوْعًاqāʿa yaqūʿu qawʿan (original form: قَوَعَ يَقْوُعُqawaʿa yaqwuʿu): (of a male animal) to leap upon the female; some have also mentioned rolling over onto the hind legs. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

قَاعٌqāʿ pl. قِيْعَةٌ وَقِيْعَانٌqīʿah wa-qīʿān (noun, ism dhāt): a level, open and flat plain with no rise or dip. ﴿فَيَذَرُهَا قَاعًا صَفْصَفًا﴾ (Ṭā Hā 20:106); ﴿كَسَرَابٍۢ بِقِيْعَةٍ﴾ (An-Nūr 24:39)
