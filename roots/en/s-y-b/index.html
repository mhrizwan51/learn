Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

س ی بs-y-b

(ض) سَابَ يَسِيْبُ سَيْبًاsāba yasību sayban (original form: سَيَبَsayaba): to flow, to be left loose, to roam unchecked.

سَائِبَةٌsāʾibah (feminine active participle, ism fāʿil): a she-camel that the people of the Jāhiliyyah would set loose on account of some vow, considering it forbidden to ride or milk her. ﴿مَا جَعَلَ اللّٰهُ مِنْۢ بَحِيْرَةٍ وَّلَا سَآئِبَةٍ وَّلَا وَصِيْلَةٍ وَّلَا حَامٍ﴾ (Al-Māʾidah 5:103)Only once in the Quran.
