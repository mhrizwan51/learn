Root ش و ب sh-w-b

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ش و بsh-w-b

(ن) شَابَ يَشُوْبُ شَوْبًاshāba yashūbu shawban (original form: شَوَبَ يَشْوُبُshawaba yashwubu): to mix, to blend.

شَوْبٌshawb: a mixture, that which is mixed in. Only once in the Quran, in the description of the drink of the people of Hell: ﴿ثُمَّ اِنَّ لَهُمْ عَلَيْهَا لَشَوْبًا مِّنْ حَمِيْمٍ﴾ (Aṣ-Ṣāffāt 37:67)
