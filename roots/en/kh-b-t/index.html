Root خ ب ت kh-b-t

Quranic citations in this entry: 3 · اردو

خ ب تkh-b-t

(ض) خَبَتَ يَخْبِتُ خَبْتًاkhabata yakhbitu khabtan: (1) for someone's renown to fade. (2) to be low and soft.

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَخْبَتَ يُخْبِتُ اِخْبَاتًاakhbata yukhbitu ikhbātan: to descend into low, soft ground; to adopt lowliness and humility. ﴿فَتُخْبِتَ لَهُۥ قُلُوْبُهُمْ﴾ (Al-Ḥajj 22:54); ﴿وَاَخْبَتُوْٓا اِلٰى رَبِّهِمْ﴾ (Hūd 11:23)

مُخْبِتٌmukhbit (active participle, ism fāʿil): one who humbles himself. ﴿وَبَشِّرِ الْمُخْبِتِيْنَ﴾ (Al-Ḥajj 22:34)
