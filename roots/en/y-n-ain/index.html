Root ی ن ع y-n-ʿ

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ی ن عy-n-ʿ

(ض، ف) يَنَعَ يَيْنِعُyanaʿa yayniʿu / يَنِعَ يَيْنَعُ يَنْعًا وَيُنُوْعًاyaniʿa yaynaʿu yanʿan wa-yunūʿan: (of fruit) to ripen and become fit to eat.

يَانِعyāniʿ (active participle, ism fāʿil): ripe. Only once in the Quran, in the context of an invitation to reflect: ﴿انْظُرُوْۤا إِلٰى ثَمَرِهِۦۤ إِذَاۤ أَثْمَرَ وَيَنْعِهِۦ﴾ (Al-Anʿām 6:99) — that is, look at its fruiting and its ripening.
