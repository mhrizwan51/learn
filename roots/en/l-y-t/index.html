Root ل ی ت l-y-t

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ل ی تl-y-t

(ض) لَاتَ يَلِيْتُ لَيْتًاlāta yalītu laytan (original form: لَيَتَ يَلْيِتُlayata yalyitu): to diminish someone's due, to reduce it. Only once in the Quran: ﴿لَا يَلِتْكُمْ مِّنْ اَعْمَالِكُمْ شَيْـًٔا﴾ (Al-Ḥujurāt 49:14)

لَيْتَlayta (حرفِ تمنّيḥrfi tmnny): would that; like «اِنَّinna», it puts its noun in the accusative and its predicate in the nominative. ﴿يٰلَيْتَنِيْ كُنْتُ تُرٰبًا﴾ (An-Nabaʾ 78:40)
