Root و د ع w-d-ʿ

Quranic citations in this entry: 4 · اردو

و د عw-d-ʿ

(ف) وَدَعَ يَدَعُ وَدْعًاwadaʿa yadaʿu wa-dʿan (original form: وَدَعَ يَوْدَعُwadaʿa yawdaʿu): (1) to leave something (2) to place something as a deposit; دَعْdaʿ (imperative, amr): leave!; the perfect of this verb is very rarely used, mostly the imperfect and the imperative occur. ﴿وَدَعْ اَذٰىهُمْ وَتَوَكَّلْ عَلَي اللّٰهِ﴾ (Al-Aḥzāb 33:48)

Form II (tafʿīl) وَدَّعَ يُوَدِّعُ تَوْدِيْعًاwaddaʿa yuwaddiʿu tawdīʿan: to bid farewell, to leave, to dissociate oneself from someone. Only once in the Quran: ﴿مَا وَدَّعَكَ رَبُّكَ وَمَا قَلٰى﴾ (Aḍ-Ḍuḥā 93:3)

Form X (istifʿāl) اِسْتَوْدَعَ يَسْتَوْدِعُ اِسْتِيْدَاعًاistawdaʿa yastawdiʿu istīdāʿan: to place something with someone as a deposit; مُسْتَوْدَعٌmustawdaʿ (passive participle (ism mafʿūl) as noun of place (ẓarf)): a place of deposit, repository. ﴿فَمُسْتَقَرٌّ وَّمُسْتَوْدَعٌ﴾ (Al-Anʿām 6:98); ﴿وَيَعْلَمُ مُسْتَقَرَّهَا وَمُسْتَوْدَعَهَا﴾ (Hūd 11:6)
