Root م ع ی m-ʿ-y

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

م ع یm-ʿ-y

— مِعًيmiʿany (noun ending in alif maqṣūrah, ism maqṣūr) pl. أَمْعَاءamʿāʾ: intestine. Only once in the Quran, describing the end of the eating and drinking of the people of Hell: ﴿وَسُقُوْا مَاءً حَمِيْمًا فَقَطَّعَ أَمْعَاءَهُمْ﴾ (Muḥammad 47:15).
