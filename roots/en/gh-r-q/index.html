غ ر قgh-r-q

(س) غَرِقَ يَغْرَقُ غَرَقًاghariqa yaghraqu gharaqan: to sink to the bottom of the water, to drown. ﴿حَتّٰٓى اِذَآ اَدْرَكَهُ الْغَرَقُ﴾ (Yūnus 10:90)

غَرْقٌgharq: to the point of drowning, i.e. (to draw out) with full force and depth. ﴿وَالنّٰزِعٰتِ غَرْقًا﴾ (An-Nāziʿāt 79:1)

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَغْرَقَ يُغْرِقُ اِغْرَاقًاaghraqa yughriqu ighrāqan: to drown someone. ﴿وَاَغْرَقْنَآ اٰلَ فِرْعَوْنَ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:50); مُغْرَقٌmughraq (passive participle, ism mafʿūl): one who is drowned. ﴿اِنَّهُمْ مُّغْرَقُوْنَ﴾ (Hūd 11:37)
