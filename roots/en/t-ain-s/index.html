Root ت ع س t-ʿ-s

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ت ع سt-ʿ-s

(س) تَعِسَ يَتْعَسُ تَعْسًاtaʿisa yatʿasu taʿsan: to fall on one's face, to perish, to fall into ruin — the opposite of intiʿāsh (rising up).

تَعْسtaʿs (verbal noun, maṣdar, in the accusative as a curse): may ruin be upon. Only once in the Quran: ﴿فَتَعْسًا لَّهُمْ وَأَضَلَّ أَعْمَالَهُمْ﴾ (Muḥammad 47:8)
