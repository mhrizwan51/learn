Root ط ل ح ṭ-l-ḥ

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ط ل حṭ-l-ḥ

— طَلْحṭalḥ (generic noun (ism jins), singular (wāḥidah) طَلْحَةṭalḥah): the banana tree (most of the exegetes), or the large shade-giving acacia tree. Manḍūd: arranged layer upon layer. Only once in the Quran, in the description of the people of Paradise: ﴿وَطَلْحٍ مَّنْضُوْدٍ﴾ (Al-Wāqiʿah 56:29).
