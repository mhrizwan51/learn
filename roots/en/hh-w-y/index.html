Root ح و ی ḥ-w-y

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ح و یḥ-w-y

(ض) حَوٰى يَحْوِيْ حَوَايَةًḥawā yaḥwī ḥawāyatan: to gather up, to wind together and collect, to encompass.

حَوَايَاḥawāyā (plural, singular حَاوِيَةḥāwiyah or حَوِيَّةḥawiyyah): intestines — so named because they are coiled. ﴿اَوِ الْحَوَايَآ﴾ (Al-Anʿām 6:146)

اَحْوٰىaḥwā (on the pattern of اَفْعَلafʿal of colours): blackish, dark green. ﴿فَجَعَلَهُۥ غُثَآءً اَحْوٰى﴾ (Al-Aʿlā 87:5)
