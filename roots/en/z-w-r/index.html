Root ز و ر z-w-r

Quranic citations in this entry: 4 · اردو

ز و رz-w-r

(ن) زَارَ يَزُوْرُ زِيَارَةًzāra yazūru ziyāratan: to go to meet someone; to visit. ﴿حَتّٰى زُرْتُمُ الْمَقَابِرَ﴾ (At-Takāthur 102:2)

(س) زَوِرَ يَزْوَرُ زَوَرًاzawira yazwaru zawaran: to have a crooked chest; to be deviated from a straight line.

زُوْرٌzūr: falsehood; what is false (because it deviates from the truth and the right). ﴿وَالَّذِيْنَ لَا يَشْهَدُوْنَ الزُّوْرَ﴾ (Al-Furqān 25:72); ﴿وَاجْتَنِبُوْا قَوْلَ الزُّوْرِ﴾ (Al-Ḥajj 22:30)

Form VI (tafāʿul) تَزَاوَرَ يَتَزَاوَرُ تَزَاوُرًاtazāwara yatazāwaru tazāwuran: (1) to go to visit one another (2) to turn away from something, avoiding it; to keep aloof (in this sense it takes the preposition «عَنْʿan»). ﴿تَّزٰوَرُ عَنْ كَهْفِهِمْ ذَاتَ الْيَمِيْنِ﴾ (Al-Kahf 18:17)
