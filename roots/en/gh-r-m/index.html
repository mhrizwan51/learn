Root غ ر م gh-r-m

Quranic citations in this entry: 4 · اردو

غ ر مgh-r-m

(س) غَرِمَ يَغْرَمُ غُرْمًا وَغَرَامَةًgharima yaghramu ghurman wa-gharāmatan: (1) for something to cling to someone (2) to be caught in a loss without any crime or fault of one's own, to be saddled with an undeserved penalty. (this verb is not used in the Quran).

غَرَامٌgharām (noun, ism dhāt): a thing that clings; an inescapable and lasting punishment. ﴿اِنَّ عَذَابَهَا كَانَ غَرَامًا﴾ (Al-Furqān 25:65)

مَغْرَمٌmaghram (noun of time or place, ẓarf, on the pattern of مَفْعَلٌmafʿal, but used as a noun, ism dhāt): a fine, a penalty. ﴿فَهُمْ مِّنْ مَّغْرَمٍ مُّثْقَلُوْنَ﴾ (Aṭ-Ṭūr 52:40)

غَارِمٌghārim pl. غٰرِمِيْنَghārimīna (active participle, ism fāʿil): one burdened with a fine or debt. ﴿وَالْغٰرِمِيْنَ وَفِيْ سَبِيْلِ اللّٰهِ﴾ (At-Tawbah 9:60)

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَغْرَمَ يُغْرِمُ اِغْرَامًاaghrama yughrimu ighrāman: to impose a fine on someone; مُغْرَمٌmughram (passive participle, ism mafʿūl): one on whom a fine has been imposed. ﴿اِنَّا لَمُغْرَمُوْنَ﴾ (Al-Wāqiʿah 56:66)
