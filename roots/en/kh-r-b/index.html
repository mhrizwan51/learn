Root خ ر ب kh-r-b

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

خ ر بkh-r-b

(س) خَرِبَ يَخْرَبُ خَرَابًاkhariba yakhrabu kharāban: (of a place) to become desolate.

خَرَابٌkharāb (noun, ism dhāt): ruin, desolation. ﴿وَسَعٰى فِيْ خَرَابِهَا﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:114)

Form II (tafʿīl) خَرَّبَ يُخَرِّبُ تَخْرِيْبًاkharraba yukharribu takhrīban: to lay waste; to ruin by wrecking (this verb is not used in the Quran).

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَخْرَبَ يُخْرِبُ اِخْرَابًاakhraba yukhribu ikhrāban: to lay waste, to ruin. ﴿يُخْرِبُوْنَ بُيُوْتَهُمْ بِاَيْدِيْهِمْ﴾ (Al-Ḥashr 59:2)
