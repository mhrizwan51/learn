Root و ف ر w-f-r

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

و ف رw-f-r

(ض) وَفَرَ يَفِرُ وَفْرًاwafara yafiru wa-fran (original form: وَفَرَ يَوْفِرُwafara yawfiru): to increase (something), to make full; (intransitive) to be abundant. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

مَوْفُوْرٌmawfūr (passive participle, ism mafʿūl): made full, ample. Only once in the Quran: ﴿فَاِنَّ جَهَنَّمَ جَزَآؤُكُمْ جَزَآءً مَّوْفُوْرًا﴾ (Al-Isrāʾ 17:63)
