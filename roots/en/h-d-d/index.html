Root ھ د د h-d-d

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ھ د دh-d-d

(ن، ض) هَدَّ يَهُدُّ وَيَهِدُّ هَدًّاhadda yahuddu wa-yahiddu haddan (original form: هَدَدَhadada): to bring something down with a loud crash, to demolish; (intransitive) to fall with a crash. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

هَدٌّhadd (verbal noun, maṣdar): falling with a crash, crumbling to pieces. Only once in the Quran: ﴿تَكَادُ السَّمٰوٰتُ يَتَفَطَّرْنَ مِنْهُ وَتَنْشَقُّ الْاَرْضُ وَتَخِرُّ الْجِبَالُ هَدًّا﴾ (Maryam 19:90)
