ع م ھʿ-m-h

(س) عَمِهَ يَعْمَهُ عَمَهًاʿamiha yaʿmahu ʿamahan: to wander for lack of insight, to roam about bewildered and confused. The lexicographers say that عَمَهʿamah is to the heart (insight) what عَمًيʿamany is to the eye. ﴿وَيَمُدُّهُمْ فِيْ طُغْيَانِهِمْ يَعْمَهُوْنَ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:15)
