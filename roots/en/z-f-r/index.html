Root ز ف ر z-f-r

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ز ف رz-f-r

(ض) زَفَرَ يَزْفِرُ زَفْرًا وَزَفِيْرًاzafara yazfiru zafran wa-zafīran: to breathe out forcefully while weeping; to sigh; to cry out.

زَفِيْرٌzafīr (verbal noun (maṣdar), used as a noun): a sigh; a cry; the forceful exhaling of breath. ﴿لَهُمْ فِيْهَا زَفِيْرٌ وَّشَهِيْقٌ﴾ (Hūd 11:106)
