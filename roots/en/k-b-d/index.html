Root ک ب د k-b-d

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ک ب دk-b-d

(ک) كَبُدَ يَكْبُدُ كَبَادَةًkabuda yakbudu kabādatan: to fall into hardship.

كَبِدkabid pl. أَكْبَادakbād: the liver; and كَبَدkabad: hardship, toil — as it were, a pain that reaches the liver. كَبِدkabid is also the middle of a thing (كَبِدُ السَّمَاءِkabidu as-samāʾ). Only once in the Quran: ﴿لَقَدْ خَلَقْنَا الْإِنْسَانَ فِيْ كَبَدٍ﴾ (Al-Balad 90:4) — that is, man was created in toil.
