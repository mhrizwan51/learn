Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ط ب قṭ-b-q

(س) طَبِقَ يَطْبَقُ طَبَقًاṭabiqa yaṭbaqu ṭabaqan: of the storeys of a building, to lie one above another.

طَبَقٌṭabaq: stage, storey, state. ﴿لَتَرْكَبُنَّ طَبَقًا عَنْ طَبَقٍ﴾ (Al-Inshiqāq 84:19)

Form III (mufāʿalah) طَابَقَ يُطَابِقُ طِبَاقًاṭābaqa yuṭābiqu ṭibāqan and مُطَابَقَةًmuṭābaqatan: of things, to lie one upon another, to correspond to one another. ﴿الَّذِيْ خَلَقَ سَبْعَ سَمٰوٰتٍ طِبَاقًا﴾ (Al-Mulk 67:3)
