Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

م ت یm-t-y

— مَتٰىmatā: (1) an interrogative noun of time (ism istifhām): when? (2) a conditional noun (ism sharṭ): whenever. It occurs nine times in the Quran, each time as a question: ﴿مَتٰى نَصْرُ اللّٰهِ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:214); ﴿وَيَقُوْلُوْنَ مَتٰى هٰذَا الْوَعْدُ اِنْ كُنْتُمْ صٰدِقِيْنَ﴾ (Yūnus 10:48)
