Root ز ر ی z-r-y

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ز ر یz-r-y

(ض) زَرٰى يَزْرِيْ زَرْيًاzarā yazrī zaryan: to find fault with; to hold in contempt.

Form VIII (iftiʿāl) اِزْدَرٰى يَزْدَرِيْ اِزْدِرَاءًizdarā yazdarī izdirāʾan (originally اِزْتَرٰىiztarā, the tāʾ changed to dāl): to regard someone as fallen in one's eyes and despicable. Only once in the Quran, in the words of Nūḥ (peace be upon him): ﴿وَلَآ اَقُوْلُ لِلَّذِيْنَ تَزْدَرِيْٓ اَعْيُنُكُمْ لَنْ يُّؤْتِيَهُمُ اللّٰهُ خَيْرًا﴾ (Hūd 11:31)
