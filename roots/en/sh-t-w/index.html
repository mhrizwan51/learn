Root ش ت و sh-t-w

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ش ت وsh-t-w

— شِتَاءٌshitāʾ pl. اَشْتِيَةٌashtiyah (noun, ism dhāt): winter, the cold season; the opposite of صَيْفṣayf (summer). It occurs in the account of the two trading journeys of Quraysh. ﴿رِحْلَةَ الشِّتَآءِ وَالصَّيْفِ﴾ (Quraysh 106:2)Only once in the Quran.
