Root ح ط ط ḥ-ṭ-ṭ

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ح ط طḥ-ṭ-ṭ

(ن) حَطَّ يَحُطُّ حَطًّاḥaṭṭa yaḥuṭṭu ḥaṭṭan: to descend; to bring something down from above (to set down a burden).

حِطَّةٌḥiṭṭah: the setting down of a burden (of sins), a plea for forgiveness. ﴿وَقُوْلُوْا حِطَّةٌ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:58)
