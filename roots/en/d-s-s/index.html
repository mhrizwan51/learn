Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

د س سd-s-s

(ن) دَسَّ يَدُسُّ دَسًّاdassa yadussu dassan: to hide something beneath something, to thrust something into something. ﴿اَمْ يَدُسُّهُۥ فِي التُّرَابِ﴾ (An-Naḥl 16:59)

Form II (tafʿīl) دَسّٰى يُدَسِّيْ تَدْسِيَةًdassā yudassī tadsiyatan (original form دَسَّسَdassasa): to bury, to hide away. ﴿وَقَدْ خَابَ مَنْ دَسّٰىهَا﴾ (Ash-Shams 91:10)
