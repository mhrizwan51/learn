Root ا ف ل ʾ-f-l

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ا ف لʾ-f-l

(ن) أَفَلَ يَأْفُلُ أُفُوْلًاafala yaʾfulu ufūlan: to set, to disappear, to vanish from sight — especially of stars, the moon and the sun. The rhetorical importance of this root is that Abraham, peace be upon him, made this very word the basis of his denial of godhood: whatever sets cannot be a Lord. ﴿فَلَمَّآ اَفَلَ قَالَ لَآ اُحِبُّ الْاٰفِلِيْنَ﴾ (Al-Anʿām 6:76)

آفِلāfil pl. آفِلِيْنāfilīn (active participle (ism fāʿil)): that which sets. ﴿لَآ اُحِبُّ الْاٰفِلِيْنَ﴾ (Al-Anʿām 6:76). Four times in the Quran: three times as a verb and once as an active participle.
