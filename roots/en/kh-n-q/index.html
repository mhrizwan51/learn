Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

خ ن قkh-n-q

(ن) خَنَقَ يَخْنُقُ خَنْقًاkhanaqa yakhnuqu khanqan: to strangle.

Form VII (infiʿāl) اِنْخَنَقَ يَنْخَنِقُ اِنْخِنَاقًاinkhanaqa yankhaniqu inkhināqan: to be strangled; مُنْخَنِقٌmunkhaniq (active participle, ism fāʿil): strangled, one that dies of strangulation. ﴿وَالْمُنْخَنِقَةُ وَالْمَوْقُوْذَةُ﴾ (Al-Māʾidah 5:3)
