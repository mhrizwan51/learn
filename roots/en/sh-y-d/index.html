Root ش ی د sh-y-d

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ش ی دsh-y-d

(ض) شَادَ يَشِيْدُ شَيْدًاshāda yashīdu shaydan: to raise a building high, to strengthen it with plaster, to adorn it with decoration.

مَشِيْدٌmashīd (passive participle (ism mafʿūl), original form مَشْيُوْدٌmashyūd): strengthened, adorned. ﴿وَبِئْرٍ مُّعَطَّلَةٍ وَّقَصْرٍ مَّشِيْدٍ﴾ (Al-Ḥajj 22:45)

Form II (tafʿīl) شَيَّدَ يُشَيِّدُ تَشْيِيْدًاshayyada yushayyidu tashyīdan: to strengthen or adorn abundantly; مُشَيَّدٌmushayyad (passive participle, ism mafʿūl): strongly fortified. ﴿وَلَوْ كُنْتُمْ فِيْ بُرُوْجٍ مُّشَيَّدَةٍ﴾ (An-Nisāʾ 4:78)
