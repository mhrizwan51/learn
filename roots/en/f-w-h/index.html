Root ف و ھ f-w-h

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ف و ھf-w-h

(ن) فَاهَ يَفُوْهُ فَوْهًاfāha yafūhu fawhan (original form: فَوَهَ يَفْوُهُfawaha yafwuhu): to speak, to utter. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

فُوْهٌfūh pl. اَفْوَاهٌafwāh (noun, ism dhāt): mouth; when it is an annexed noun (muḍāf) it becomes فُوْfū in the nominative, fā in the accusative and فِيْfī in the genitive (one of the five nouns, al-asmāʾ al-khamsah). ﴿لِيَبْلُغَ فَاهُ﴾ (Ar-Raʿd 13:14); ﴿يَقُوْلُوْنَ بِاَفْوَاهِهِمْ مَّا لَيْسَ فِيْ قُلُوْبِهِمْ﴾ (Āl ʿImrān 3:167)
