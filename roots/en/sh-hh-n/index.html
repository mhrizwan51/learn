Root ش ح ن sh-ḥ-n

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ش ح نsh-ḥ-n

(ف) شَحَنَ يَشْحَنُ شَحْنًاshaḥana yashḥanu shaḥnan: to fill, to load to the brim; from this root also comes شَحْنَاءُshaḥnāʾu (rancour, enmity), as though the breast were filled with hostility.

مَشْحُوْنٌmashḥūn (passive participle, ism mafʿūl): filled, laden. Three times in the Quran, each time in the description of the ship: ﴿فِي الْفُلْكِ الْمَشْحُوْنِ﴾ (Ash-Shuʿarāʾ 26:119, Yā Sīn 36:41); ﴿اِلَي الْفُلْكِ الْمَشْحُوْنِ﴾ (Aṣ-Ṣāffāt 37:140)
