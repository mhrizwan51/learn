Root ج س م j-s-m

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ج س مj-s-m

(ک) جَسُمَ يَجْسُمُ جَسَامَةًjasuma yajsumu jasāmatan: to be of large and heavy body.

جِسْمjism pl. أَجْسَامajsām: body, the tangible frame. Twice in the Quran, both times in the context of stature: ﴿وَزَادَهُۥ بَسْطَةً فِي الْعِلْمِ وَالْجِسْمِ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:247); ﴿وَإِذَا رَأَيْتَهُمْ تُعْجِبُكَ أَجْسَامُهُمْ﴾ (Al-Munāfiqūn 63:4)

جَسِيْمjasīm: stout, well-built.

Form II (tafʿīl) جَسَّمَ يُجَسِّمُ تَجْسِيْمًاjassama yujassimu tajsīman: to give a body to; to give bodily form to (this verb is not used in the Quran).
