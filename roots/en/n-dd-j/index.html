ن ض جn-ḍ-j

(س) نَضِجَ يَنْضَجُ نُضْجًاnaḍija yanḍaju nuḍjan: (of fruit or meat) to be cooked through, to ripen. Only once in the Quran, in the account of the punishment of the people of Hell: ﴿كُلَّمَا نَضِجَتْ جُلُوْدُهُمْ بَدَّلْنٰهُمْ جُلُوْدًا غَيْرَهَا﴾ (An-Nisāʾ 4:56)
