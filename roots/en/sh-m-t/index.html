Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ش م تsh-m-t

(س) شَمِتَ يَشْمَتُ شَمَاتَةًshamita yashmatu shamātatan: to rejoice at someone's misfortune.

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَشْمَتَ يُشْمِتُ اِشْمَاتًاashmata yushmitu ishmātan: to gladden others by hurting someone, to gladden the enemy. ﴿فَلَا تُشْمِتْ بِيَ الْاَعْدَآءَ﴾ (Al-Aʿrāf 7:150)
