ب ط رb-ṭ-r

(س) بَطِرَ يَبْطَرُ بَطَرًاbaṭira yabṭaru baṭaran: (1) to be led astray by abundant blessings, to grow insolent. (2) to be ungrateful for a blessing, to fail to value it. ﴿بَطِرَتْ مَعِيْشَتَهَا﴾ (Al-Qaṣaṣ 28:58)

بَطَرٌbaṭar (verbal noun, maṣdar): insolence, exultant pride. ﴿خَرَجُوْا مِنْ دِيَارِهِمْ بَطَرًا وَّرِئَآءَ النَّاسِ﴾ (Al-Anfāl 8:47)
