Root ش ک ک sh-k-k

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ش ک کsh-k-k

(ن) شَكَّ يَشُكُّ شَكًّاshakka yashukku shakkan (original form: شَكَكَ يَشْكُكُshakaka yashkuku): to doubt, to waver between two things; the original sense is of piercing and running through with a spear, and doubt was given this name because it pierces certainty.

شَكٌّshakk pl. شُكُوْكٌshukūk (noun, ism dhāt): doubt, wavering. 15 times in the Quran, every time as a noun (ism dhāt). ﴿وَاِنَّهُمْ لَفِيْ شَكٍّ مِّنْهُ مُرِيْبٍ﴾ (Hūd 11:110)
