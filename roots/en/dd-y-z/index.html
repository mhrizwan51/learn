Root ض ی ز ḍ-y-z

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ض ی زḍ-y-z

(ض) ضَازَ يَضِيْزُ ضَيْزًاḍāza yaḍīzu ḍayzan (original form: ضَيَزَḍayaza): to wrong, to curtail someone's due, to divide unjustly.

ضِيْزَيٰḍīzā (pattern فُعْلَيٰfuʿlā, adjective): crooked, unjust — the morphological point is that although its pattern is فُعْلَيٰfuʿlā, the ḍād was given a kasrah so that the yāʾ remains intact, and the grammarians called this a peculiarity of the word. Only once in the Quran: ﴿تِلْكَ إِذًا قِسْمَةٌ ضِيْزٰى﴾ (An-Najm 53:22).
