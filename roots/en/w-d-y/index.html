Root و د ی w-d-y

Quranic citations in this entry: 3 · اردو

و د یw-d-y

(ض) وَدٰى يَدِيْ وَدْيًاwadā yadī wa-dyan (original form: وَدَيَ يَوْدِيُwadaya yawdiyu): (1) of a thing, to flow (2) to pay blood-money. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

دِيَةٌdiyah (اصلاً وِدْيَةٌaṣlan widyah, noun, ism dhāt): blood-money. ﴿فَدِيَةٌ مُّسَلَّمَةٌ اِلٰٓى اَهْلِهِۦ﴾ (An-Nisāʾ 4:92)

وَادٍwād pl. اَوْدِيَةٌawdiyah (اصلاً وَادِيٌaṣlan wa-diy, active participle (ism fāʿil) used as a noun (ism dhāt)): the low ground between two hills or mountains where rainwater flows, valley. ﴿اِنَّكَ بِالْوَادِ الْمُقَدَّسِ طُوًي﴾ (Ṭā Hā 20:12); ﴿فَسَالَتْ اَوْدِيَةٌۢ بِقَدَرِهَا﴾ (Ar-Raʿd 13:17)
