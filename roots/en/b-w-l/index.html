Root ب و ل b-w-l

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ب و لb-w-l

(ن) بَالَ يَبُوْلُ بَوْلًاbāla yabūlu bawlan: to expel what is inside, like spitting out phlegm, clearing the nose, etc.

بَالٌbāl: (1) what is hidden in the heart, the real state; the condition of the heart. ﴿وَاَصْلَحَ بَالَهُمْ﴾ (Muḥammad 47:2)(2) outward condition, state. ﴿قَالَ فَمَا بَالُ الْقُرُوْنِ الْاُوْلٰى﴾ (Ṭā Hā 20:51)
