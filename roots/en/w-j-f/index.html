Root و ج ف w-j-f

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

و ج فw-j-f

(ض) وَجَفَ يَجِفُ وَجْفًا وَوَجِيْفًاwajafa yajifu wa-jfan wa-wajīfan (original form: وَجَفَ يَوْجِفُwajafa yawjifu): to move fast; (of the heart) to throb, to tremble. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

وَاجِفَةٌwājifah (active participle (ism fāʿil), feminine): throbbing, agitated. Only once in the Quran: ﴿قُلُوْبٌ يَّوْمَئِذٍ وَّاجِفَةٌ﴾ (An-Nāziʿāt 79:8)

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَوْجَفَ يُوْجِفُ اِيْجَافًاawjafa yūjifu ījāfan: to make a horse or camel gallop. Only once in the Quran: ﴿فَمَآ اَوْجَفْتُمْ عَلَيْهِ مِنْ خَيْلٍ وَّلَا رِكَابٍ﴾ (Al-Ḥashr 59:6)
