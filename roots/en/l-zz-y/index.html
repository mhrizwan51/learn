Root ل ظ ی l-ẓ-y

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ل ظ یl-ẓ-y

— لَظيٰlaẓyā: pure blazing fire, flame — one of the names of Hell, and a diptote (ghayr munṣarif) (because it is a proper noun and feminine). ﴿كَلَّا إِنَّهَا لَظٰى﴾ (Al-Maʿārij 70:15)

Form V (tafaʿʿul) تَلَظّٰى يَتَلَظّٰى تَلَظِّيًاtalaẓẓā yatalaẓẓā talaẓẓiyan: to blaze fiercely, to throw out flames; the original form was تَتَلَظّٰىtatalaẓẓā, and one tāʾ for ease of pronunciation was elided. Only once in the Quran: ﴿فَأَنْذَرْتُكُمْ نَارًا تَلَظّٰى﴾ (Al-Layl 92:14).
