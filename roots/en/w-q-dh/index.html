Root و ق ذ w-q-dh

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

و ق ذw-q-dh

(ض) وَقَذَ يَقِذُ وَقْذًاwaqadha yaqidhu wa-qdhan (original form: وَقَذَ يَوْقِذُwaqadha yawqidhu): to deal a fatal blow (with a stick or a stone). (this verb is not used in the Quran).

مَوْقُوْذَةٌmawqūdhah (passive participle (ism mafʿūl), feminine): an animal killed by a blow (of a stick or a stone). Only once in the Quran, in the enumeration of forbidden animals: ﴿وَالْمُنْخَنِقَةُ وَالْمَوْقُوْذَةُ﴾ (Al-Māʾidah 5:3)
