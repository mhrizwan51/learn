Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ھ ش مh-sh-m

(ض) هَشَمَ يَهْشِمُ هَشْمًاhashama yahshimu hashman: to break a (dry) thing, to crumble it to bits. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

هَشِيْمٌhashīm (on the pattern of فَعِيْلٌfaʿīl, in the sense of a passive participle, ism mafʿūl): a broken thing, crumbled dry grass, chaff. ﴿فَاَصْبَحَ هَشِيْمًا تَذْرُوْهُ الرِّيٰحُ﴾ (Al-Kahf 18:45); ﴿فَكَانُوْا كَهَشِيْمِ الْمُحْتَظِرِ﴾ (Al-Qamar 54:31)
