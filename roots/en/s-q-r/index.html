Quranic citations in this entry: 3 · اردو

س ق رs-q-r

(ن) سَقَرَ يَسْقُرُ سَقْرًاsaqara yasquru saqran: (of the sun) to scorch something and change its colour. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

سَقَرُsaqar (proper noun, ʿalam; diptote, ghayr munṣarif): a name of Hell. Four times in the Quran: ﴿سَاُصْلِيْهِ سَقَرَ﴾ (Al-Muddaththir 74:26); ﴿وَمَآ اَدْرٰىكَ مَا سَقَرُ﴾ (Al-Muddaththir 74:27); ﴿ذُوْقُوْا مَسَّ سَقَرَ﴾ (Al-Qamar 54:48)
