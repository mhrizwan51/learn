Root و ت ن w-t-n

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

و ت نw-t-n

— وَتِيْنwatīn: the great artery of the heart through which blood flows into the body (the aorta); the lexicographers say that severing it necessarily brings death, and that is what is meant in this verse. Only once in the Quran: ﴿ثُمَّ لَقَطَعْنَا مِنْهُ الْوَتِيْنَ﴾ (Al-Ḥāqqah 69:46).
