س ب حs-b-ḥ

(ف) سَبَحَ يَسْبَحُ سَبْحًا وَسِبَاحَةًsabaḥa yasbaḥu sabḥan wa-sibāḥatan: to swim; to hold one's lofty position, as a swimmer keeps himself on the surface of the water; also to work actively and strive with absorption. ﴿كُلٌّ فِيْ فَلَكٍ يَّسْبَحُوْنَ﴾ (Yā Sīn 36:40); ﴿اِنَّ لَكَ فِي النَّهَارِ سَبْحًا طَوِيْلًا﴾ (Al-Muzzammil 73:7)

سٰبِحٰتٌsābiḥāt (active participle (ism fāʿil), feminine plural): those that glide along swimming. ﴿وَالسّٰبِحٰتِ سَبْحًا﴾ (An-Nāziʿāt 79:3)

Form II (tafʿīl) سَبَّحَ يُسَبِّحُ تَسْبِيْحًاsabbaḥa yusabbiḥu tasbīḥan: to maintain someone in his lofty position, i.e. to declare him free of every deficiency and every evil. ﴿سَبِّحِ اسْمَ رَبِّكَ الْاَعْلٰى﴾ (Al-Aʿlā 87:1)

سُبْحٰنَsubḥāna (verbal noun (maṣdar), as a cognate accusative (mafʿūl muṭlaq)): glorification, i.e. (God) is free of every deficiency. ﴿سُبْحٰنَ الَّذِيْٓ اَسْرٰى بِعَبْدِهِۦ﴾ (Al-Isrāʾ 17:1)

سُبُّوْحٌsubbūḥ (intensive form (ṣīghat al-mubālaghah) on the pattern of فُعُّوْلٌfuʿʿūl): most holy; sanctified — a name of God, free of every evil and full of blessing.
