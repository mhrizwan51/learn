Root ص ی ف ṣ-y-f

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ص ی فṣ-y-f

— صَيْفṣayf: summer, the hot season; the opposite of شِتَاءshitāʾ (winter). Only once in the Quran, and that together with شِتَاءshitāʾ: ﴿رِحْلَةَ الشِّتَاءِ وَالصَّيْفِ﴾ (Quraysh 106:2).
