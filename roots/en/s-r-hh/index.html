Root س ر ح s-r-ḥ

Quranic citations in this entry: 3 · اردو

س ر حs-r-ḥ

(ف) سَرَحَ يَسْرَحُ سَرْحًاsaraḥa yasraḥu sarḥan: to turn camels loose to graze on the سَرْحsarḥ tree (a thornless tree); to take cattle out to pasture in the morning. ﴿وَحِيْنَ تَسْرَحُوْنَ﴾ (An-Naḥl 16:6)

Form II (tafʿīl) سَرَّحَ يُسَرِّحُ تَسْرِيْحًاsarraḥa yusarriḥu tasrīḥan: to release completely; to set free. ﴿اَوْ تَسْرِيْحٌۢ بِاِحْسَانٍ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:229)

سَرَاحٌsarāḥ (verbal noun, ism maṣdar): releasing; setting free. ﴿وَسَرِّحُوْهُنَّ سَرَاحًا جَمِيْلًا﴾ (Al-Aḥzāb 33:49)
