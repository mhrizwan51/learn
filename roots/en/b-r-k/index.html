Root ب ر ک b-r-k

Quranic citations in this entry: 4 · اردو

ب ر کb-r-k

(ن) بَرَكَ يَبْرُكُ بُرُوْكًاbaraka yabruku burūkan: (of a camel) to kneel down, to settle firmly; hence to possess abiding, intangible good and blessing.

بَرَكَةٌbarakah pl. بَرَكَاتbarakāt (noun, ism dhāt): blessing. ﴿لَفَتَحْنَا عَلَيْهِمْ بَرَكٰتٍ مِّنَ السَّمَآءِ وَالْاَرْضِ﴾ (Al-Aʿrāf 7:96)

Form III (mufāʿalah) بَارَكَ يُبَارِكُ مُبَارَكَةًbāraka yubāriku mubārakatan: to bless someone. ﴿الَّذِيْ بٰرَكْنَا حَوْلَهُۥ﴾ (Al-Isrāʾ 17:1)

مُبَارَكٌmubārak (passive participle, ism mafʿūl): blessed. ﴿كِتٰبٌ اَنْزَلْنٰهُ اِلَيْكَ مُبٰرَكٌ﴾ (Ṣād 38:29)

Form V (tafaʿʿul) تَبَرَّكَ يَتَبَرَّكُ تَبَرُّكًاtabarraka yatabarraku tabarrukan: to seek blessing from something (this verb is not used in the Quran).

Form VI (tafāʿul) تَبَارَكَtabāraka: to be blessed; this form (ṣīghah) is reserved for God alone. ﴿تَبٰرَكَ الَّذِيْ بِيَدِهِ الْمُلْكُ﴾ (Al-Mulk 67:1)
