Root ن ف ش n-f-sh

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ن ف شn-f-sh

(ن) نَفَشَ يَنْفُشُ نَفْشًاnafasha yanfushu nafshan: (1) to card cotton or wool, to tease it apart (2) (of livestock) to graze at night without a shepherd (in someone's field). Only once in the Quran: ﴿اِذْ نَفَشَتْ فِيْهِ غَنَمُ الْقَوْمِ﴾ (Al-Anbiyāʾ 21:78)

مَنْفُوْشٌmanfūsh (passive participle, ism mafʿūl): carded, teased apart. Only once in the Quran: ﴿وَتَكُوْنُ الْجِبَالُ كَالْعِهْنِ الْمَنْفُوْشِ﴾ (Al-Qāriʿah 101:5)
