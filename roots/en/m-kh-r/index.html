Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

م خ رm-kh-r

(ف) مَخَرَ يَمْخَرُ مَخْرًاmakhara yamkharu makhran: (of a ship) to move cleaving the water with a sound. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

مَاخِرَةٌmākhirah pl. مَوَاخِرُmawākhiru (active participle, ism fāʿil): (a ship) cleaving the water. ﴿وَتَرَي الْفُلْكَ مَوَاخِرَ فِيْهِ﴾ (An-Naḥl 16:14); ﴿وَتَرَي الْفُلْكَ فِيْهِ مَوَاخِرَ﴾ (Fāṭir 35:12)
