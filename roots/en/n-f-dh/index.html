Root ن ف ذ n-f-dh

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ن ف ذn-f-dh

(ن) نَفَذَ يَنْفُذُ نُفُوْذًا وَنَفَاذًاnafadha yanfudhu nufūdhan wa-nafādhan: to pass through, to penetrate right through, to pass beyond a boundary; hence the term «nufūdh». In only one verse of the Quran, in which this root occurs three times: ﴿يٰمَعْشَرَ الْجِنِّ وَالْاِنْسِ اِنِ اسْتَطَعْتُمْ اَنْ تَنْفُذُوْا مِنْ اَقْطَارِ السَّمٰوٰتِ وَالْاَرْضِ فَانْفُذُوْا لَا تَنْفُذُوْنَ اِلَّا بِسُلْطٰنٍ﴾ (Ar-Raḥmān 55:33)

Form II (tafʿīl) نَفَّذَ يُنَفِّذُ تَنْفِيْذًاnaffadha yunaffidhu tanfīdhan: to execute (an order etc.); to put into effect (this verb is not used in the Quran).
