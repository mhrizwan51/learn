Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

م ل قm-l-q

(ف) مَلَقَ يَمْلَقُ مَلْقًاmalaqa yamlaqu malqan: to rub (something) smooth and polished, to efface. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَمْلَقَ يُمْلِقُ اِمْلَاقًاamlaqa yumliqu imlāqan: to become destitute (as though the hand, emptied of wealth, had become smooth). ﴿وَلَا تَقْتُلُوْٓا اَوْلَادَكُمْ مِّنْ اِمْلَاقٍ﴾ (Al-Anʿām 6:151); ﴿وَلَا تَقْتُلُوْٓا اَوْلَادَكُمْ خَشْيَةَ اِمْلَاقٍ﴾ (Al-Isrāʾ 17:31)
