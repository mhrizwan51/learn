Root غ د ر gh-d-r

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

غ د رgh-d-r

(ض) غَدَرَ يَغْدِرُ غَدْرًاghadara yaghdiru ghadran: to break a pledge, to be faithless; the basic sense is to leave something behind. (this verb is not used in the Quran).

Form III (mufāʿalah) غَادَرَ يُغَادِرُ مُغَادَرَةًghādara yughādiru mughādaratan: to leave something out, to let it remain. ﴿لَا يُغَادِرُ صَغِيْرَةً وَّلَا كَبِيْرَةً اِلَّآ اَحْصٰىهَا﴾ (Al-Kahf 18:49); ﴿وَحَشَرْنٰهُمْ فَلَمْ نُغَادِرْ مِنْهُمْ اَحَدًا﴾ (Al-Kahf 18:47)
