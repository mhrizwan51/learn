Root ق ی ل q-y-l

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ق ی لq-y-l

(ض) قَالَ يَقِيْلُ قَيْلًا وَقَيْلُوْلَةًqāla yaqīlu qaylan wa-qaylūlatan (original form: قَيَلَ يَقْيِلُqayala yaqyilu): to rest at midday, whether or not one sleeps. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

قَائِلٌqāʾil pl. قَآئِلُوْنَqāʾilūna (active participle, ism fāʿil): one who rests at midday. ﴿اَوْ هُمْ قَآئِلُوْنَ﴾ (Al-Aʿrāf 7:4)

مَقِيْلٌmaqīl (noun of time or place, ẓarf): a place of midday rest. ﴿وَاَحْسَنُ مَقِيْلًا﴾ (Al-Furqān 25:24)
