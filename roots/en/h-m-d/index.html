Root ھ م د h-m-d

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ھ م دh-m-d

(ن) هَمَدَ يَهْمُدُ هُمُوْدًاhamada yahmudu humūdan: (of fire) to go out, to grow cold; (of land) to be bare of vegetation. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

هَامِدَةٌhāmidah (adjective on the pattern of فَاعِلَةٌfāʿilah): extinguished, i.e. cold; (of land) without vegetation, barren. Only once in the Quran: ﴿وَتَرَي الْاَرْضَ هَامِدَةً فَاِذَآ اَنْزَلْنَا عَلَيْهَا الْمَآءَ اهْتَزَّتْ﴾ (Al-Ḥajj 22:5)
