ق ل دq-l-d

(ض) قَلَدَ يَقْلِدُ قَلْدًاqalada yaqlidu qaldan: (1) to twist a rope (2) to hang something (a sword, a necklace) around the neck. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

قِلَادَةٌqilādah pl. قَلَآئِدُqalāʾidu (noun, ism dhāt): a thing put around the neck, a collar, a necklace; here the sacrificial animals are meant, around whose necks collars were hung as a mark. ﴿وَلَا الْهَدْيَ وَلَا الْقَلَآئِدَ﴾ (Al-Māʾidah 5:2)

مِقْلَادٌmiqlād pl. مَقَالِيْدُmaqālīdu (noun of instrument, ism ālah): a key. ﴿لَهُۥ مَقَالِيْدُ السَّمٰوٰتِ وَالْاَرْضِ﴾ (Az-Zumar 39:63)

Form II (tafʿīl) قَلَّدَ يُقَلِّدُ تَقْلِيْدًاqallada yuqallidu taqlīdan: (1) to put a necklace or collar round the neck (2) to follow someone unquestioningly (this verb is not used in the Quran).
