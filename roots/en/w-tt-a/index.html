Root و ط ء w-ṭ-ʾ

Quranic citations in this entry: 4 · اردو

و ط ءw-ṭ-ʾ

(س) وَطِئَ يَطَاُ وَطْاًwaṭiʾa yaṭaʾu wa-ṭan (original form: وَطِئَ يَوْطَاُwaṭiʾa yawṭaʾu): to tread something underfoot, to trample; to set foot (in a place). ﴿وَاَرْضًا لَّمْ تَطَـُٔوْهَا﴾ (Al-Aḥzāb 33:27); ﴿وَلَا يَطَـُٔوْنَ مَوْطِئًا يَّغِيْظُ الْكُفَّارَ﴾ (At-Tawbah 9:120)

وَطْاٌwaṭʾ (verbal noun, maṣdar): treading; trampling (the self), i.e. hardship. ﴿اِنَّ نَاشِئَةَ الَّيْلِ هِيَ اَشَدُّ وَطْاً﴾ (Al-Muzzammil 73:6)

مَوْطِئٌmawṭiʾ (noun of time or place, ẓarf): a place of treading or of setting foot. (In the above-mentioned verse, al-Tawbah 9:120.) Form III (mufāʿalah) وَاطَاَ يُوَاطِئُ مُوَاطَاَةًwāṭaʾa yuwāṭiʾu muwāṭaʾatan: to make (something) agree (with another), to make equal (as the metre of verses). Only once in the Quran: ﴿لِّيُوَاطِـُٔوْا عِدَّةَ مَا حَرَّمَ اللّٰهُ﴾ (At-Tawbah 9:37)
