Root ذ خ ر dh-kh-r

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ذ خ رdh-kh-r

(ف) ذَخَرَ يَذْخَرُ ذُخْرًاdhakhara yadhkharu dhukhran: to store up for a time of need.

Form VIII (iftiʿāl) اِذْتَخَرَ يَذْتَخِرُ اِذْتِخَارًاidhtakhara yadhtakhiru idhtikhāran (original) ⟵ اِدَّخَرَ يَدَّخِرُ اِدِّخَارًاiddakhara yaddakhiru iddikhāran (form in use): to gather carefully, to hoard. ﴿وَمَا تَدَّخِرُوْنَ فِيْ بُيُوْتِكُمْ﴾ (Āl ʿImrān 3:49)
