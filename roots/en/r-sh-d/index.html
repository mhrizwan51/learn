ر ش دr-sh-d

(ن، س) رَشَدَ يَرْشُدُrashada yarshudu / رَشِدَ يَرْشَدُ رُشْدًا وَرَشَدًاrashida yarshadu rushdan wa-rashadan: to walk the right, i.e. righteous, path; to be rightly guided, to find guidance. ﴿لَعَلَّهُمْ يَرْشُدُوْنَ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:186)

رُشْدٌrushd: (1) the right path, guidance. ﴿قَدْ تَّبَيَّنَ الرُّشْدُ مِنَ الْغَيِّ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:256); (2) sound judgement, discernment (which leads to guidance). ﴿فَاِنْ اٰنَسْتُمْ مِّنْهُمْ رُشْدًا﴾ (An-Nisāʾ 4:6)

رَشَدٌrashad: the good path, the right path. ﴿وَّهَيِّئْ لَنَا مِنْ اَمْرِنَا رَشَدًا﴾ (Al-Kahf 18:10)

رَشَادٌrashād (noun, ism dhāt): righteousness, good, guidance. ﴿اَهْدِكُمْ سَبِيْلَ الرَّشَادِ﴾ (Ghāfir 40:38)

رَاشِدٌrāshid pl. رٰشِدُوْنَrāshidūna (active participle, ism fāʿil): one who is rightly guided. ﴿اُولٰٓئِكَ هُمُ الرّٰشِدُوْنَ﴾ (Al-Ḥujurāt 49:7)

رَشِيْدٌrashīd (adjective on the pattern of فَعِيْلٌfaʿīl): rightly guided, of upright conduct. ﴿اَلَيْسَ مِنْكُمْ رَجُلٌ رَّشِيْدٌ﴾ (Hūd 11:78)

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَرْشَدَ يُرْشِدُ اِرْشَادًاarshada yurshidu irshādan: to show the right path, to guide.

مُرْشِدٌmurshid (active participle, ism fāʿil): one who shows the straight path, a guide. ﴿فَلَنْ تَجِدَ لَهُۥ وَلِيًّا مُّرْشِدًا﴾ (Al-Kahf 18:17)
