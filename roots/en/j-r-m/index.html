Root ج ر م j-r-m

Quranic citations in this entry: 4 · اردو

ج ر مj-r-m

(ض) جَرَمَ يَجْرِمُ جَرْمًاjarama yajrimu jarman: to incite someone to an evil deed; then in a general sense it is also used for inciting; also to earn. ﴿وَلَا يَجْرِمَنَّكُمْ شَنَاٰنُ قَوْمٍ عَلٰٓى اَلَّا تَعْدِلُوْا﴾ (Al-Māʾidah 5:8)

جَرَمٌjaram: clear, certain.

لَا جَرَمَlā jarama contains a detached lā (lā munfaṣilah): just as لَآ اُقْسِمُlā uqsimu does not mean "I do not swear", but rather the lā stands apart and اُقْسِمُuqsimu stands apart — i.e. "No! I do swear". Likewise the lā in لَا جَرَمَlā jarama is detached; rather than entangle the ordinary reader in this subtlety, it is generally translated "there is no doubt". ﴿لَا جَرَمَ اَنَّ لَهُمُ النَّارَ وَاَنَّهُمْ مُّفْرَطُوْنَ﴾ (An-Naḥl 16:62); ﴿لَا جَرَمَ اَنَّهُمْ فِي الْاٰخِرَةِ هُمُ الْاَخْسَرُوْنَ﴾ (Hūd 11:22)

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَجْرَمَ يُجْرِمُ اِجْرَامًاajrama yujrimu ijrāman: to do evil, to commit a crime. ﴿اِنَّا مِنَ الْمُجْرِمِيْنَ مُنْتَقِمُوْنَ﴾ (As-Sajdah 32:22)
