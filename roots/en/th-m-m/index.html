ث م مth-m-m

(ن) ثَمَّ يَثُمُّ ثَمًّاthamma yathummu thamman: to set a thing right.

ثُمَّthumma: then, thereupon, after that; a conjunctive particle that serves to keep the order of the discourse right. ﴿ثُمَّ اسْتَوٰٓى اِلَي السَّمَآءِ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:29)

ثَمَّthamma: used as a demonstrative of distance: there, in that very place. ﴿فَاَيْنَمَا تُوَلُّوْا فَثَمَّ وَجْهُ اللّٰهِ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:115)
