ج ذ وj-dh-w

— جَذْوَةjadhwah pl. جِذًيjidhany: a burning ember, a piece of wood with fire at its end. Only once in the Quran, in the account of Mūsā (peace be upon him): ﴿أَوْ جَذْوَةٍ مِّنَ النَّارِ لَعَلَّكُمْ تَصْطَلُوْنَ﴾ (Al-Qaṣaṣ 28:29)
