Root و ک د w-k-d

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

و ک دw-k-d

(ض) وَكَدَ يَكِدُ وُكُوْدًاwakada yakidu wukūdan (original form: وَكَدَ يَوْكِدُwakada yawkidu): to remain settled in a place; to persist (in a task). (this verb is not used in the Quran).

Form II (tafʿīl) وَكَّدَ يُوَكِّدُ تَوْكِيْدًاwakkada yuwakkidu tawkīdan: to make firm, to strengthen (a covenant or a knot). Only once in the Quran: ﴿وَلَا تَنْقُضُوا الْاَيْمَانَ بَعْدَ تَوْكِيْدِهَا﴾ (An-Naḥl 16:91)
