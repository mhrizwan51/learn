Root ح و ز ḥ-w-z

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ح و زḥ-w-z

(ن) حَازَ يَحُوْزُ حَوْزًاḥāza yaḥūzu ḥawzan: to gather, to collect.

Form V (tafaʿʿul) تَحَيَّزَ يَتَحَيَّزُ تَحَيُّزًاtaḥayyaza yataḥayyazu taḥayyuzan (اصلًا تَحَوُّزaṣlan taḥawwuz): of a snake, to coil up; to join up with a supporter.

مُتَحَيِّزٌmutaḥayyiz (active participle, ism fāʿil): one who joins up with. ﴿اَوْ مُتَحَيِّزًا اِلٰى فِئَةٍ﴾ (Al-Anfāl 8:16)
