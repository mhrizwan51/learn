Root ع ی ر ʿ-y-r

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ع ی رʿ-y-r

(ض) عَارَ يَعِيْرُ عَيْرًاʿāra yaʿīru ʿayran: to go to and fro. (this verb is not used in the Quran).

عِيْرٌʿīr (collective noun, ism jamʿ): caravan, especially a caravan of camels bringing grain and goods. ﴿اَيَّتُهَا الْعِيْرُ اِنَّكُمْ لَسٰرِقُوْنَ﴾ (Yūsuf 12:70); ﴿وَلَمَّا فَصَلَتِ الْعِيْرُ﴾ (Yūsuf 12:94)
