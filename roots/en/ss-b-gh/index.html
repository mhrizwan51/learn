Root ص ب غ ṣ-b-gh

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ص ب غṣ-b-gh

(ف) صَبَغَ يَصْبَغُ صَبْغًاṣabagha yaṣbaghu ṣabghan: to dye, to colour; to baptise.

صِبْغَةٌṣibghah: dye, colour; figuratively the religion and way that takes hold of a man like a dye. ﴿صِبْغَةَ اللّٰهِ وَمَنْ اَحْسَنُ مِنَ اللّٰهِ صِبْغَةً﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:138)

صِبْغٌṣibgh: vinegar or a sauce (in which bread is dipped). ﴿تَنْۢبُتُ بِالدُّهْنِ وَصِبْغٍ لِّلْاٰكِلِيْنَ﴾ (Al-Muʾminūn 23:20)
