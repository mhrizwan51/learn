Root و ق ر w-q-r

Quranic citations in this entry: 5 · اردو

و ق رw-q-r

(س) وَقِرَ يَوْقَرُ وَقْرًاwaqira yawqaru wa-qran: (of the ear) to be heavy, to be deaf. (this verb is not used in the Quran).

وَقْرٌwaqr (verbal noun (maṣdar), also a noun): heaviness (of the ear), deafness. ﴿وَفِيْٓ اٰذَانِنَا وَقْرٌ﴾ (Fuṣṣilat 41:5); ﴿كَاَنَّ فِيْٓ اُذُنَيْهِ وَقْرًا﴾ (Luqmān 31:7)

وِقْرٌwiqr (noun, ism dhāt): load, burden. Only once in the Quran: ﴿فَالْحٰمِلٰتِ وِقْرًا﴾ (Adh-Dhāriyāt 51:2)

(ک) وَقُرَ يَوْقُرُ وَقَارًاwaqura yawquru wa-qāran: to be grave and sober, to be dignified. (this verb is not used in the Quran).

وَقَارٌwaqār (verbal noun (maṣdar), also a noun): gravity, sobriety, majesty. Only once in the Quran: ﴿مَا لَكُمْ لَا تَرْجُوْنَ لِلّٰهِ وَقَارًا﴾ (Nūḥ 71:13)

Form II (tafʿīl) وَقَّرَ يُوَقِّرُ تَوْقِيْرًاwaqqara yuwaqqiru tawqīran: to honour and revere someone. Only once in the Quran: ﴿وَتُعَزِّرُوْهُ وَتُوَقِّرُوْهُ﴾ (Al-Fatḥ 48:9)
