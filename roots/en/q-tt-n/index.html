Root ق ط ن q-ṭ-n

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ق ط نq-ṭ-n

(ن) قَطَنَ يَقْطُنُ قُطُوْنًاqaṭana yaqṭunu quṭūnan: to dwell in a place, to settle there. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

يَقْطِيْنٌyaqṭīn (on the pattern of يَفْعِيْلٌyafʿīl): any creeping plant without a stem that spreads over the ground, such as the gourd and the cucumber; the exegetes say a gourd vine is meant here. Only once in the Quran: ﴿وَاَنْۢبَتْنَا عَلَيْهِ شَجَرَةً مِّنْ يَّقْطِيْنٍ﴾ (Aṣ-Ṣāffāt 37:146)
