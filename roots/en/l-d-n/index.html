Root ل د ن l-d-n

Quranic citations in this entry: 3 · اردو

ل د نl-d-n

(ک) لَدُنَ يَلْدُنُ لَدَانَةًladuna yaldunu ladānatan: to be soft and pliant (this verb is not used in the Quran).

لَدُنْladun (indeclinable adverb, ẓarf mabnī): (1) the time at which something begins (2) with someone, from his own presence in particular; "مِنْ لَّدُنْmin lladun" usually gives this second meaning. ﴿وَعَلَّمْنٰهُ مِنْ لَّدُنَّا عِلْمًا﴾ (Al-Kahf 18:65); ﴿وَهَبْ لَنَا مِنْ لَّدُنْكَ رَحْمَةً﴾ (Āl ʿImrān 3:8)

لَدٰىladā (noun of time or place, ẓarf): with, near. ﴿وَّاَلْفَيَا سَيِّدَهَا لَدَا الْبَابِ﴾ (Yūsuf 12:25)
