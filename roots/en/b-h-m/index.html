Root ب ھ م b-h-m

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ب ھ مb-h-m

— No bare triliteral verb is in use.

بَهِيْمَةٌbahīmah: an animal whose utterance is indistinct, a beast, quadruped. ﴿اُحِلَّتْ لَكُمْ بَهِيْمَةُ الْاَنْعَامِ﴾ (Al-Māʾidah 5:1)

Form IV (ifʿāl) أَبْهَمَ يُبْهِمُ إِبْهَامًاabhama yubhimu ibhāman: to make a statement unclear; to speak vaguely and evasively (this verb is not used in the Quran).
