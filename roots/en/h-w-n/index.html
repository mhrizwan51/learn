Root ھ و ن h-w-n

Quranic citations in this entry: 7 · اردو

ھ و نh-w-n

(ن) هَانَ يَهُوْنُ هَوْنًا وَهُوْنًاhāna yahūnu hawnan wa-hūnan (original form: هَوَنَ يَهْوُنُhawana yahwunu): (1) to be easy and light (2) to be lowly, abased. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

هَوْنٌhawn (verbal noun, maṣdar): gentleness, calmness, dignity. ﴿وَعِبَادُ الرَّحْمٰنِ الَّذِيْنَ يَمْشُوْنَ عَلَي الْاَرْضِ هَوْنًا﴾ (Al-Furqān 25:63)

هُوْنٌhūn (verbal noun, maṣdar, also a noun, ism dhāt): abasement, disgrace. ﴿فَالْيَوْمَ تُجْزَوْنَ عَذَابَ الْهُوْنِ﴾ (Al-Aḥqāf 46:20)

هَيِّنٌhayyin (adjective on the pattern of فَيْعِلٌfayʿil): easy, light. ﴿قَالَ رَبُّكَ هُوَ عَلَيَّ هَيِّنٌ﴾ (Maryam 19:9)

اَهْوَنُahwanu (elative, ism tafḍīl): easier. Only once in the Quran: ﴿وَهُوَ اَهْوَنُ عَلَيْهِ﴾ (Ar-Rūm 30:27)

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَهَانَ يُهِيْنُ اِهَانَةًahāna yuhīnu ihānatan: to abase, to humiliate; مُهِيْنٌmuhīn (active participle, ism fāʿil): one who abases, humiliating; مُهَانٌmuhān (passive participle, ism mafʿūl): abased, humiliated. ﴿فَيَقُوْلُ رَبِّيْٓ اَهَانَنِ﴾ (Al-Fajr 89:16); ﴿وَلِلْكٰفِرِيْنَ عَذَابٌ مُّهِيْنٌ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:90); ﴿وَيَخْلُدْ فِيْهِۦ مُهَانًا﴾ (Al-Furqān 25:69) — Note: «مُهِيْنmuhīn» (h-w-n) and «مَهِيْنmahīn» (m-h-n) are from different roots.
