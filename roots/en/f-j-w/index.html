Root ف ج و f-j-w

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ف ج وf-j-w

(ن) فَجَا يَفْجُوْ فَجْوًاfajā yafjū fajwan (original form: فَجَوَ يَفْجُوُfajawa yafjuwu): to open a door. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

فَجْوَةٌfajwah (noun (ism dhāt)): a wide space between two things, a courtyard. ﴿وَهُمْ فِيْ فَجْوَةٍ مِّنْهُ﴾ (Al-Kahf 18:17). Only once in the Quran.
