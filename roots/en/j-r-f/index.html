Root ج ر ف j-r-f

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ج ر فj-r-f

(ن) جَرَفَ يَجْرُفُ جَرْفًاjarafa yajrufu jarfan: to dig up earth, to scrape away earth.

جُرُفٌjuruf: a riverbank whose earth the water has washed away, an overhanging ledge (cliff). ﴿عَلٰى شَفَا جُرُفٍ هَارٍ﴾ (At-Tawbah 9:109)
