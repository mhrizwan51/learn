Root س و غ s-w-gh

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

س و غs-w-gh

(ن) سَاغَ يَسُوْغُ سَوْغًاsāgha yasūghu sawghan: to go down the throat easily, to be agreeable.

سَائِغٌsāʾigh (adjective on the pattern of فَاعِلٌfāʿil): that which goes down agreeably, i.e. agreeable, palatable. ﴿لَبَنًا خَالِصًا سَآئِغًا لِّلشّٰرِبِيْنَ﴾ (An-Naḥl 16:66)

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَسَاغَ يُسِيْغُ اِسَاغَةًasāgha yusīghu isāghatan: to make go down the throat easily, to let swallow. ﴿يَّتَجَرَّعُهُۥ وَلَا يَكَادُ يُسِيْغُهُۥ﴾ (Ibrāhīm 14:17)
