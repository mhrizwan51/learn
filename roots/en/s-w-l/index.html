Root س و ل s-w-l

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

س و لs-w-l

(ف) سَوَالًاsawālan: to ask, to question.

Form II (tafʿīl) سَوَّلَ يُسَوِّلُ تَسْوِيْلًاsawwala yusawwilu taswīlan: to present something foul in an attractive guise, to make seem fair. ﴿بَلْ سَوَّلَتْ لَكُمْ اَنْفُسُكُمْ اَمْرًا﴾ (Yūsuf 12:18); ﴿اَلشَّيْطٰنُ سَوَّلَ لَهُمْ﴾ (Muḥammad 47:25)
