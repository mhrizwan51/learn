Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ع د نʿ-d-n

(ض، ن) عَدَنَ يَعْدِنُ وَيَعْدُنُ عَدْنًاʿadana yaʿdinu wa-yaʿdunu ʿadnan: to make a place one's home; to settle permanently (this verb is not used in the Quran).

عَدْنٌʿadn: an everlasting abode; the name of a place in Paradise (also the name of a city in Yemen, a use not found in the Quran). ﴿جَنّٰتِ عَدْنٍ الَّتِيْ وَعَدَ الرَّحْمٰنُ عِبَادَهُۥ بِالْغَيْبِ﴾ (Maryam 19:61)
