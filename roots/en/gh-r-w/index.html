Root غ ر و gh-r-w

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

غ ر وgh-r-w

(س) غَرِيَ يَغْرٰى غَرًاghariya yaghrā gharan: to cling, to become attached. (this verb is not used in the Quran).

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَغْرٰى يُغْرِيْ اِغْرَاءًaghrā yughrī ighrāʾan: (1) to make something cling, to make it binding (2) to set someone upon another, to incite. ﴿فَاَغْرَيْنَا بَيْنَهُمُ الْعَدَاوَةَ وَالْبَغْضَآءَ﴾ (Al-Māʾidah 5:14); ﴿لَنُغْرِيَنَّكَ بِهِمْ﴾ (Al-Aḥzāb 33:60)
