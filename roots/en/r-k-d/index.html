Root ر ک د r-k-d

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ر ک دr-k-d

(ن) رَكَدَ يَرْكُدُ رُكُوْدًاrakada yarkudu rukūdan: to come to a stop, to become still, to cease moving.

رَاكِدٌrākid pl. رَوَاكِدُrawākidu (active participle, ism fāʿil): still, motionless. Only once in the Quran, describing the wind falling still: ﴿اِنْ يَّشَاْ يُسْكِنِ الرِّيْحَ فَيَظْلَلْنَ رَوَاكِدَ عَلٰى ظَهْرِهِۦ﴾ (Ash-Shūrā 42:33)
