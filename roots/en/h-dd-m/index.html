Root ھ ض م h-ḍ-m

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ھ ض مh-ḍ-m

(ض) هَضَمَ يَهْضِمُ هَضْمًاhaḍama yahḍimu haḍman: to crush something soft by pressing it; from this basic sense (1) to diminish someone's right (2) to be soft and tender. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

هَضْمٌhaḍm (verbal noun, maṣdar, also a noun, ism dhāt): curtailment of one's right, injustice. Only once in the Quran: ﴿فَلَا يَخٰفُ ظُلْمًا وَّلَا هَضْمًا﴾ (Ṭā Hā 20:112)

هَضِيْمٌhaḍīm (adjective on the pattern of فَعِيْلٌfaʿīl): soft, tender; (of a cluster of fruit) layered and bowed under its weight. Only once in the Quran: ﴿وَّزُرُوْعٍ وَّنَخْلٍ طَلْعُهَا هَضِيْمٌ﴾ (Ash-Shuʿarāʾ 26:148)
