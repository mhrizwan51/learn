Root ھ ر ب h-r-b

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ھ ر بh-r-b

(ن) هَرَبَ يَهْرُبُ هَرَبًا وَهُرُوْبًاharaba yahrubu haraban wa-hurūban: to run away, to flee.

هَرَبharab (verbal noun, maṣdar, in the sense of a noun, ism dhāt): a place of fleeing, or flight. Only once in the Quran, spoken by the jinn: ﴿وَأَنَّا ظَنَنَّاۤ أَنْ لَّنْ نُّعْجِزَ اللّٰهَ فِي الْأَرْضِ وَلَنْ نُّعْجِزَهُۥ هَرَبًا﴾ (Al-Jinn 72:12) — i.e. even by fleeing they cannot escape Him.
