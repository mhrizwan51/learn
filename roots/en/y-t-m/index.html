Root ی ت م y-t-m

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ی ت مy-t-m

(ض، س) يَتَمَ يَيْتِمُyatama yaytimu / يَتِمَ يَيْتَمُ يُتْمًاyatima yaytamu yutman: to be left without a father. (this verb is not used in the Quran).

يَتِيْمyatīm pl. يَتَامَيٰ وَأَيْتَامyatāmā wa-ʾaytām: orphan — the lexicographers state that among humans the orphan is one who has lost his father, and among animals one that has lost its mother; the basic sense, moreover, is solitariness and singularity (دُرَّة يَتِيْمَةdurrah yatīmah = a peerless pearl). A morphological point: يَتَامَيٰyatāmā is on the same pattern (فَعَالَيٰfaʿālā) as أَيَامَيٰayāmā and مَرْضَيٰmarḍā. Examples: ﴿فَأَمَّا الْيَتِيْمَ فَلَا تَقْهَرْ﴾ (Aḍ-Ḍuḥā 93:9) and ﴿وَآتُوا الْيَتَامٰۤي أَمْوَالَهُمْ﴾ (An-Nisāʾ 4:2).
