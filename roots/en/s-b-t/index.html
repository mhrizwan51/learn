Quranic citations in this entry: 3 · اردو

س ب تs-b-t

(ض) سَبَتَ يَسْبِتُ سَبْتًاsabata yasbitu sabtan: to cut a thing off; to cease from work, to rest; also to observe the Sabbath (Saturday). ﴿وَيَوْمَ لَا يَسْبِتُوْنَ ۙ لَا تَاْتِيْهِمْ﴾ (Al-Aʿrāf 7:163)

سَبْتٌsabt: Saturday, the Sabbath (the Jews' day of rest and worship). ﴿اِذْ يَعْدُوْنَ فِي السَّبْتِ﴾ (Al-Aʿrāf 7:163)

سُبَاتٌsubāt: rest; repose (in which work is suspended). ﴿وَجَعَلْنَا نَوْمَكُمْ سُبَاتًا﴾ (An-Nabaʾ 78:9)
