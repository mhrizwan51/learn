Root ن ف و n-f-w

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ن ف وn-f-w

— this root is synonymous with «n f y» (to remove someone from a place, to banish); the Quranic form ﴿اَوْ يُنْفَوْا مِنَ الْاَرْضِ﴾ (Al-Māʾidah 5:33) is from the yāʾ root, so see: n f y.
