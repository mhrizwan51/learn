Root ک م ھ k-m-h

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ک م ھk-m-h

(س) كَمِهَ يَكْمَهُ كَمَهًاkamiha yakmahu kamahan: to be blind from birth (this verb is not used in the Quran).

اَكْمَهُakmahu (an adjective on the pattern of اَفْعَلُafʿalu, used for colours and defects): one born blind. Twice in the Quran, both times among the miracles of Jesus (peace be upon him): ﴿وَاُبْرِئُ الْاَكْمَهَ وَالْاَبْرَصَ﴾ (Āl ʿImrān 3:49); ﴿وَتُبْرِئُ الْاَكْمَهَ وَالْاَبْرَصَ﴾ (Al-Māʾidah 5:110)
