ر ھ قr-h-q

(س) رَهِقَ يَرْهَقُ رَهَقًاrahiqa yarhaqu rahaqan: to overpower someone by force; (1) to use force, to oppress (2) to come upon someone, to cover, to overspread. ﴿وَلَا يَرْهَقُ وُجُوْهَهُمْ قَتَرٌ وَّلَا ذِلَّةٌ﴾ (Yūnus 10:26)

رَهَقٌrahaq (noun, ism dhāt): oppression, insolence, domineering, bullying. ﴿فَزَادُوْهُمْ رَهَقًا﴾ (Al-Jinn 72:6)

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَرْهَقَ يُرْهِقُ اِرْهَاقًاarhaqa yurhiqu irhāqan: to force something upon someone, to cover with, to burden with. ﴿سَاُرْهِقُهُۥ صَعُوْدًا﴾ (Al-Muddaththir 74:17)
