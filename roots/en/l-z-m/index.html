Root ل ز م l-z-m

Quranic citations in this entry: 4 · اردو

ل ز مl-z-m

(س) لَزِمَ يَلْزَمُ لُزُوْمًاlazima yalzamu luzūman: to cling to someone, to become inseparable, not to part (this verb is not used in the Quran).

لِزَامٌlizām (verbal noun (maṣdar) of form mufāʿalah, in the sense of an adjective): clinging, inseparable (punishment). ﴿فَسَوْفَ يَكُوْنُ لِزَامًا﴾ (Al-Furqān 25:77)

Form III (mufāʿalah) لَازَمَ يُلَازِمُ مُلَازَمَةًlāzama yulāzimu mulāzamatan: to stay close to someone; to accompany constantly; to adhere to a task persistently (this verb is not used in the Quran).

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَلْزَمَ يُلْزِمُ اِلْزَامًاal-zama yulzimu ilzāman: to make someone cling to something, to make binding upon. ﴿اَنُلْزِمُكُمُوْهَا وَاَنْتُمْ لَهَا كٰرِهُوْنَ﴾ (Hūd 11:28); ﴿وَكُلَّ اِنْسَانٍ اَلْزَمْنٰهُ طٰٓئِرَهُۥ فِيْ عُنُقِهِۦ﴾ (Al-Isrāʾ 17:13); ﴿وَاَلْزَمَهُمْ كَلِمَةَ التَّقْوٰى﴾ (Al-Fatḥ 48:26)
