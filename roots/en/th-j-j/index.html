Root ث ج ج th-j-j

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ث ج جth-j-j

(ن) ثَجَجَ يَثْجُجُthajaja yathjuju (original form) → ثَجَّ يَثُجُّ ثَجًّاthajja yathujju thajjan: to pour out or flow forcefully, to fall in torrents.

ثَجَّاجthajjāj (pattern فَعَّالfaʿʿāl, intensive form, ṣīghat al-mubālaghah): flowing with great force, torrential. Only once in the Quran: ﴿وَأَنْزَلْنَا مِنَ الْمُعْصِرَاتِ مَاءً ثَجَّاجًا﴾ (An-Nabaʾ 78:14)
