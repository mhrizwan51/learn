Root ا م ل ʾ-m-l

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ا م لʾ-m-l

(ن) اَمَلَ يَاْمُلُ اَمَلًاamala yāmulu amalan: to hope.

اَمَلٌamal (noun, ism dhāt): hope. ﴿وَيُلْهِهِمُ الْاَمَلُ﴾ (Al-Ḥijr 15:3); ﴿وَخَيْرٌ اَمَلًا﴾ (Al-Kahf 18:46)

Form V (tafaʿʿul) تَأَمَّلَ يَتَأَمَّلُ تَأَمُّلًاtaʾammala yataʾammalu taʾammulan: to look at something attentively; to reflect; to ponder (this verb is not used in the Quran).
