Root م ز ج m-z-j

Quranic citations in this entry: 3 · اردو

م ز جm-z-j

(ض) مَزَجَ يَمْزِجُ مَزْجًاmazaja yamziju mazjan: to mix, to blend.

مِزَاجmizāj: that which is mixed in, an admixture. Three times in the Quran, each time in describing the drinks of Paradise: ﴿وَمِزَاجُهُۥ مِنْ تَسْنِيْمٍ﴾ (Al-Muṭaffifīn 83:27), ﴿كَانَ مِزَاجُهَا كَافُوْرًا﴾ (Al-Insān 76:5), ﴿كَانَ مِزَاجُهَا زَنْجَبِيْلًا﴾ (Al-Insān 76:17).

Form VIII (iftiʿāl) اِمْتَزَجَ يَمْتَزِجُ اِمْتِزَاجًاimtazaja yamtaziju imtizājan: to mix with one another; to blend together (this verb is not used in the Quran).
