Root ک ف ت k-f-t

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ک ف تk-f-t

(ض) كَفَتَ يَكْفِتُ كَفْتًاkafata yakfitu kaftan: to gather up, to take in within oneself.

كِفَاتkifāt: that which gathers up; a vessel or place that takes things into itself. Only once in the Quran, in the description of the earth: ﴿أَلَمْ نَجْعَلِ الْأَرْضَ كِفَاتًا أَحْيَاءً وَّأَمْوَاتًا﴾ (Al-Mursalāt 77:25–26) — that is, the earth holds within it both the living and the dead.
