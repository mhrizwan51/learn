Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ل د دl-d-d

(س) لَدَّ يَلَدُّ لَدَدًاladda yaladdu ladadan (original form: لَدِدَ يَلْدَدُladida yaldadu): to be fiercely quarrelsome, to dispute obstinately (this verb is not used in the Quran).

اَلَدُّal-add pl. لُدٌّludd (adjective on the pattern of اَفْعَلُafʿalu): extremely quarrelsome. ﴿وَهُوَ اَلَدُّ الْخِصَامِ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:204); ﴿وَتُنْذِرَ بِهِۦ قَوْمًا لُّدًّا﴾ (Maryam 19:97)
