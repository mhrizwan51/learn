Root ی د ی y-d-y

Quranic citations in this entry: 4 · اردو

ی د یy-d-y

— No verb from the bare triliteral (thulāthī mujarrad) occurs in the Quran.

يَدٌyad (اصلاً يَدْيٌaṣlan yady, defective root (nāqiṣ) with yāʾ, the final radical elided), dual يَدَانِyadāni pl. اَيْدٍayd (اصلاً اَيْدِيٌaṣlan aydiy): hand; figuratively: power, authority, favour; "بِاَيْدِيْهِمْbiʾaydīhim" = with their own hands; "بَيْنَ يَدَيْهِbayna yadayhi" = before him, prior to it. ﴿تَبَّتْ يَدَآ اَبِيْ لَهَبٍ وَّتَبَّ﴾ (Al-Masad 111:1); ﴿بَلْ يَدٰهُ مَبْسُوْطَتٰنِ﴾ (Al-Māʾidah 5:64); ﴿يَدُ اللّٰهِ فَوْقَ اَيْدِيْهِمْ﴾ (Al-Fatḥ 48:10); ﴿مُصَدِّقًا لِّمَا بَيْنَ يَدَيْهِ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:97)
