Root ح ط م ḥ-ṭ-m

Quranic citations in this entry: 3 · اردو

ح ط مḥ-ṭ-m

(ض) حَطَمَ يَحْطِمُ حَطْمًاḥaṭama yaḥṭimu ḥaṭman: to break into fragments, to smash to pieces. ﴿لَا يَحْطِمَنَّكُمْ سُلَيْمٰنُ وَجُنُوْدُهُۥ﴾ (An-Naml 27:18). This root occurs 6 times in the Quran.

حُطَامḥuṭām: chaff, something that crumbles into fragments — a most telling word for worldly wealth. ﴿ثُمَّ يَكُوْنُ حُطٰمًا﴾ (Al-Ḥadīd 57:20)

حُطَمَةḥuṭamah (pattern فُعَلَةfuʿalah, intensive form): that which smashes utterly — one of the names of Hell. ﴿لَيُنْۢبَذَنَّ فِي الْحُطَمَةِ﴾ (Al-Humazah 104:4)
