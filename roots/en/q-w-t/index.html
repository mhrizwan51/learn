ق و تq-w-t

(ن) قَاتَ يَقُوْتُ قَوْتًاqāta yaqūtu qawtan (original form: قَوَتَ يَقْوُتُqawata yaqwutu): to feed, to provide sustenance. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

قُوْتٌqūt pl. اَقْوَاتٌaqwāt (noun, ism dhāt): food, sustenance. ﴿وَقَدَّرَ فِيْهَآ اَقْوَاتَهَا﴾ (Fuṣṣilat 41:10)

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَقَاتَ يُقِيْتُ اِقَاتَةًaqāta yuqītu iqātatan: to convey sustenance to someone; then, to have power over a thing and watch over it.

مُقِيْتٌmuqīt (active participle, ism fāʿil): one who has power, a guardian. Only once in the Quran: ﴿وَكَانَ اللّٰهُ عَلٰى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ مُّقِيْتًا﴾ (An-Nisāʾ 4:85)
