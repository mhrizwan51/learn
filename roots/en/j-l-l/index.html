Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ج ل لj-l-l

(ض) جَلَلَ يَجْلِلُjalala yajlilu (original form) ⟵ جَلَّ يَجِلُّ جَلَالًا وَجَلَالَةًjalla yajillu jalālan wa-jalālatan: to be great and exalted in rank.

جَلَالjalāl: majesty and grandeur — applied to Allah, the word denotes His awe and greatness, and together with "الْإِكْرَامal-ʾikrām" it encompasses both beauty and majesty. Twice in the Quran, both times in the same surah: ﴿ذُو الْجَلٰلِ وَالْإِكْرَامِ﴾ (Ar-Raḥmān 55:27, 78)
