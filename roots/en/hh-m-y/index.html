Root ح م ی ḥ-m-y

Quranic citations in this entry: 4 · اردو

ح م یḥ-m-y

(ض) حَمٰى يَحْمِيْ حِمَايَةًḥamā yaḥmī ḥimāyatan: to keep something from someone, to protect, to defend.

حَامٍḥām (active participle, ism fāʿil): protector; technically it is the name for a camel that was declared sacred and set free. ﴿وَلَا وَصِيْلَةٍ وَّلَا حَامٍ﴾ (Al-Māʾidah 5:103)

(س) حَمِيَ يَحْمٰى حَمْيًا وَحُمُوًّاḥamiya yaḥmā ḥamyan wa-ḥumuwwan: for something to become very hot; it becomes transitive with the preposition عَلٰىʿalā: to heat something thoroughly, to make red-hot. ﴿يَّوْمَ يُحْمٰى عَلَيْهَا فِيْ نَارِ جَهَنَّمَ﴾ (At-Tawbah 9:35)

حَامِيَةٌḥāmiyah (active participle, ism fāʿil): that which is hot (f.). ﴿تَصْلٰى نَارًا حَامِيَةً﴾ (Al-Ghāshiyah 88:4)

حَمِيَّةٌḥamiyyah (noun, ism dhāt): the boiling heat of passion, zeal, arrogance. ﴿اِذْ جَعَلَ الَّذِيْنَ كَفَرُوْا فِيْ قُلُوْبِهِمُ الْحَمِيَّةَ حَمِيَّةَ الْجَاهِلِيَّةِ﴾ (Al-Fatḥ 48:26)
