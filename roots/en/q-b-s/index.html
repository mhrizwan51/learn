Root ق ب س q-b-s

Quranic citations in this entry: 3 · اردو

ق ب سq-b-s

(ض) قَبَسَ يَقْبِسُ قَبْسًاqabasa yaqbisu qabsan: to take fire from a fire, to kindle. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

قَبَسٌqabas (noun, ism dhāt): fire taken from a fire, a burning brand. ﴿لَّعَلِّيْٓ اٰتِيْكُمْ مِّنْهَا بِقَبَسٍ﴾ (Ṭā Hā 20:10); ﴿اَوْ اٰتِيْكُمْ بِشِهَابٍ قَبَسٍ﴾ (An-Naml 27:7)

Form VIII (iftiʿāl) اِقْتَبَسَ يَقْتَبِسُ اِقْتِبَاسًاiqtabasa yaqtabisu iqtibāsan: to obtain a part or portion of fire or of some other thing. ﴿انْظُرُوْنَا نَقْتَبِسْ مِنْ نُّوْرِكُمْ﴾ (Al-Ḥadīd 57:13)
