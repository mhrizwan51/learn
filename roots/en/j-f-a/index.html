Root ج ف ا j-f-ʾ

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ج ف اj-f-ʾ

(ف) جَفَأَ يَجْفَأُ جَفْأًjafaʾa yajfaʾu jafʾan: for a pot to boil over its brim; for something to go to waste, to become useless.

جُفَاءٌjufāʾ (adjective on the pattern fuʿāl): useless, wasted. ﴿فَاَمَّا الزَّبَدُ فَيَذْهَبُ جُفَآءً﴾ (Ar-Raʿd 13:17)
