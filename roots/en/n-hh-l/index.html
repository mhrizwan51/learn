Root ن ح ل n-ḥ-l

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ن ح لn-ḥ-l

(ف) نَحَلَ يَنْحَلُ نَحْلًاnaḥala yanḥalu naḥlan: to give someone something willingly (without recompense). (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

نِحْلَةٌniḥlah (noun, ism dhāt): (1) a gift, a present (2) willingness of heart. Only once in the Quran: ﴿وَاٰتُوا النِّسَآءَ صَدُقٰتِهِنَّ نِحْلَةً﴾ (An-Nisāʾ 4:4)

نَحْلٌnaḥl (generic noun, ism jins): the honey bee; the surah takes its name from it. ﴿وَاَوْحٰى رَبُّكَ اِلَي النَّحْلِ﴾ (An-Naḥl 16:68)
