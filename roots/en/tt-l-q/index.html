Root ط ل ق ṭ-l-q

Quranic citations in this entry: 4 · اردو

ط ل قṭ-l-q

(ض) طَلْقًاṭalqan: to undo someone's bond.

طَلَاقٌṭalāq (verbal noun, ism maṣdar): dissolving the bond of marriage. ﴿اَلطَّلَاقُ مَرَّتٰنِ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:229)

Form II (tafʿīl) طَلَّقَ يُطَلِّقُ تَطْلِيْقًاṭallaqa yuṭalliqu taṭlīqan: to set someone free and separate; to divorce.

طَلِّقْṭalliq (imperative, amr): divorce!

مُطَلَّقَةٌmuṭallaqah (passive participle, ism mafʿūl): a divorced woman. ﴿يٰٓاَيُّهَا النَّبِيُّ اِذَا طَلَّقْتُمُ النِّسَآءَ﴾ (Aṭ-Ṭalāq 65:1); ﴿وَالْمُطَلَّقٰتُ يَتَرَبَّصْنَ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:228)

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَطْلَقَ يُطْلِقُ اِطْلَاقًاaṭlaqa yuṭliqu iṭlāqan: to unfasten and set free; to release from restraint; (with ʿalā) to apply a name or ruling to something (this verb is not used in the Quran).

Form VII (infiʿāl) اِنْطَلَقَ يَنْطَلِقُ اِنْطِلَاقًاinṭalaqa yanṭaliqu inṭilāqan: to be free of a bond; to set off.

اِنْطَلِقْinṭaliq (imperative, amr): go! ﴿فَانْطَلَقَا حَتّٰٓى اِذَا لَقِيَا غُلٰمًا﴾ (Al-Kahf 18:74)
