Root ث ل ل th-l-l

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ث ل لth-l-l

— ثُلَّةthullah (noun, ism dhāt): a large company, a throng; the lexicographers say the original sense is that of a heap and a band. Three times in the Quran, each time in Sūrat al-Wāqiʿah in the account of the division of the people of Paradise: ﴿ثُلَّةٌ مِّنَ الْأَوَّلِيْنَ ۝ وَقَلِيْلٌ مِّنَ الْآخِرِيْنَ﴾ (Al-Wāqiʿah 56:13–14); ﴿ثُلَّةٌ مِّنَ الْأَوَّلِيْنَ ۝ وَثُلَّةٌ مِّنَ الْآخِرِيْنَ﴾ (Al-Wāqiʿah 56:39–40)
