ب ق لb-q-l

— بَقْلbaql (generic noun (ism jins), singular (wāḥidah) بَقْلَةbaqlah): herbs and greens that grow from the earth and have no stem. Only once in the Quran: ﴿مِمَّا تُنْبِتُ الْأَرْضُ مِنْ بَقْلِهَا﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:61)
