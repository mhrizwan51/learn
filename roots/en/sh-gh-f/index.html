Root ش غ ف sh-gh-f

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ش غ فsh-gh-f

(ف) شَغَفَ يَشْغَفُ شَغْفًاshaghafa yashghafu shaghfan: (1) to wound the pericardium (شَغَافٌshaghāf), the membrane of the heart (2) to take possession of someone's heart. ﴿قَدْ شَغَفَهَا حُبًّا﴾ (Yūsuf 12:30)
