Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

م ز قm-z-q

(ض) مَزَقَ يَمْزِقُ مَزْقًاmazaqa yamziqu mazqan: to tear, to cut into pieces. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

Form II (tafʿīl) مَزَّقَ يُمَزِّقُ تَمْزِيْقًاmazzaqa yumazziqu tamzīqan: to tear utterly to pieces (here Form II, tafʿīl, denotes intensity and repetition); مُمَزَّقٌmumazzaq (passive participle, ism mafʿūl; also a mīmī verbal noun, maṣdar mīmī): torn to shreds; a scattering in every direction. ﴿اِذَا مُزِّقْتُمْ كُلَّ مُمَزَّقٍ اِنَّكُمْ لَفِيْ خَلْقٍ جَدِيْدٍ﴾ (Sabaʾ 34:7); ﴿وَمَزَّقْنٰهُمْ كُلَّ مُمَزَّقٍ﴾ (Sabaʾ 34:19)
