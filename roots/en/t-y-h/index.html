Root ت ی ھ t-y-h

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ت ی ھt-y-h

(ض) تَيَهَ يَتْيِهُtayaha yatyihu (original form) → تَاهَ يَتِيْهُ تَيْهًا وَتِيْهًاtāha yatīhu tayhan wa-tīhan: to go astray, to lose the way and wander about bewildered. Only once in the Quran: ﴿أَرْبَعِيْنَ سَنَةً يَتِيْهُوْنَ فِي الْأَرْضِ﴾ (Al-Māʾidah 5:26)

التِّيْهat-tīh: a wilderness in which no way can be found — the forty years' wandering of the Children of Israel is known by this name.
