س ک بs-k-b

(ن) سَكَبَ يَسْكُبُ سَكْبًاsakaba yaskubu sakban: to pour out, to pour continuously.

مَسْكُوْبٌmaskūb (passive participle, ism mafʿūl): poured out without cease — i.e. water that never stops; it occurs in the description of Paradise. ﴿وَّمَآءٍ مَّسْكُوْبٍ﴾ (Al-Wāqiʿah 56:31)Only once in the Quran.
