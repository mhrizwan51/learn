Root ج ھ ز j-h-z

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ج ھ زj-h-z

(ف) جَهَزَ يَجْهَزُ جَهْزًاjahaza yajhazu jahzan: to complete an unfinished task, as in finishing off a wounded man by killing him.

جَهَازٌjahāz: the provisions that meet someone's need.

Form II (tafʿīl) جَهَّزَ يُجَهِّزُ تَجْهِيْزًاjahhaza yujahhizu tajhīzan: to provide someone with needed provisions, to equip. ﴿فَلَمَّا جَهَّزَهُمْ بِجَهَازِهِمْ﴾ (Yūsuf 12:70)
