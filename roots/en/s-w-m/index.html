Root س و م s-w-m

Quranic citations in this entry: 5 · اردو

س و مs-w-m

(ن) سَامَ يَسُوْمُ سَوْمًاsāma yasūmu sawman: to go somewhere in quest of something; with this basic sense it is used in various meanings: (1) (of cattle) to go out to pasture (2) to afflict someone in order to gain one's end (3) to set a mark so that one may go to, or obtain, (a thing) whenever one wishes. ﴿يَسُوْمُوْنَكُمْ سُوْٓءَ الْعَذَابِ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:49)

سِيْمَاءُsīmāʾu (noun, ism dhāt): mark, sign. ﴿سِيْمَاهُمْ فِيْ وُجُوْهِهِمْ مِّنْ اَثَرِ السُّجُوْدِ﴾ (Al-Fatḥ 48:29)

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَسَامَ يُسِيْمُ اِسَامَةًasāma yusīmu isāmatan: to take cattle out to pasture, to graze. ﴿فِيْهِ تُسِيْمُوْنَ﴾ (An-Naḥl 16:10)

Form II (tafʿīl) سَوَّمَ يُسَوِّمُ تَسْوِيْمًاsawwama yusawwimu taswīman: to mark, to brand.

مُسَوِّمٌmusawwim (active participle, ism fāʿil): one who marks. ﴿بِخَمْسَةِ اٰلٰفٍ مِّنَ الْمَلٰٓئِكَةِ مُسَوِّمِيْنَ﴾ (Āl ʿImrān 3:125)

مُسَوَّمٌmusawwam (passive participle, ism mafʿūl): marked. ﴿وَالْخَيْلِ الْمُسَوَّمَةِ﴾ (Āl ʿImrān 3:14)
