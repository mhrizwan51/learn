ب ق رb-q-r

(ن) بَقَرَ يَبْقُرُ بَقْرًاbaqara yabquru baqran: to split, to tear open.

بَقَرbaqar (generic noun (ism jins), singular (wāḥidah) بَقَرَةbaqarah, pl. بَقَرَاتbaqarāt): cattle, cow — so named because it splits the earth in ploughing; بَقَرٌbaqar is the generic noun (ism jins), بَقَرَةbaqarah any one cow or ox. Nine times in the Quran, each time as a noun: ﴿إِنَّ اللّٰهَ يَأْمُرُكُمْ أَنْ تَذْبَحُوْا بَقَرَةً﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:67); ﴿اِنَّ الْبَقَرَ تَشٰبَهَ عَلَيْنَا﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:70); ﴿سَبْعَ بَقَرٰتٍ سِمَانٍ﴾ (Yūsuf 12:43)
