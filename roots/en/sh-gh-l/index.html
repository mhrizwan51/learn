Root ش غ ل sh-gh-l

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ش غ لsh-gh-l

(ف) شَغَلَ يَشْغَلُ شَغْلًا وَشُغُلًاshaghala yashghalu shaghlan wa-shughulan: to keep busy, to occupy with some work. ﴿شَغَلَتْنَآ اَمْوَالُنَا وَاَهْلُوْنَا﴾ (Al-Fatḥ 48:11)

شُغُلٌshughul: occupation. ﴿اِنَّ اَصْحٰبَ الْجَنَّةِ الْيَوْمَ فِيْ شُغُلٍ فٰكِهُوْنَ﴾ (Yā Sīn 36:55)This root occurs twice in the Quran — in one place as an excuse and in one place as a blessing.
