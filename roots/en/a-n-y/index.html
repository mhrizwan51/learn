Root ا ن ی ʾ-n-y

Quranic citations in this entry: 7 · اردو

ا ن یʾ-n-y

(ض) أَنٰى يَأْنِيْ أَنْيًا وَإِنًيanā yaʾnī anyan wa-ʾinany: (1) (of the time of something) to draw near (2) (of a thing) to reach its limit, (of its time) to arrive, to ripen, to mature. The verb occurs only once in the Quran: ﴿أَلَمْ يَأْنِ لِلَّذِيْنَ آمَنُوْا﴾ (Al-Ḥadīd 57:16). Note: the verb form (bāb) is determined by this Quranic imperfect (muḍāriʿ) itself, "يَأْنِyaʾni", whose ʿayn carries kasrah.

إِنًيinany (noun, ism dhāt): the time of ripeness. ﴿غَيْرَ نٰظِرِيْنَ اِنٰىهُ﴾ (Al-Aḥzāb 33:53)

آنٍān, feminine آنِيَةٌāniyah (active participle (ism fāʿil)): (1) that which draws near, that is, near (2) that which reaches its limit, that is, extreme; boiling water. ﴿تُسْقٰى مِنْ عَيْنٍ اٰنِيَةٍ﴾ (Al-Ghāshiyah 88:5); ﴿حَمِيْمٍ اٰنٍ﴾ (Ar-Raḥmān 55:44)ānāʾ (plural, singular إِنًيinany or أَنًيanany): the hours of the night. ﴿وَمِنْ اٰنَآئِ الَّيْلِ فَسَبِّحْ﴾ (Ṭā Hā 20:130)

آنِيَةāniyah pl. أَوَانٍawān: vessel. ﴿بِاٰنِيَةٍ مِّنْ فِضَّةٍ﴾ (Al-Insān 76:15)

أَنّٰىannā (interrogative and conditional noun): whence, how — it occurs 28 times in the Quran. ﴿اَنّٰى يَكُوْنُ لِيْ غُلٰمٌ﴾ (Maryam 19:20). This root occurs 36 times in the Quran, in six forms.
