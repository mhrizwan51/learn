Root س ن د s-n-d

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

س ن دs-n-d

(ن) سَنَدَ يَسْنُدُ سُنُوْدًاsanada yasnudu sunūdan: to lean against, to take support.

سَنَدٌsanad (noun, ism dhāt): support, prop; from this comes the ḥadīth term «sanad» (chain of transmission).

Form II (tafʿīl) سَنَّدَ يُسَنِّدُ تَسْنِيْدًاsannada yusannidu tasnīdan: to prop up, to set upright against a support.

مُسَنَّدَةٌmusannadah (passive participle, ism mafʿūl): propped up; it occurs in a simile for the hypocrites. ﴿كَاَنَّهُمْ خُشُبٌ مُّسَنَّدَةٌ﴾ (Al-Munāfiqūn 63:4)Only once in the Quran.

Form VIII (iftiʿāl) اِسْتَنَدَ يَسْتَنِدُ اِسْتِنَادًاistanada yastanidu istinādan: to lean against something; to rely on something (this verb is not used in the Quran).
