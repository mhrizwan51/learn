Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ح ر دḥ-r-d

(ض) حَرَدَ يَحْرِدُ حَرْدًاḥarada yaḥridu ḥardan: to intend and resolve upon a task; also to grow angry and to withhold.

حَرْدḥard: intent, resolve; the exegetes have mentioned both meanings at this place: to withhold with intent. Only once in the Quran: ﴿وَّغَدَوْا عَلٰى حَرْدٍ قٰدِرِيْنَ﴾ (Al-Qalam 68:25)
