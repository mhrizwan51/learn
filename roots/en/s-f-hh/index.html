Root س ف ح s-f-ḥ

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

س ف حs-f-ḥ

(ف) سَفَحَ يَسْفَحُ سَفْحًاsafaḥa yasfaḥu safḥan: to shed blood, tears, etc.

مَسْفُوْحٌmasfūḥ (passive participle, ism mafʿūl): shed, poured out. ﴿اَوْ دَمًا مَّسْفُوْحًا﴾ (Al-Anʿām 6:145)

Form III (mufāʿalah) سَافَحَ يُسَافِحُ سِفَاحًاsāfaḥa yusāfiḥu sifāḥan: to outdo one another in shedding; hence it comes in the sense of committing fornication; مُسَافِحٌmusāfiḥ (active participle, ism fāʿil): fornicator. ﴿مُّحْصِنِيْنَ غَيْرَ مُسٰفِحِيْنَ﴾ (An-Nisāʾ 4:24)
