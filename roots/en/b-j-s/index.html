Root ب ج س b-j-s

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ب ج سb-j-s

(ن، ض) بَجَسَ يَبْجُسُbajasa yabjusu / يَبْجِسُ بَجْسًاyabjisu bajsan: to lance a wound, to make water flow from a narrow opening.

Form VII (infiʿāl) اِنْبَجَسَ يَنْبَجِسُ اِنْبِجَاسًاinbajasa yanbajisu inbijāsan: (of water) to flow out of a narrow opening, to gush forth. ﴿فَانْۢبَجَسَتْ مِنْهُ اثْنَتَا عَشْرَةَ عَيْنًا﴾ (Al-Aʿrāf 7:160)
