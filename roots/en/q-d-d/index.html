Quranic citations in this entry: 3 · اردو

ق د دq-d-d

(ن) قَدَّ يَقُدُّ قَدًّاqadda yaquddu qaddan (original form: قَدَدَ يَقْدُدُqadada yaqdudu): to cut or tear a thing lengthwise. ﴿وَقَدَّتْ قَمِيْصَهُۥ مِنْ دُبُرٍ﴾ (Yūsuf 12:25); passive voice: ﴿اِنْ كَانَ قَمِيْصُهُۥ قُدَّ مِنْ قُبُلٍ﴾ (Yūsuf 12:26)

قِدَّةٌqiddah pl. قِدَدٌqidad (noun, ism dhāt): a piece cut off from a thing; hence groups following separate ways, people of differing persuasions. ﴿كُنَّا طَرَآئِقَ قِدَدًا﴾ (Al-Jinn 72:11)
