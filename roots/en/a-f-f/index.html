Root ا ف ف ʾ-f-f

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ا ف فʾ-f-f

(ن، ض) اَفَّ يَؤُفُّaffa yaʾuffu / يَئِفُّ اَفًّاyaʾiffu affan: to say "uff, uff" in pain or restlessness.

اُفٍّuff (verbal interjection (ism fiʿl)): I am displeased, I am fed up. ﴿فَلَا تَقُلْ لَّهُمَآ اُفٍّ﴾ (Al-Isrāʾ 17:23)
