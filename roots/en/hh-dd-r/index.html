Root ح ض ر ḥ-ḍ-r

Quranic citations in this entry: 7 · اردو

ح ض رḥ-ḍ-r

(ن) حَضَرَ يَحْضُرُ حُضُوْرًاḥaḍara yaḥḍuru ḥuḍūran: the basic sense is to take up residence in a town; it mostly carries two meanings: (1) to be present at a place. (2) to be before someone, i.e. to be in attendance. ﴿اِذَا حَضَرَ اَحَدَكُمُ الْمَوْتُ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:180)

حَاضِرٌḥāḍir (adjective on the pattern of fāʿil): present, in attendance. ﴿وَوَجَدُوْا مَا عَمِلُوْا حَاضِرًا﴾ (Al-Kahf 18:49)

حَاضِرَةٌḥāḍirah: both the feminine of ḥāḍir and a noun (ism dhāt); as a noun it means: a settlement, a town. ﴿حَاضِرَةَ الْبَحْرِ﴾ (Al-Aʿrāf 7:163)

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَحْضَرَ يُحْضِرُ اِحْضَارًاaḥḍara yuḥḍiru iḥḍāran: to bring someone before someone, to present. ﴿عَلِمَتْ نَفْسٌ مَّآ اَحْضَرَتْ﴾ (At-Takwīr 81:14); ﴿وَاُحْضِرَتِ الْاَنْفُسُ الشُّحَّ﴾ (An-Nisāʾ 4:128)

مُحْضَرٌmuḥḍar (passive participle, ism mafʿūl): brought forward, presented. ﴿يَوْمَ تَجِدُ كُلُّ نَفْسٍ مَّا عَمِلَتْ مِنْ خَيْرٍ مُّحْضَرًا﴾ (Āl ʿImrān 3:30)

Form VIII (iftiʿāl) اِحْتَضَرَ يَحْتَضِرُ اِحْتِضَارًاiḥtaḍara yaḥtaḍiru iḥtiḍāran: to present with care, to bring forward by turns.

مُحْتَضَرٌmuḥtaḍar (passive participle, ism mafʿūl, also as a noun of time or place, ẓarf): brought forward; the place or turn of attendance. ﴿كُلُّ شِرْبٍ مُّحْتَضَرٌ﴾ (Al-Qamar 54:28)
