Root م و ر m-w-r

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

م و رm-w-r

(ن) مَارَ يَمُوْرُ مَوْرًاmāra yamūru mawran (original form: مَوَرَmawara): to move to and fro, to sway, to spin rapidly.

مَوْرmawr: agitation, quaking. Three times in the Quran, mostly in describing the sky on the Day of Resurrection: ﴿يَوْمَ تَمُوْرُ السَّمَاءُ مَوْرًا﴾ (Aṭ-Ṭūr 52:9) and ﴿أَنْ يَّخْسِفَ بِكُمُ الْأَرْضَ فَإِذَا هِيَ تَمُوْرُ﴾ (Al-Mulk 67:16).
