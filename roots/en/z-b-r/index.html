Quranic citations in this entry: 4 · اردو

ز ب رz-b-r

(ک) زَبُرَ يَزْبُرُ زَبَارَةًzabura yazburu zabāratan: to be large-bodied, to be stout, to be strong.

زُبْرَةٌzubrah pl. زُبَرٌzubar: a large piece of iron, an iron slab. ﴿اٰتُوْنِيْ زُبَرَ الْحَدِيْدِ﴾ (Al-Kahf 18:96)

زَبُوْرٌzabūr pl. زُبُرٌzubur (on the pattern of فَعُوْلٌfaʿūl, in the sense of a passive participle, ism mafʿūl): (1) a text written in bold letters, a booklet, a scripture. ﴿وَاٰتَيْنَا دَاوُۥدَ زَبُوْرًا﴾ (An-Nisāʾ 4:163); ﴿وَكُلُّ شَيْءٍ فَعَلُوْهُ فِي الزُّبُرِ﴾ (Al-Qamar 54:52) (2) a piece of something. ﴿فَتَقَطَّعُوْٓا اَمْرَهُمْ بَيْنَهُمْ زُبُرًا﴾ (Al-Muʾminūn 23:53)
