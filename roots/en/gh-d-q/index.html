غ د قgh-d-q

(س) غَدِقَ يَغْدَقُ غَدَقًاghadiqa yaghdaqu ghadaqan: for water to be abundant.

غَدَقghadaq: abundant, sweet water. The lexicographers say that the basic sense is abundance. Only once in the Quran, in the description of the fruit of seeking forgiveness: ﴿وَأَنْ لَّوِ اسْتَقَامُوْا عَلَي الطَّرِيْقَةِ لَأَسْقَيْنَاهُمْ مَّاءً غَدَقًا﴾ (Al-Jinn 72:16).
