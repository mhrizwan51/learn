Root د ر ج d-r-j

Quranic citations in this entry: 3 · اردو

د ر جd-r-j

(ن) دَرَجَ يَدْرُجُ دُرُوْجًاdaraja yadruju durūjan: to climb stairs, to walk slowly.

دَرَجَةٌdarajah pl. دَرَجٰتٌdarajāt: a step of a staircase, elevation, rank. ﴿هُمْ دَرَجٰتٌ عِنْدَ اللّٰهِ﴾ (Āl ʿImrān 3:163); ﴿وَلِلرِّجَالِ عَلَيْهِنَّ دَرَجَةٌ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:228)

Form VII (infiʿāl) اِنْدَرَجَ يَنْدَرِجُ اِنْدِرَاجًاindaraja yandariju indirājan: to fall within the scope of something; to be included in it (this verb is not used in the Quran).

Form X (istifʿāl) اِسْتَدْرَجَ يَسْتَدْرِجُ اِسْتِدْرَاجًاistadraja yastadriju istidrājan: to lead on gradually (step by step). ﴿سَنَسْتَدْرِجُهُمْ مِّنْ حَيْثُ لَا يَعْلَمُوْنَ﴾ (Al-Aʿrāf 7:182)
