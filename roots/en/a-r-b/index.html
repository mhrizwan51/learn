Root ا ر ب ʾ-r-b

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ا ر بʾ-r-b

(س) اَرِبَ يَاْرَبُ اَرَبًاariba yārabu araban: to be very fond of and eager for something; to be in need; to be needy.

اِرْبَةٌirbah: need, want. ﴿غَيْرِ اُولِي الْاِرْبَةِ مِنَ الرِّجَالِ﴾ (An-Nūr 24:31)

مَاْرَبَةٌmārabah pl. مَاٰرِبُmāribu (noun of time or place (ẓarf)): the place or time of fulfilling a need, that is, an occasion; a need. ﴿وَلِيَ فِيْهَا مَاٰرِبُ اُخْرٰى﴾ (Ṭā Hā 20:18)
