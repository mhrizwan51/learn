Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ا د دʾ-d-d

(ن) اَدَّ يَؤُدُّ اِدًّاadda yaʾuddu iddan: to be heavy and hard to bear.

اِدٌّidd: dreadful, grievous. ﴿لَقَدْ جِئْتُمْ شَيْئًا اِدًّا﴾ (Maryam 19:89)
