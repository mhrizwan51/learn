Root ف ن ی f-n-y

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ف ن یf-n-y

(س) فَنِيَ يَفْنَيٰ فَنَاءًfaniya yafnā fanāʾan: to come to an end, to be effaced, to perish — the opposite of بَقَاءbaqāʾ. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

فَانٍfān (active participle, ism fāʿil): perishing, that which passes away. Only once in the Quran, set against the everlastingness of God: ﴿كُلُّ مَنْ عَلَيْهَا فَانٍ وَّيَبْقٰى وَجْهُ رَبِّكَ ذُو الْجَلَالِ وَالْإِكْرَامِ﴾ (Ar-Raḥmān 55:26–27).
