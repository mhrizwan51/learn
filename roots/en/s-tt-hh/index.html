Root س ط ح s-ṭ-ḥ

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

س ط حs-ṭ-ḥ

(ف) سَطَحَ يَسْطَحُ سَطْحًاsaṭaḥa yasṭaḥu saṭḥan: to spread out; to make level and even. ﴿وَاِلَي الْاَرْضِ كَيْفَ سُطِحَتْ﴾ (Al-Ghāshiyah 88:20)

سَطْحٌsaṭḥ pl. سُطُوْحٌsuṭūḥ (noun, ism dhāt): a flat upper surface; a roof.

مِسْطَحٌmisṭaḥ (noun of instrument, ism ālah): a board on which things are spread out.Only once in the Quran, and that in the passive voice.
