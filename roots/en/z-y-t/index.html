Root ز ی ت z-y-t

Quranic citations in this entry: 3 · اردو

ز ی تz-y-t

(ض) زَاتَ يَزِيْتُ زَيْتًاzāta yazītu zaytan: to put or apply oil (denominative).

زَيْتٌzayt: oil; especially: olive oil. ﴿يَّكَادُ زَيْتُهَا يُضِيْٓءُ وَلَوْ لَمْ تَمْسَسْهُ نَارٌ﴾ (An-Nūr 24:35)

زَيْتُوْنٌzaytūn (generic noun (ism jins), singular زَيْتُوْنَةٌzaytūnah): the olive tree and its fruit; most of the lexicographers regard it as inert (jāmid) and call the pattern فَعْلُوْنٌfaʿlūn rare in Arabic; some have said its pattern is فَيْعُوْلٌfayʿūl. ﴿يُّوْقَدُ مِنْ شَجَرَةٍ مُّبٰرَكَةٍ زَيْتُوْنَةٍ﴾ (An-Nūr 24:35); ﴿وَالتِّيْنِ وَالزَّيْتُوْنِ﴾ (At-Tīn 95:1)
