Root خ ی ر kh-y-r

Quranic citations in this entry: 7 · اردو

خ ی رkh-y-r

(ض) خَارَ يَخِيْرُ خَيْرًاkhāra yakhīru khayran: to be beneficial.

خَيْرٌkhayr pl. خَيْرٰتٌkhayrāt (noun, ism dhāt): (1) that whose outcome is beneficial, good, goodness. ﴿فَمَنْ يَّعْمَلْ مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ خَيْرًا يَّرَهُۥ﴾ (Az-Zalzalah 99:7); ﴿فَاسْتَبِقُوا الْخَيْرٰتِ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:148); (2) a desirable thing, wealth and property. ﴿اِنْ تَرَكَ خَيْرَا ۨ الْوَصِيَّةُ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:180)

خَيْرٌkhayr (as an elative, ism tafḍīl): better. ﴿وَالْاٰخِرَةُ خَيْرٌ وَّاَبْقٰى﴾ (Al-Aʿlā 87:17)

خَيْرَةٌkhayrah pl. خَيْرٰتٌkhayrāt: a virtuous and excellent woman. ﴿فِيْهِنَّ خَيْرٰتٌ حِسَانٌ﴾ (Ar-Raḥmān 55:70)

Form VIII (iftiʿāl) اِخْتَيَرَ يَخْتَيِرُikhtayara yakhtayiru (original form) ⟵ اِخْتَارَ يَخْتَارُ اِخْتِيَارًاikhtāra yakhtāru ikhtiyāran: to obtain the desirable thing, to choose. ﴿وَرَبُّكَ يَخْلُقُ مَا يَشَآءُ وَيَخْتَارُ﴾ (Al-Qaṣaṣ 28:68)

خِيَرَةٌkhiyarah: choice, the right to choose. ﴿مَا كَانَ لَهُمُ الْخِيَرَةُ﴾ (Al-Qaṣaṣ 28:68)

Form X (istifʿāl) اِسْتَخَارَ يَسْتَخِيْرُ اِسْتِخَارَةًistakhāra yastakhīru istikhāratan: to seek what is good in a matter; to ask Allah for guidance to the better course (this verb is not used in the Quran).
