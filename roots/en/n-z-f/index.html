Root ن ز ف n-z-f

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ن ز فn-z-f

(ض) نَزَفَ يَنْزِفُ نَزْفًاnazafa yanzifu nazfan: to draw out all the water (of a well) and exhaust it; to shed blood; passive voice نُزِفَnuzifa: to lose one's reason (through wine), to become intoxicated. ﴿لَا فِيْهَا غَوْلٌ وَّلَا هُمْ عَنْهَا يُنْزَفُوْنَ﴾ (Aṣ-Ṣāffāt 37:47)

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَنْزَفَ يُنْزِفُ اِنْزَافًاanzafa yunzifu inzāfan: to lose one's reason, to become intoxicated; also (of a well) to run dry. ﴿لَّا يُصَدَّعُوْنَ عَنْهَا وَلَا يُنْزِفُوْنَ﴾ (Al-Wāqiʿah 56:19) — Note: both verses describe the wine of Paradise, and both times with negation.
