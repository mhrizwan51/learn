Root ا ز ز ʾ-z-z

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ا ز زʾ-z-z

(ن) اَزَّ يَؤُزُّ اَزًّاazza yaʾuzzu azzan: (1) (of a cooking-pot) to come to the boil, to seethe (intransitive). (2) to bring a pot to the boil (transitive). Then used of spurring and inciting someone to an act. ﴿تَؤُزُّهُمْ اَزًّا﴾ (Maryam 19:83)
