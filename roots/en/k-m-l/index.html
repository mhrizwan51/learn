Root ک م ل k-m-l

Quranic citations in this entry: 4 · اردو

ک م لk-m-l

(ن، ک) كَمَلَ يَكْمُلُ وَكَمُلَ يَكْمُلُ كَمَالًاkamala yakmulu wa-kamula yakmulu kamālan: to be complete, to be whole (this verb is not used in the Quran).

كَامِلٌkāmil, feminine كَامِلَةٌkāmilah (active participle, ism fāʿil): complete, whole. ﴿تِلْكَ عَشَرَةٌ كَامِلَةٌ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:196); ﴿حَوْلَيْنِ كَامِلَيْنِ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:233)

Form II (tafʿīl) كَمَّلَ يُكَمِّلُ تَكْمِيْلًاkammala yukammilu takmīlan: to complete; to perfect (this verb is not used in the Quran).

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَكْمَلَ يُكْمِلُ اِكْمَالًاakmala yukmilu ikmālan: to complete, to make whole. ﴿اَلْيَوْمَ اَكْمَلْتُ لَكُمْ دِيْنَكُمْ﴾ (Al-Māʾidah 5:3); ﴿وَلِتُكْمِلُوا الْعِدَّةَ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:185)
