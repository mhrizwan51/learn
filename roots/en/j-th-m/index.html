Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ج ث مj-th-m

(ن) جَثَمَ يَجْثُمُ جُثُوْمًاjathama yajthumu juthūman: to press the chest to the ground, to lie face down.

جَاثِمٌjāthim (active participle, ism fāʿil): one lying face down, one fallen face down. ﴿فَاَصْبَحُوْا فِيْ دَارِهِمْ جٰثِمِيْنَ﴾ (Al-Aʿrāf 7:78)
