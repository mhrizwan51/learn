Root ن ز غ n-z-gh

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ن ز غn-z-gh

(ف) نَزَغَ يَنْزَغُ نَزْغًاnazagha yanzaghu nazghan: to cause corruption; hence (1) to incite someone against someone, to sow discord (2) to goad into a wrong deed. ﴿مِنْۢ بَعْدِ اَنْ نَّزَغَ الشَّيْطٰنُ بَيْنِيْ وَبَيْنَ اِخْوَتِيْ﴾ (Yūsuf 12:100)

نَزْغٌnazgh (verbal noun, maṣdar, also a noun, ism dhāt): a discord-sowing word, an incitement, an evil suggestion. ﴿وَاِمَّا يَنْزَغَنَّكَ مِنَ الشَّيْطٰنِ نَزْغٌ فَاسْتَعِذْ بِاللّٰهِ﴾ (Al-Aʿrāf 7:200)
