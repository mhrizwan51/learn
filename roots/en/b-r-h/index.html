ب ر ھb-r-h

(س) بَرِهَ يَبْرَهُ بَرَهًاbariha yabrahu barahan: (of the body) to be healthy and radiant.

بُرْهَانburhān: exceedingly radiant, i.e. a clear and decisive proof. ﴿قُلْ هَاتُوْا بُرْهَانَكُمْ اِنْ كُنْتُمْ صٰدِقِيْنَ﴾ (Al-Baqarah 2:111)
