Root ل ق ح l-q-ḥ

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ل ق حl-q-ḥ

(ف) لَقَحَ يَلْقَحُ لَقْحًاlaqaḥa yalqaḥu laqḥan: to fertilize (by placing the blossom of the male date-palm in the female), to impregnate (pollination) (this verb is not used in the Quran).

لَاقِحٌlāqiḥ pl. لَوَاقِحُlawāqiḥu (active participle, ism fāʿil): fertilizing; used for both masculine and feminine. Only once in the Quran, in the description of the winds: ﴿وَاَرْسَلْنَا الرِّيٰحَ لَوَاقِحَ﴾ (Al-Ḥijr 15:22)
