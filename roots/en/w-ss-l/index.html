Root و ص ل w-ṣ-l

Quranic citations in this entry: 3 · اردو

و ص لw-ṣ-l

(ض) وَصَلَ يَصِلُ وَصْلًا وَصِلَةًwaṣala yaṣilu wa-ṣlan wa-ṣilatan (original form: وَصَلَ يَوْصِلُwaṣala yawṣilu): (intransitive) to reach someone, to arrive at; (transitive) to join, to connect. ﴿وَالَّذِيْنَ يَصِلُوْنَ مَآ اَمَرَ اللّٰهُ بِهِۦٓ اَنْ يُّوْصَلَ﴾ (Ar-Raʿd 13:21); ﴿لَنْ يَّصِلُوْٓا اِلَيْكَ﴾ (Hūd 11:81)

Form II (tafʿīl) وَصَّلَ يُوَصِّلُ تَوْصِيْلًاwaṣṣala yuwaṣṣilu tawṣīlan: to join on continuously, to convey in succession. Only once in the Quran: ﴿وَلَقَدْ وَصَّلْنَا لَهُمُ الْقَوْلَ لَعَلَّهُمْ يَتَذَكَّرُوْنَ﴾ (Al-Qaṣaṣ 28:51)

Form III (mufāʿalah) وَاصَلَ يُوَاصِلُ مُوَاصَلَةًwāṣala yuwāṣilu muwāṣalatan: to continue without interruption; to keep meeting and in contact with one another (this verb is not used in the Quran).

Form VIII (iftiʿāl) اِتَّصَلَ يَتَّصِلُ اِتِّصَالًاittaṣala yattaṣilu ittiṣālan: to be connected (with something); to be joined (this verb is not used in the Quran).
