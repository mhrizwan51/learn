Root ذ ر و dh-r-w

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ذ ر وdh-r-w

(ن) ذَرَوَ يَذْرُوُdharawa yadhruwu (original) ⟵ ذَرَا يَذْرُوْ ذَرْوًاdharā yadhrū dharwan (form in use): to blow dust about, to scatter. ﴿فَاَصْبَحَ هَشِيْمًا تَذْرُوْهُ الرِّيٰحُ﴾ (Al-Kahf 18:45)

ذَارٍdhār feminine ذَارِيَةdhāriyah pl. ذَارِيَاتdhāriyāt (active participle, ism fāʿil): that which blows about, which scatters. ﴿وَالذّٰرِيٰتِ ذَرْوًا﴾ (Adh-Dhāriyāt 51:1)
