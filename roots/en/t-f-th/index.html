Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ت ف ثt-f-th

(س) تَفِثَ يَتْفَثُ تَفَثًاtafitha yatfathu tafathan: for dirt and grime to build up on a thing.

تَفَثٌtafath (noun, ism dhāt): dirt, grime. ﴿ثُمَّ لْيَقْضُوْا تَفَثَهُمْ﴾ (Al-Ḥajj 22:29)
