Root ح ب ک ḥ-b-k

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ح ب کḥ-b-k

(ض) حَبَكَ يَحْبِكُ حَبْكًاḥabaka yaḥbiku ḥabkan: to weave cloth firmly and beautifully, to set the warp and weft right.

حُبُكḥubuk (plural, singular حِبَاكḥibāk or حَبِيْكَةḥabīkah): twisted lines, beautiful tracks and streaks. Only once in the Quran: ﴿وَالسَّمَآءِ ذَاتِ الْحُبُكِ﴾ (Adh-Dhāriyāt 51:7) — i.e. the sky with its beautiful pathways.
