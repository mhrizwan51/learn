Root ھ ر ع h-r-ʿ

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ھ ر عh-r-ʿ

(ف) هَرَعَ يَهْرَعُ هَرْعًاharaʿa yahraʿu harʿan: to run somewhere in agitation and haste, to rush headlong. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَهْرَعَ يُهْرِعُ اِهْرَاعًاahraʿa yuhriʿu ihrāʿan: to drive someone somewhere in agitation, to make rush headlong; passive voice اُهْرِعَuhriʿa: to be driven headlong. ﴿وَجَآءَهُۥ قَوْمُهُۥ يُهْرَعُوْنَ اِلَيْهِ﴾ (Hūd 11:78); ﴿فَهُمْ عَلٰٓى اٰثٰرِهِمْ يُهْرَعُوْنَ﴾ (Aṣ-Ṣāffāt 37:70)
