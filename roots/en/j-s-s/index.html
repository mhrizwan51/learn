Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ج س سj-s-s

(ن) جَسَّ يَجُسُّ جَسًّاjassa yajussu jassan: to feel the pulse to ascertain the state of health; to feel something out to ascertain its condition.

Form V (tafaʿʿul) تَجَسَّسَ يَتَجَسَّسُ تَجَسُّسًاtajassasa yatajassasu tajassusan: to go deliberately prying into someone's private affairs, to spy. ﴿وَلَا تَجَسَّسُوْا﴾ (Al-Ḥujurāt 49:12)
