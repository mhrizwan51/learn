Root ش ن ء sh-n-ʾ

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ش ن ءsh-n-ʾ

(ف، س) شَنَاَshanaʾa / شَنِئَ يَشْنَاُ شَنْاً وَشَنَاٰنًاshaniʾa yashnaʾu shanan wa-shanānan: to bear malice, to hate.

شَنَاٰنٌshanān (verbal noun, maṣdar): malice, enmity. ﴿وَلَا يَجْرِمَنَّكُمْ شَنَاٰنُ قَوْمٍ عَلٰٓى اَلَّا تَعْدِلُوْا﴾ (Al-Māʾidah 5:8)

شَانِئٌshāniʾ (active participle, ism fāʿil): one who bears malice. ﴿اِنَّ شَانِئَكَ هُوَ الْاَبْتَرُ﴾ (Al-Kawthar 108:3) [Indeed, it is the one who hates you (peace be upon him) who is utterly cut off, without name or trace]
