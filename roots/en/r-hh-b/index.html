Root ر ح ب r-ḥ-b

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ر ح بr-ḥ-b

(ک) رَحُبَ يَرْحُبُ رُحْبًاraḥuba yarḥubu ruḥban: for a place to be spacious. ﴿وَضَاقَتْ عَلَيْكُمُ الْاَرْضُ بِمَا رَحُبَتْ﴾ (At-Tawbah 9:25)

مَرْحَبٌmarḥab (noun of time or place, ẓarf, on the pattern of مَفْعَلmafʿal): a time or occasion of spaciousness; mostly used in the sense of welcome. ﴿لَا مَرْحَبًۢا بِهِمْ﴾ (Ṣād 38:59)
