Root ش ر د sh-r-d

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

ش ر دsh-r-d

(ن) شَرَدَ يَشْرُدُ شُرُوْدًاsharada yashrudu shurūdan: to shy away, to bolt.

Form II (tafʿīl) شَرَّدَ يُشَرِّدُ تَشْرِيْدًاsharrada yusharridu tashrīdan: to frighten off, to put to flight, to scatter. ﴿فَشَرِّدْ بِهِمْ مَّنْ خَلْفَهُمْ﴾ (Al-Anfāl 8:57)
