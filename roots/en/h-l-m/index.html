Root ھ ل م h-l-m

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ھ ل مh-l-m

— No verb is in use from the bare triliteral (thulāthī mujarrad).

هَلُمَّhalumma (verbal interjection, ism fiʿl): (1) come! (intransitive) (2) produce!, bring! (transitive); among the people of Ḥijāz it is used unchanged for singular, dual and plural alike, and in the Quran it occurs in this dialect. ﴿وَالْقَآئِلِيْنَ لِاِخْوَانِهِمْ هَلُمَّ اِلَيْنَا﴾ (Al-Aḥzāb 33:18); ﴿قُلْ هَلُمَّ شُهَدَآءَكُمُ﴾ (Al-Anʿām 6:150)
