Root ب خ س b-kh-s

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

ب خ سb-kh-s

(ف) بَخَسَ يَبْخَسُ بَخْسًاbakhasa yabkhasu bakhsan: to diminish someone's due, to reduce the measure, weight or price. ﴿وَلَا تَبْخَسُوا النَّاسَ أَشْيَاءَهُمْ﴾ (Al-Aʿrāf 7:85). Note: the verb form (bāb) is determined from the Quranic form (ṣīghah) «يَبْخَسْyabkhas». Seven times in the Quran.

بَخْسbakhs (verbal noun (maṣdar) in the sense of a passive participle (ism mafʿūl)): diminished, deficient. ﴿بِثَمَنٍۢ بَخْسٍ﴾ (Yūsuf 12:20)
