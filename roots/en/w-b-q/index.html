Root و ب ق w-b-q

Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

و ب قw-b-q

(ض) وَبَقَ يَبِقُ وَبْقًا وَوُبُوْقًاwabaqa yabiqu wa-bqan wa-wubūqan (original form: وَبَقَ يَوْبِقُwabaqa yawbiqu): to perish. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

مَوْبِقٌmawbiq (noun of time or place, ẓarf, on the pattern of مَفْعِلٌmafʿil): a place of destruction. Only once in the Quran: ﴿وَجَعَلْنَا بَيْنَهُمْ مَّوْبِقًا﴾ (Al-Kahf 18:52)

Form IV (ifʿāl) اَوْبَقَ يُوْبِقُ اِيْبَاقًاawbaqa yūbiqu ībāqan: to destroy someone. Only once in the Quran: ﴿اَوْ يُوْبِقْهُنَّ بِمَا كَسَبُوْا وَيَعْفُ عَنْ كَثِيْرٍ﴾ (Ash-Shūrā 42:34)
