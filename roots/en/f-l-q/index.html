ف ل قf-l-q

(ض) فَلَقَ يَفْلِقُ فَلْقًاfalaqa yafliqu falqan: to split a thing open and bring something forth from it, as the light of dawn cleaving the darkness of night, or a sprout breaking out of a seed. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

فَالِقٌfāliq (active participle, ism fāʿil): (1) the Cleaver, one who splits (2) one who brings forth. ﴿اِنَّ اللّٰهَ فَالِقُ الْحَبِّ وَالنَّوٰى﴾ (Al-Anʿām 6:95); ﴿فَالِقُ الْاِصْبَاحِ﴾ (Al-Anʿām 6:96)

فَلَقٌfalaq: that which appears when a thing is split open, such as the dawn, a plant and the like. ﴿قُلْ اَعُوْذُ بِرَبِّ الْفَلَقِ﴾ (Al-Falaq 113:1)

Form VII (infiʿāl) اِنْفَلَقَ يَنْفَلِقُ اِنْفِلَاقًاinfalaqa yanfaliqu infilāqan: to be split asunder. ﴿فَانْفَلَقَ فَكَانَ كُلُّ فِرْقٍ كَالطَّوْدِ الْعَظِيْمِ﴾ (Ash-Shuʿarāʾ 26:63)
