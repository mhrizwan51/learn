Root غ ز ل gh-z-l

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

غ ز لgh-z-l

(ض) غَزَلَ يَغْزِلُ غَزْلًاghazala yaghzilu ghazlan: to spin yarn or wool. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

غَزْلٌghazl (verbal noun (maṣdar) in the sense of a passive participle (ism mafʿūl)): spun yarn. ﴿وَلَا تَكُوْنُوْا كَالَّتِيْ نَقَضَتْ غَزْلَهَا مِنْۢ بَعْدِ قُوَّةٍ اَنْكَاثًا﴾ (An-Naḥl 16:92)
