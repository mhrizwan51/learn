Quranic citations in this entry: 2 · اردو

و ث نw-th-n

(ض) وَثَنَ يَثِنُ وُثُوْنًاwathana yathinu wuthūnan: to remain fixed in a place, to stay firm. (This verb is not used in the Quran.)

وَثَنٌwathan pl. اَوْثَانٌawthān (noun, ism dhāt): idol (especially one of stone or the like that is set up and fixed in place). ﴿اِنَّمَا تَعْبُدُوْنَ مِنْ دُوْنِ اللّٰهِ اَوْثَانًا﴾ (Al-ʿAnkabūt 29:17); ﴿فَاجْتَنِبُوا الرِّجْسَ مِنَ الْاَوْثَانِ﴾ (Al-Ḥajj 22:30)
