Root و ح ش w-ḥ-sh

Quranic citations in this entry: 1 · اردو

و ح شw-ḥ-sh

— No verb from the bare triliteral (thulāthī mujarrad) occurs in the Quran.

وَحْشٌwaḥsh pl. وُحُوْشٌwuḥūsh (noun, ism dhāt): wild animal, one not tame towards man — hence it is the opposite of ʾ-n-s. Only once in the Quran, among the scenes of the Resurrection: ﴿وَاِذَا الْوُحُوْشُ حُشِرَتْ﴾ (At-Takwīr 81:5)
