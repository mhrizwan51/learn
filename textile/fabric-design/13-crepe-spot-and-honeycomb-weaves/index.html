Crepe, Spot and Honeycomb Weaves

Crepe weaves on a sateen base

A crepe weave has an all-over pebbled, non-directional look. The classic way to construct one is to start with a sateen base and add one or more marks to every base mark in a fixed arrangement, or to superimpose two sateens. The hex codes below are the lecture’s own designs.

Honeycomb

A pointed draft and a pointed lifting plan produce diamond-shaped cells whose ridges are long floats and whose hollows are tightly interlaced — a towelling weave that traps water. The 8-end version is the ordinary honeycomb; the 16-end version is the Brighton-style large cell.
