What a “set” is

A set is one full creel of cones warped onto the required number of warper’s beams, then sized onto weaver’s beams. The size of the set is fixed by the cone: a 4.17 lb cone of Ne 20 holds 4.17 × 840 × 20 = 70,056 yd, less 2% wastage. Each loom gets a sized beam of a given length; multiply by looms and by beams per set to get the yardage the cones must supply, and divide by the yardage per cone to get the number of sets.

length per cone = cone lb × 840 × Ne × (1 − wastage)   ·   sets = beams × looms × sized length per beam ÷ length per cone   ·   bags = ends per beam × sets ÷ 24

How long a sized beam can be comes from the beam volume (Lecture 7) and the yarn diameter d = 1/(26√Ne) inches: the yarn cross-section × ends × length cannot exceed the beam’s volume.
